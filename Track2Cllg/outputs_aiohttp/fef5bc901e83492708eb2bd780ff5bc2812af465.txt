                  Transfer admission requirements 

You must meet minimum UC subject and scholarship requirements for transfer admission. However, students admitted to UC Davis also need to meet any additional GPA thresholds.
UC transferable units 
Complete at least 60 semester or 90 quarter UC transferable units by the end of the spring term prior to fall enrollment.
Courses
Complete the following 7-course pattern by the end of spring term prior to enrollment with C grades or better.
Two transferable courses in English composition
One transferable course in mathematical or quantitative reasoning
Four transferable courses from at least two of the following subject areas:
Arts and humanities
Social and behavioral sciences
Physical and biological sciences
Students need to complete all required course work — this includes 
selective major prerequisites
 — by end of spring, prior to fall enrollment.
GPA requirements 
Earn at least a 2.40 GPA in UC-transferable courses (2.80 for California non-residents) to meet UC requirements. UC Davis requires a minimum 2.80 GPA for admission. 
Selective majors
 require higher overall, series and group GPAs. 
Transfer Admission Guarantee
, or TAG, requires various GPAs for admission to different colleges.

Strive to achieve the highest GPA.
Selective major requirements
Some majors at UC Davis require 
additional major preparation courses
 for admission. Select your college below to view its list of selective majors. All selective majors 
except
 business, computer science and data science are open to TAG.
College of Agricultural and Environmental Sciences
College of Biological Sciences
College of Engineering
College of Letters and Science
Graduate School of Management
Class standing 
Very few lower-division transfers are admitted. The College of Agricultural and Environmental Sciences occasionally considers applications from students with 
less than junior-level standing
. All other academic divisions only consider applications from junior-level transfers.
Pass/No Pass units 
Students may take up to but not exceed 14 semester (21 quarter) Pass/No Pass units. Due to COVID-19, courses taken with Pass/No Pass or Credit/No Credit grading in the winter, spring and summer terms of 2020 do not count toward this limit.

Learn about the Transfer Admission Guarantee 

TAG offers eligible California Community College students in select majors a guaranteed path to UC Davis. Students who meet GPA, coursework and application requirements can secure early admission and jumpstart their transfer journey.
