Learning Objectives

Students completing an ELTS language-focused program will be able to:

Develop advanced proficiency in the target language and use it effectively in academic and cultural contexts.

Analyze literature, film, media, and other cultural forms using appropriate critical and interpretive approaches.

Demonstrate knowledge of the histories, cultures, and societies associated with the language and its broader cultural contexts.

Understand language and culture from a transcultural perspective, including connections across national, linguistic, and disciplinary boundaries.

Conduct research and engage critically with primary and secondary sources using appropriate methods and scholarly conventions.

Communicate ideas effectively through written, oral, and other forms of academic or creative work.
