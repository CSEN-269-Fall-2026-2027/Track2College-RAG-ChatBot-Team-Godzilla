Why should I study Political Science?

Many students who choose political science learn about government policies not just in the United States, but all over the world. It also goes beyond that. Studying political science is an easy way to affect real change in politics and obtain a deep understanding of policy issues. 
On a broader scale, majoring in political science will help you discover methods of analysis that are useful in your personal and professional life and is an excellent preparation to help you succeed in almost any career. 

What are my job prospects?

Political Science graduates will be able to pursue careers in a variety of different fields, including:

Policy analyst or consultant 

Politician

Public relations specialist 

Attorney 

Campaign manager 

Civil rights advocate 

FBI/CIA agent 

What kind of skills will I learn?

A degree in Political Science gives students the skills to understand and work with various concepts. Some of those skills include:

Logical thinking

Communication 

Planning & development 

Analytical & research 

Argument & debate
