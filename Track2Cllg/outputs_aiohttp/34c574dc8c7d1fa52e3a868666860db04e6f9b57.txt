First-Time Freshmen Admission Requirements and Application Process

College at UWA is a transformative journey. With small classes led by expert professors, you’ll receive personalized attention and support. Beyond academics, there are ample social, academic, and service growth opportunities. UWA fosters an atmosphere where everyone is dedicated to your success. Surrounded by supportive peers, you’ll thrive academically and personally. Start your journey to understand why everyone says, “There’s something about this place!”

Apply Now

Minimum Requirements for Admission

First-time freshmen students must meet 
one
 of the following to be eligible for admission to UWA:

ACT:
 17 composite

SAT:
 920

High School GPA:
 2.5 cumulative GPA*

*Students must graduate high school with a successful diploma type or complete a GED.

Application Process

Ready to apply? A Recruitment and Enrollment Specialist is here to help you from start to finish. Visit our 
Find Your Recruitment and Enrollment Specialist
 page to connect with the specialist assigned to your area and get personalized support.

Next:

Submit your application for admission and pay the $30 non-refundable application fee.

Send your ACT or SAT scores (if applicable).

Request that your official high school transcript be sent to UWA.

Submit your immunization records. 
Click here to learn more about required immunizations.

Please deliver transcripts, test scores and the Immunization Records and Medical History Form to:

The University of West Alabama
Office of Admissions
Station 4
Livingston, Alabama 35470

Email to: 
[email protected]
Fax with cover sheet to: (205) 652-3881

Next Steps

Be on the lookout for your acceptance letter! Once you’re admitted, you can review the next steps to finalize your enrollment and get ready for college, including orientation, housing, financial aid, registration, and important deadlines.
