Psychology

The mission of UNA Psychology is to help students achieve their personal and professional potential by emphasizing the scientific study of human thought and behavior, promoting a spirit of active inquiry and critical thinking, and fostering a sense of social responsibility in the global community. Sociology

The mission of UNA Sociology is to help students acquire critical thinking and analytical skills grounded in the sociological perspective. There are essential tools that today's leaders in any sector and career must have to understand social phenomena, group dynamics, and socio-cultural forces behind our collective and individual behaviors and interactions in society.
