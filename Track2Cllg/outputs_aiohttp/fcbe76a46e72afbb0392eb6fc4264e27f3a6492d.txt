Meet Savannah!

Savannah Pedroza arrived with a passion for serving others and found a TROY community
                     that challenged her to lead with purpose and compassion. As a nursing student, she
                     has immersed herself in campus life, serving as a Trojan Ambassador, SGA Presidential
                     Cabinet member, Alpha Gamma Delta member, Freshman Forum Delegate and student worker
                     in the Office of Admissions. Since joining the Trojan family, she has grown as a leader
                     and strengthened her commitment to serving others, all while pursuing her goal of
                     becoming a certified registered nurse anesthetist. And for Savannah, this is only
                     the beginning.

How to Apply to TROY

Visit troy.edu/enroll.

Log in or create account (This is your TROY Application Profile).

Select your application type and begin application.

Submit your application and pay the application fee.

After applying, submit required documents. 

Check your TROY Application Profile for status updates and missing items.
