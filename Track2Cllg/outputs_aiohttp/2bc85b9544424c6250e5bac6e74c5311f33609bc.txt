Your Next Steps to McMurry

At McMurry, we look at each student individually. Your application is reviewed based on your academic record, involvement, and other experiences that help us understand your strengths and potential.

McMurry is test-optional, so you can choose whether to submit ACT or SAT scores. You may also submit optional materials such as letters of recommendation or an essay.

 Steps to Apply

Complete your free online application.

Submit your high school transcript.

Submit ACT or SAT scores if you choose to include them with your application.

Check your application portal for your status and any additional items needed.

Advance Your Career at McMurry

Graduate students bring valuable professional experience, academic achievement, and fresh perspectives to the McMurry community. Whether you’re advancing your career, developing new skills, or pursuing a personal goal, we’re excited you’re considering McMurry for your graduate education.

Our admission team is here to help make your transition into graduate study as smooth as possible.

Check your application portal for your status and any additional items needed.

 Steps to Apply

Complete your online application. Submit your graduate admission application through McMurry's application portal.

Pay the $50 application fee. The non-refundable application fee is waived for McMurry alumni.

Submit your college transcripts. Provide transcripts from all colleges and universities you have attended.

Review additional program requirements. Some graduate programs may require supplemental materials. Review your program's admission requirements and check your application portal for any additional items.

Join the McMurry Dual Credit Academy

The McMurry Dual Credit Academy offers flexible, affordable college-level courses for high school students ready to get a head start on college. Courses are offered online, are transferable, and can connect to Fast Track pathways at McMurry University.

All students who complete a McMurry Dual Credit Academy course will receive a one-time $1,000 scholarship to attend McMurry University as an incoming freshman.

 Steps to Apply

Submit your free online application. Be sure to enter your high school counselor as a reference on the application.

Have your counselor upload a high school transcript to your application reference.

Check your application portal for your status and any additional items needed.

Important Dates

Admission Deadline

Dual credit courses at McMurry begin each Fall (August) and Spring (January). Applications are accepted until the first day of classes each semester. Submit your application as soon as you’re ready, and we’ll help you start classes in the next available semester!

Honors Program

The McMurry University Honors Program is a distinct track designed to enrich the academic experience of our most outstanding students. The program includes Honors-specific classes, travel and scholarship opportunities, and writing a senior-year thesis. Students studying any major and involved in any on-campus activity, including athletics, are encouraged to apply and join.
