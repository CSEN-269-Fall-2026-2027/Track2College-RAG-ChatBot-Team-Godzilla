Degree Plans

The AACSB-accredited Bachelor of Business Administration in Finance combines a strong business foundation with specialized coursework in investments, financial institutions and markets, capital budgeting, portfolio management, and financial analysis. 

The curriculum explores how organizations manage money, evaluate risk, and make investment decisions. Students also gain hands-on experience through internships and participation in a student- managed investment fund. 

Students may pursue a double major, such as finance with accounting or economics, or add a business certificate to broaden their expertise.
