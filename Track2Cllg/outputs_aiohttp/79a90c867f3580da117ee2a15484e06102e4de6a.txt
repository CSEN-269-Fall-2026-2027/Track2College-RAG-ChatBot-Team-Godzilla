A College for the Future

USF’s Bellini College of Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity and Computing is the
                     nation’s first named college focused on all three disciplines. Through interdisciplinary
                     research, industry-connected education and partnerships with business and government,
                     the college prepares students to lead in a rapidly changing technology landscape while
                     advancing responsible innovation that strengthens Florida’s economy and global competitiveness.

Tech Career Advancement Program

The Tech Career Advancement Program, known as tCAP, prepares Bellini College students
                     to turn their education into a successful technology career. Through professional
                     development, hands-on learning, industry mentorship and real-world experiences, students
                     build the technical knowledge, workplace skills and confidence to graduate ready for
                     a rapidly changing workforce.
