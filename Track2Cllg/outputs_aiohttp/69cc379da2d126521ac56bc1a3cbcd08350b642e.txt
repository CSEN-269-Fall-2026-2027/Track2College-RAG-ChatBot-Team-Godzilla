                  			Bachelor's in Graphic Design Curriculum

                  			The bachelor's degree in graphic design starts with the basics, such as visual
                  communication principles, layout, color theory and typography. From there, you'll
                  move into more advanced courses like digital illustration, branding, motion graphics,

                  projects that help you think creatively, solve problems and develop a professional
                  portfolio. While specific courses may vary from year to year, your classes are designed
                  to give you both the artistic vision and technical expertise you need to succeed in

FAQ About the Bachelor's Degree in Graphic Design

Learn more about the field of graphic design and this program.

What is graphic design degree?

A graphic design degree teaches you how to communicate ideas visually using images,
                        typography, color and digital tools. You'll learn how to design logos, websites, social
                        media graphics, branding materials, print and digital ads, video graphics and more.
                        At TROY, you'll learn how to design for a target audience, refine your work and use
                        professional software to bring your ideas to life. You'll build your portfolio with
                        hands-on projects that set you apart in the job market.

What do graphic designers do?

Graphic designers create visual content that helps businesses and organizations communicate
                           with customers and other audiences. Graphic designers develop logos, websites, advertisements,
                           social media graphics, packaging and other branded materials that help businesses
                           create a personality and compel audiences to act. 

A major part of graphic design involves researching audiences, developing creative
                           concepts, selecting typography and color, and telling stories visually. Designers
                           often collaborate with marketing teams, developers and clients, playing a key part
                           in branding, user experience and content creation across print and digital platforms.

What can you do with a graphic design degree?

Because nearly every organization relies on strong visual communication, 
careers for graphic design majors
 can be found in advertising agencies, corporate marketing departments, nonprofits
                           and startups. You could also work as a freelance graphic designer. 

Specific 
careers in graphic design
 include:

Web designer

Art director

Special effects artist or animator

Graphic designer

Desktop publisher

UX/UI designer

Packaging designer

Production artist

Graduates of TROY's program typically launch their 
careers in graphic design
 with creative agencies or as in-house designers. Many students also use the commercial
                           skills they acquire at TROY to establish their own creative studios as entrepreneurs.

Is graphic design a good major?

Yes, graphic design is a good major if you enjoy creativity, problem-solving and using
                           art to convey a message. As a graphic design major, you'll learn how to communicate
                           ideas clearly through images, typography and digital media while building practical
                           skills employers value like audience research, design software proficiency, collaboration
                           and critical thinking. You'll also have opportunities to work in a wide variety of
                           environments — businesses, nonprofits and other organizations all rely on strong branding
                           and digital content.

Graphic design majors
 at TROY graduate with a professional portfolio that showcases their work to set them
                           apart for careers in marketing, advertising, web design, motion graphics or content
                           creation.
