Military & Veterans

We’re enthusiastic about making the admissions process as seamless as possible for veteran students. If you are a veteran or other military-eligible student, complete a regular ACU application along with the Veterans Administration benefits application process. Application forms are available at ACU’s Veteran’s Office, from your local VA Office or online at 
benefits.va.gov/gibill
.

          Explore Military and Veterans Admissions
