A Worldly Curriculum 
Throughout the international business curriculum at the USD’s Knauss School of Business, you’ll work on real-world projects for companies that conduct business internationally. This way, you’ll gain needed experience and an understanding of business impacts — near and far.
Featured Classes
MGMT 309
International Comparative Management
Learn how to address the potential roadblocks and chances for deeper connection that managers face as they work in multicultural and global environments.
BSCM 300
Global Purchasing and Supply Management
Learn how global supply chains operate and how companies manage the flow of goods across borders. Explore how multinational companies build efficient and resilient networks in an interconnected world.  
MGMT 414
International Management Consulting
Work on real-world consulting projects with a fast-growing San Diego company seeking to expand into global markets. Gain hands-on experience in international consulting and international market entry planning.
MKTG 305
Global Marketing
Ever wondered how marketing adapts its messaging to audiences on a global scale? In this course, uncover solutions to challenges that marketing managers face in a global marketplace.
