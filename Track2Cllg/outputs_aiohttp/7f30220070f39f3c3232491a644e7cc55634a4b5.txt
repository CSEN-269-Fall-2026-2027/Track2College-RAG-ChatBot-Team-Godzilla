Careers

The products you interact with on a daily basis—from footwear to consumer electronics to medical devices (and everything in between)—are the result of extensive human-centered design processes. ArtCenter’s Bachelor of Science degree in Product Design equips you to create functional, relevant and appealing products. Here, you’ll develop the commercial mindset along with the digital and hands-on design skills needed to tackle new frontiers in design and innovation.

Program Overview

ArtCenter’s product design major prepares students to enter a workforce with the highly sought after design, research and analytical skills necessary to thrive as a creative mind in a variety of professional environments. Under the guidance of our faculty of working designers, you’ll develop products that improve lives while gaining a keen understanding of what it takes to succeed as an entrepreneur or within an existing organization. If you’re especially interested in the business side of design, you can spend a semester in INSEAD business school’s top-ranked MBA program.

Our bachelor’s in product design program provides an immersive education in visual, creative, technical and analytical best practices. Deep-dive into special topics like 3D modeling, packaging design, and industrial design. Gain comprehensive expertise in design research methodologies, business principles, materials technologies, manufacturing processes, global trends and sustainable practices. Expand your marketable skillsets in our state-of-the-art research lab, 
CMTEL (Color, Materials and Trends Exploration Laboratory)
.

Real Designs, Real Prototypes, Real Business Partners

At ArtCenter, your classroom learning forms the foundation of your professional experiences. We’ll invite you to participate in 
DesignStorms®
 and other Sponsored Studios, which are commissioned by corporate partners who trust ArtCenter students to explore new frontiers in design and innovation. Past partnerships include Meta, Jaguar, Under Armour, Arby’s and Adidas.

The product design major equips you with an array of real-world expertise. You’ll prototype designs in on-campus labs, build a web portfolio to prepare for internship placements and have the option to study away in 
Portland 
in our brand-sponsored design studio where you'll work on-site at top athletic brands like Nike, Adidas and Under Armor.

Careers in Product Design

With a product design degree, you’ll come up with functional, relevant products that have visual and emotional appeal. You’ll engage in practices that minimize environmental impact from production to disposal. You'll then emerge with a professional-quality portfolio that will appeal to corporations and organizations that prioritize smart design on local and global scales. Whether you plan to work for an industry giant or branch out on your own entrepreneurial path, ArtCenter prepares you to become an innovative creator and a paradigm-shifting leader in roles like:

Consumer Product Design

Package Design

Toy Design

Furniture and Lighting Design

Exhibition Design

Entertainment Design
