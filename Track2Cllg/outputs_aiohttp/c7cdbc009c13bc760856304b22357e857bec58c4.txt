The Undergraduate Experience 

The St. Thomas College of Engineering 

 We are known for our hands-on learning, personal advising, and our amazing faculty that bring their real-world experience to the classroom. 
When it comes to academic quality, U.S. News & World Report ranks St. Thomas
 #29 in the U.S. and #1 in Minnesota for engineering colleges without a doctoral program.

Students can participate in student-faculty research as undergraduates, mentorship programs, internships, and all engineering students take a two-semester industry partnered Senior Design Clinic. All are reasons why, more than 1 in 9 St. Thomas undergraduate students major in engineering.

View our 
2025 Engineer Magazine
 which features innovative student projects and impactful research, exceptional faculty leadership and partnerships to advance the Center for Microgrid Research, the St. Thomas Applied & Renewable Technologies (START) Center, no-cost hands-on CNC optimization boot camps, and exceptional facilities such as the Blattner Robotics and Automation Lab, Five-Axis Machining Center and 3D Data Visualization Studio that fuel innovation and collaboration.

Laura Hernandez Cancelado '24 - Civil Engineering

What do you enjoy most about Engineering at the University of St. Thomas? "The small class sizes and the close relationships with faculty create a personalized learning experience. Professors not only know students by name but also actively seek our opinions, ensuring an engaged and collaborative learning environment."

Cienna Becker '24- Computer Engineering 

Cienna Becker worked on the 
St. Thomas Microgrid
 as a student researcher her senior year. Today she is employed as a Software Engineer at Vocxi Health, a breath diagnostics company.
" I love the freedom that the St. Thomas Computer Engineering degree offered me... I’ve gotten to take everything from environmental classes to statistics to graphics to microprocessors. It was great hopping from subject to subject and every semester felt unique from the previous ones!" said Becker.

Mary Murphy '24 - Mechanical Engineering 

Mary Murphy is employed as a manufacturing engineer at Graco. "I appreciate all the hands-on labs we got to do for our degrees. It is so unique [at St. Thomas] that we have so many resources open to us for personal use in the machine and wood shops," said Murphy.

Dagmawe Mamo '22 - Mechanical Engineering

Dagmawe Mamo is the CEO of Omega VR. "I definitely enjoyed the small class sizes and the professors are very personable. St. Thomas wants you to do well, wants you to graduate and wants you to be good at your job," said Mamo.
