                     Discover the Power of the Past with TROY's History Major

                     Knowledge of history helps us create a better world. At Troy University, you can gain
                     a greater understanding of the past to work toward a stronger future in the Bachelor
                     of Science in History program. Through engaging coursework, practice with archival
                     methods and hands-on learning experiences, you'll explore the stories and cultures
                     that have shaped our world today. You'll also have the opportunity to tailor your
                     degree to your future career in history by choosing from two concentrations: American/Latin
                     American history and European/Asian/African history.

                     An Engaging History Program

At TROY, you'll take exciting courses with engaging and innovative teaching methods.
                           From working firsthand with historical artifacts to experiencing history through gaming,
                           music and movies, you'll study history through an interactive lens. You'll also have
                           the opportunity to contribute to archival projects, conduct research and sharpen your
                           writing skills.

                     A B.S. in History with Global Perspectives

history major

Prepare for Law School with our Accelerated Law Option

If you're interested in going to law school, you can choose the accelerated law option
                              to earn your 
history degree
 and Juris Doctor (J.D.) degree within six years instead of seven or eight. TROY's

                  			Faculty Profiles

Elizabeth (Scout) Blum,  Ph.D.

Dr. Elizabeth "Scout" Blum's main areas of research involve the intersections of gender,
                                 age, and environmental attitudes over time. Dr. Blum is currently working on a project
                                 about environmental messages presented to American children since World War I. This
                                 project evolved from her first book, entitled Love Canal Revisited (University Press
                                 of Kansas, 2008). She is also a proponent of using board, video, and role-playing
                                 games in the classroom, and currently teaches a class entitled History Through Games.
                                 In that class, students work through various games to investigate depictions of historical
                                 events and themes, and end the class by designing their own game.

Margaret K. Gnoinska, Ph.D.

Dr. Gnoinska is a Fulbright Scholar and Boren NSEP Fellow. She has published, as well
                                 as presented, her research in the United States and around the world. She traveled
                                 extensively throughout East and Southeast Asia, including Japan, China, Korea, and

Allen E. Jones, Ph.D.

Dr. Allen Jones is the chair of the history and philosophy department. He has published
                                 several works about antiquities. In August 2020 Jones published Death and Afterlife
                                 in the Pages of Gregory of Tours: Religion and Society in Late Antique Gaul (Amsterdam:
                                 Amsterdam University Press, 2020). Bishop Gregory of Tours was a prolific writer of
                                 hagiography and author of a ten-book history popularly called the History of the Franks.
                                 In 2009 Jones published Social Mobility in Late Antique Gaul: Strategies and Opportunities
                                 for the Non-Elite (Cambridge and New York: Cambridge University Press; reprinted in
                                 paperback, 2014). The work investigates the extent to which people who were not part
                                 of the power structures of Gaul impacted society as they pursued their own goals.

FAQ About the History Program

Learn more about TROY's history major and what you can do with a history degree.

What can you do with a history degree?

A 
history degree 
opens doors to careers in education, public history, museums, research, law and various
                        other fields. The skills you develop, such as critical thinking and research, are
                        highly valued in many professions. Specific 
history degree jobs
 include history teacher, museum curator, archivist, research analyst, historical
                        consultant, public historian and policy advisor.

Can you be a teacher with a history degree?

Yes, a
 history degree
 can prepare you for a career in education, but you may need additional certification
                        depending on where you want to teach. Many graduates become history teachers at the
                        middle school, high school or even college level with a master's degree.

Can you get a history degree online?

Yes, you can get a 
history degree online
. TROY's online 
history degree 
program provides the same rigorous curriculum and quality education as our in-person
                        program, giving you the flexibility to study from anywhere.
