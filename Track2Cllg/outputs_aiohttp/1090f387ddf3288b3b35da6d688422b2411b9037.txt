Connecting with Chemistry

Hands down, Kendall McGahey '16 says she would choose UMHB again if she had the chance. “I really connected with people there since it was a small school.” Kendall, who is now earning her Doctor of Pharmacy degree at UNT Health Science Center to become a pediatric pharmacist, says this made it easy to get “plugged in.” While at UMHB, she was a teaching assistant for the chemistry department, president of the chemistry club, attended two chemistry conferences, and volunteered at the local children’s hospital. She especially loved creating a new program called “Chemistry with the Cru” that took chemistry experiments and activities to the hospital for pediatric patients to enjoy.

Why study a chemistry degree at UMHB?

Internships give majors opportunities to work in interesting settings such as the Baylor Scott & White Clinic Cyto-Genetics Lab and the U.S. Department of Agriculture lab in Temple. 

Chemistry labs are fitted with outstanding instrumentation: Atomic Absorption Spectroscopy, Colorimetry, Fluorescence Spectroscopy, Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy, Gas Chromatography, High Performance Liquid Chromatography, Magnetic Susceptibility, Mass Spectroscopy, Nuclear Magnetic Resonance, pH meters, Potentiostat, and Ultraviolet-Visible Spectrophotometers give majors opportunities to work with instruments they will encounter in the field after graduation.
