Department of Biological Sciences
From the inner workings of our DNA to the global impact of ecosystems, FIU Biological Sciences offers an immersive experience in the living laboratory of South Florida. Our graduates embark on careers in medicine, conservation, education and more. Join us and become part of a network of scientists dedicated to making a positive impact on the world.
Get the degree. Build the network. Change everything.

Life as a Biological Sciences Major
As a student, you will be part of the traditions and experiences that unite our department and build connections including:
Guest Lectures and Seminars
 – past speakers include top researchers and high-profile conservationists including Sylvia Earle and Jane Goodall.
Biology Research Forum and Summer Research Poster Showcase
 – opportunities for students to showcase latest research. 
Sprouting Program
 – literature review instruction for early undergraduates to get foundational research skills. 
Research Expo
 – tabling event providing information on research opportunities.
Organizations to grow your network
 including the Undergraduate Research Society, QBiC Club and Society for Women in Marine Science can be found on 
Panther Connect
.
