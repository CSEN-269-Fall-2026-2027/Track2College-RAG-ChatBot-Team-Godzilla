Accounting at Southwestern Adventist University provides a strong foundation in financial, managerial, and tax accounting that prepares students for success in both the corporate and non-profit worlds.

With pathways in both standard and professional accounting, students gain the knowledge and experience needed to pursue CPA certification and other advanced career opportunities.

Curriculum and Courses

Our comprehensive curriculum includes courses in financial accounting, auditing, income taxation, managerial accounting, and financial reporting. Through real-world case studies and hands-on projects, students build analytical skills and practical experience. Internships and business practicums provide essential exposure to professional environments, preparing students for success after graduation.

Admissions Requirements

Students interested in Accounting must first apply to Southwestern Adventist University. Visit our Admissions Page for application steps, scholarships, and deadlines.
