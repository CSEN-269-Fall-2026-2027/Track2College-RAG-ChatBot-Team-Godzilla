Undergraduate Admissions

The Biomedical Engineering (BME) undergraduate major at UF is a 
limited-enrollment program
. Freshman admission decisions are handled entirely by the UF Office of Admissions.  The BME department does not review or influence freshman applications. Students interested in BME should apply to UF as pre-BME majors. As pre-BME students, you’ll begin by completing the required critical-tracking courses to prepare for upper-division study.

For more information about applying to UF as a freshman, please visit 
www.admissions.ufl.edu

During the 
fall semester of sophomore year
, pre-BME students apply for admission to the upper-division BME program, which begins the following 
spring semester
. The department also admits a select number of transfer students in the fall, spring, and summer terms. Thanks to the program’s growth, up to 120 students per cohort are accepted.

Admission Requirements for Upper Division

To be considered for admission to the upper-division BME major, students must:

Earn at least a 
2.8 GPA 
in critical-tracking courses (best attempt counted
*
)

Receive a 
minimum grade of C
 in each critical-tracking course

Attempt each critical-tracking course 
no more than twice
 (withdrawals included)

Complete the first two semesters of the 
BME plan of study
 and be actively enrolled in the third semester by the fall of application.

Submit the 
BME departmental online application
. – (Only active during fall semester)

*
For admission, only the best attempt in each critical-tracking course will be considered.
