Undergraduate and Graduate Tuition and Fees

The Cost of Attendance (COA) displayed below is the 
estimated 
amount it costs to attend Otis College. The COA includes direct and indirect costs.
                  Direct costs are charges billed by the school. Indirect costs are estimated budgetary
                  numbers based on average student expenditures. Actual ‘indirect' costs vary from student
                  to student.

If you have any questions related to tuition and fees or the Cost of Attendance, we
                  encourage you to reach out to Office of Financial Aid at The One Stop by email 
onestop@otis.edu
 or by phone 310-665-6999. Please also review the 
net price calculator
 for additional information.

BFA/MFA programs have two semesters per academic year.
