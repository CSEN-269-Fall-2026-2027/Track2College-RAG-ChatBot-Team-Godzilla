Why should I study Finance?

No organization can properly function and succeed without the tools and analyses used by financial professionals.
If you enjoy planning, strategy, and have an affinity for numbers, the finance program at UTPB may be your calling!

What are my job prospects?

A Bachelor of Business Administration in Finance will set you on the path for many career opportunities, including:

Corporate Financial Analyst 

Credit Analyst 

Investment Analyst 

Financial Planner 

Accounting Manager

Banker

What kind of skills will I learn?

Earning a degree in finance will prepare you for real-world job responsibilities. Finance encompasses the management of money at the personal, corporate, and public levels.
Students learn the big picture of business and can prepare for skills in investing, decision-making, management, and technology skills to leverage new financial systems.

Our business programs have once again been extended accreditation by the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB). These programs include Management, Marketing, Accounting, Finance, and our graduate programs
