How to Apply

USC offers exceptional students a world-class education, with top-ranked programs in virtually every discipline — from cinema to the sciences. Here, students begin a journey of intellectual challenges, personal and professional achievement, and lifelong friendships.

What We Look For

We look for students we believe will thrive in our world-class academic environment. Our review process is holistic, taking into consideration more than just grades and test scores. We also look at your personal qualities, your potential, and your ability to become a partner in learning and research. The Common App for each admission cycle opens August 1.

First-Year Students

Incoming first-year students demonstrate superior academic performance — and the potential to do more. Generally, they have taken the most rigorous academic schedule available to them. First-year students can apply Early Decision, Early Action or Regular Decision.

Learn more

Transfer Students

USC accepts sophomore and junior transfer students from across California, the U.S., and around the world. Successful transfer students have demonstrated their potential to succeed academically in our dynamic academic programs.

Learn more

International Students

USC enrolls international students from around the globe and hosts one of the most vibrant student populations in the U.S. You are considered an international student if you will enter or have already entered the United States with a visa to live and study.
