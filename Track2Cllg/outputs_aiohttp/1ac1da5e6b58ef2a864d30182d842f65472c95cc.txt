Campus Community
At Southwestern Adventist University, the Music program is grounded in a commitment to excellence and performance. Whether your goal is to compose, perform, teach, or inspire, you will be supported by a close-knit department with a passion for nurturing talent.

With regular performance opportunities, one-on-one mentorship, and ensemble tours both locally and globally, students gain immersive musical experiences that prepare them for rewarding careers in the music industry.

Curriculum and Courses

Our music curriculum includes courses in music theory, history, ear training, conducting, and ensemble performance. Students receive personalized instruction through private lessons and take part in studio sessions and group ensembles. These experiences build both technical proficiency and artistic expression, helping you grow as a well-rounded musician.

Admission Requirements

Applying to the Music program begins with admission to Southwestern Adventist University. Visit our Admissions Page to learn how to apply, explore financial aid opportunities, and connect with an admissions counselor.
