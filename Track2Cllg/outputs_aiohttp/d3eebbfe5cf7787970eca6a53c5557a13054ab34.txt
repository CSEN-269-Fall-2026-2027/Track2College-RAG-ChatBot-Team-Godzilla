Important Deadlines

						October 1 - FAFSA/TASFA and Scholarship Applications Open

Complete these important applications as soon as they open in October. Certain types of financial aid are available on a first-come, first-served basis, so don't wait to apply!

						January 15 - First-Year FAFSA/TASFA Priority Deadline

To maximize your financial aid opportunity as a first-year student for the upcoming academic year, we encourage you to submit your FAFSA or TASFA to Texas A&M University-San Antonio by this date.

						March 15 - Transfer FAFSA/TASFA Priority Deadline

To maximize your financial aid opportunity as a transfer student for the upcoming academic year, we encourage you to submit your FAFSA or TASFA to Texas A&M University-San Antonio by this date.

						Early April, Current Student Offers Posted & Summer Aid

Award Notifications are sent to current students for the next academic year. Also, Summer Financial Aid awards begin in April.

						June 30 - General Scholarship Application Closes

This is the final deadline! The 
Texas A&M University-San Antonio
 General Scholarship Application closes on June 30.

						June 30 - FAFSA/TASFA Final Application Deadline

This is the final deadline! Your last chance to submit your FAFSA/TASFA to Texas A&M University-San Antonio closes on June 30.
