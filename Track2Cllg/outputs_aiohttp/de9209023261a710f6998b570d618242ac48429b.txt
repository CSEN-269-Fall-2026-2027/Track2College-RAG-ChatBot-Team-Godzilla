Experience TLU
Personalized Campus Visits

Visiting campus is one of the best ways to see if TLU is the right place for you. TLU offers personalized campus visits Monday through Friday. In addition to your campus tour and admissions presentation, you’ll have the option to personalize your visit schedule with the following activities:

Meet with a professor

Attend an actual TLU class

Enjoy a complimentary lunch with students

Attend a chapel service

Meet with financial aid

Connect with a coach or performing arts director

We were greeted with such warmth that it made a lasting impression on us. We really enjoyed the 1:1 tour of campus and were given great information regarding the application process and scholarships.
