Meet Financial Aid

Investing in your education

At the Mount, we believe that your best investment is an education, and its pursuit should not be limited by financial concerns. For that reason, we are committed to helping you afford your college education by offering both merit and need-based financial aid.

Traditional undergraduate students
﻿

Post-traditional undergraduate students
﻿

For students interested in our Weekend/Evening & Online College, professional certificate programs and our adult nursing programs (Accelerated BSN and RN to BSN).

Graduate students

For students interested in our graduate programs.

Financial Aid at the Mount

We understand that managing the rising cost of education, particularly at a private college, can be challenging. Our Office of Financial Aid is dedicated to educating students and their families about the financial aid process while providing the financial resources and support that will enable our students to invest in their future.
