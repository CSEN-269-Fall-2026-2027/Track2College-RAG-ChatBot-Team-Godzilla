Computer Science | Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

The bachelors of science in Computer Science prepares students to excel in the rapidly expanding fields of software engineering, design, and security. The Computer Science curriculum aligns with national standards, and all courses are taught by highly-qualified faculty members who work to provide each student with a positive, personalized learning experience. The curriculum is presented in a Christian context, which gives students opportunities to discuss topics such as professional ethics and how computer science can be used to find creative and compassionate solutions to the problems faced in our world.

Important Internships in Software Engineering

When Jessica Livingston completed her BS in Computer Science in 2017, she knew exactly where she would be headed next. “The internship hours required for my degree were a big help in preparing for my first job,” she recalls. “As a student, I was hired to be an intern at Integrity, a local software development company that specializes in electronic medical records for ophthalmologists and optometrists. When my degree was complete, they offered me a fulltime job as a software developer.” The internship provided the perfect finish to her UMHB experience. “I really enjoyed my time at UMHB,” she says. “All of the people there were so willing and ready to help with whatever I needed. I wouldn’t have wanted to go to any other school.”

Why study a computer science degree at UMHB?

All students majoring in Computer Science are required to complete a minimum of 300 hours of practical experience while enrolled at UMHB, through full-time employment, part-time employment, internships, apprenticeships, international experiences, or co-op experiences. Professors work to match students to opportunities for paid internships, but students can also seek other positions on their own with approval by CSEP faculty. These internship experiences often serve as stepping stones to full-time employment in the students’ areas of interest after graduation.
