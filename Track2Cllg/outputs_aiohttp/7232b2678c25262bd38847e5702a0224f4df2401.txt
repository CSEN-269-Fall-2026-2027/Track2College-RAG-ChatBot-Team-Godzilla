                     Create Your Own Career with the Chemistry Major

                     Do you have a passion for scientific discovery? Are you fascinated by how substances
                     interact and transform? Then consider the Bachelor of Science in Chemistry at Troy
                     University. As a TROY chemistry major, you'll work closely with faculty, gain research
                     experience and develop problem-solving skills valued by employers in fields like healthcare,
                     education and business. You'll also benefit from small class sizes that promote personalized
                     learning and mentorship. Whether you plan to enter the workforce, pursue advanced
                     studies or teach, a bachelor's in chemistry from TROY prepares you for success.

                     Research Experience that Sets You Apart

Conduct research in diverse fields like organic chemistry, biochemistry and drug discovery
                        alongside TROY's accomplished faculty. As a chemistry major, you can also participate
                        in summer programs through the 
U.S. National Science Foundation Research Experiences for Undergraduates (NSF REU)
. Through it, you'll collaborate with active researchers and explore opportunities
                        at national laboratories and graduate schools across the country to boost your academic
                        and career credentials. These experiences build your research and collaboration skills
                        and make your graduate school — and job — applications stand out.

FAQ About the Chemistry Degree Program

Learn more about the field of chemistry and our program.

What can you do with a chemistry degree from TROY?

With a bachelor's in chemistry from TROY, you can pursue 
careers in chemistry
, such as pharmaceutical research, environmental science, forensic analysis or education.

Chemistry degree jobs
 include roles such as analytical chemist, biochemist, chemical engineer, forensic
                        scientist and quality control analyst. TROY's 
chemistry curriculum
 equips you with practical skills and knowledge that are highly sought after in both
                        industry and academia. The skills you gain also prepare you for graduate or professional
                        school in fields like medicine and dentistry. 

What puts TROY among the best chemistry programs?

TROY offers small class sizes, hands-on lab experience and faculty mentorship that
                        put it right up there with the 
best chemistry programs
 in the country. At TROY, you'll gain a strong foundation in theory and practice that
                        prepare you for 
careers in chemistry
 or further academic study.

Are chemistry internships available for TROY students?

Yes! TROY supports chemistry internships through faculty connections, national programs
                        and partnerships with labs and industries. 
Chemistry internships
 help you gain real-world experience and make your 
chemistry degree
 more valuable in the job market.
