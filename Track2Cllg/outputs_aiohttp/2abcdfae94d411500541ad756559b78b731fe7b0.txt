Financial Aid & Scholarships

At Wayland, we are committed to making our private Christian education affordable for students and families. To that end, our faithful, knowledgeable professionals in the Office of Financial Aid will work closely with you to explore every possible financial aid option. A life-changing Wayland education is within reach – let us show you how.

Complete the 
Free Application for Federal Student Aid 
(FAFSA) to be considered for federal, state, and institutional financial aid. The school code for WBU is 003663. You will need to provide information about your income and assets.

Complete the 
WBU Scholarship Application 
(2026-27 applications are due by September 15, 2026) to be considered for several hundred need-based institutional scholarships. The Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) is required for consideration.
