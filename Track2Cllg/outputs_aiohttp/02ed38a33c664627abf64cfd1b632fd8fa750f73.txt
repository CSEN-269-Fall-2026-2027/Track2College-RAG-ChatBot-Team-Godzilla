Texas Teaching Certifications | Education

The College of Education offers undergraduate and post-baccalaureate teacher certification programs at four levels: Early Childhood-6th Grade, 4th-8th Grade, 7th-12th Grade (Secondary), and Interdisciplinary (which includes teaching special needs students, English language learners, and elementary students). You may also choose to pursue all-level certification in art, music, physical education, or Spanish, or secondary certification in many other areas.
