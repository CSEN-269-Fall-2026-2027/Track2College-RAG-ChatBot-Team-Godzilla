Why Study Accounting at Trinity?

Accounting is more than numbers. Accounting is about solving problems, building relationships and making informed decisions that shape organizations and communities.  At Trinity, you’ll develop technical expertise, professional judgment and confidence to succeed through close faculty mentorship, meaningful engagement with employers and alumni and hands-on experiences that connect the classroom to the real world.
The people you learn with today become the colleagues, mentors and friends who shape your future. That's the Trinity difference.

Trinity provided the tools to start building my career from zero. I learned from my dad that the important thing is not how long you live, but what you accomplish with your life. Every step has prepared me to fulfill my dream of serving the public, honoring my father's legacy while making my own mark.

James King '23, M'24
