At Southwestern Adventist University, our English program offers a dynamic exploration of literature, writing, and human expression.

With small class sizes and dedicated professors, you will engage deeply with classic and contemporary texts, hone your creative and analytical writing skills, and participate in meaningful classroom dialogue. You will also have the opportunity to gain real-world experience through internships, including teaching and tutoring roles at our on-campus writing center, The Write Spot.

Curriculum and Courses

Our English curriculum balances creative and critical development. You will take courses in literature, composition, rhetoric, and advanced writing. Interactive workshops and close textual analysis deepen your ability to think critically and communicate with clarity. Hands-on opportunities in writing, editing, and teaching prepare you for careers in a wide range of fields.

The Write Spot

The Write Spot is our on-campus writing center, where students can work as peer tutors and mentors. Through this experience, you will strengthen your teaching, communication, and leadership skills while supporting fellow students with their writing. It is a unique opportunity to engage in human-centered service and gain experience in education and communication.

Faculty & Mentorship

Our English faculty brings a passion for literature, writing, and student success. They offer personalized guidance, helping students grow as writers, readers, and thinkers. You will benefit from close mentorship and collaborative learning in a supportive academic environment.
