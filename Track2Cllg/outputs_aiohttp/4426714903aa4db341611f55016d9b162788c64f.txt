Frequently Asked Questions about a Business Administration Degree from St. Edward's University

What will I learn as a Business Administration major at St. Edward's University?

The Basics
As a Business Administration major, you'll build a strong foundation across the core areas of business, including management, marketing, finance, accounting and business strategy. You'll learn how organizations operate, how to make informed decisions and how to lead teams and projects in an increasingly global and technology-driven economy.
Along the way, you'll develop highly transferable skills that are valued by employers across industries, including:
Leadership and teamwork
Strategic thinking and problem-solving
Communication and presentation skills
Data analysis and decision-making
Project management
Ethical decision-making
Collaboration and relationship-building
Adaptability and innovation
Critical and creative thinking
Intercultural skills
Experiential Learning
Business is best learned by doing. Through internships, case studies, consulting projects, business competitions and experiential learning opportunities, you'll apply classroom concepts to real-world challenges while building your professional network and résumé.
Students also benefit from St. Edward's location in Austin, one of the nation's leading centers for innovation, entrepreneurship and business growth. Through Career Treks, networking events and employer partnerships, you'll gain firsthand exposure to a wide range of industries and career paths.
Professional Development
From your first year through graduation, faculty mentors, academic advisors and career coaches will help you explore your interests, identify career goals and prepare for internships and employment opportunities. Whether you're interested in entrepreneurship, marketing, finance, management or another business field, you'll graduate with the skills, confidence and experience to take the next step.

What are the requirements for the Business Administration degree at St. Edward's?

Major Requirements:

The BBA in Business Administration requires 69 hours of major coursework.
General Education Requirements:

All majors require 44 hours of general education that students complete over four years, in addition to their major courses.

A few examples of the Business Administration courses students take:
International Business Administration
 – Work in teams to research current business environments, and gain an understanding of the business ethics and concepts used in developed versus developing nations.
Strategic Business Analysis and Practice
 – Learn to gather, analyze and use data in making executive decisions. This course examines business practice from the executive team’s perspective.
Internship
 – Participate in a public or private, paid or unpaid, internship at an organization, and reflect on the experience by demonstrating your accomplishments and development of skills.

What internship and career preparation opportunities are there as a Business Administration major at St. Edward's University?

Overview
As part of the Business Administration major, students at St. Edward’s gain real-world experience through a required internship embedded into the curriculum, industry-recognized certifications and the Munday School of Business’ strong connections to Austin organizations and employers.
Learn by Doing
In the Social Responsibility of Business course, you’ll participate in a service activity in the community and write a paper about the experience. You’ll also work in a team to create a new product or service for a company that will meet a social need for an underserved community.
In the Organizational Behavior and Leading Teams course, you’ll work in teams to interview top leaders in local businesses and non-profit organizations. In addition to learning about leadership and building your social networks, you will devise plans to develop your own leadership skills.
St. Edward’s has a full-scale, state-of-the-art Bloomberg Finance lab (with 12 terminals). Students can earn three Bloomberg certifications on campus. Earn a Bloomberg Certification to boost your skill set and marketability. The eight-hour, self-directed course on Bloomberg Market Concepts covers economics, fixed income, equities and currencies; how to use the Terminal; and portfolio management. Including a Bloomberg Certification or @bloomberg.net email address on your resume and LinkedIn profile shows prospective employers you know how to use the Terminal.
Internships
You'll stay competitive in the market. All BBA students complete at least one internship before graduation, preparing you for real world scenarios, and real world networking. In recent semesters, Business Administration majors have completed internships at the following organizations:

 Making an Impact
Business Administration alumnus Marco Martinez ’22 
turned hands-on experience at St. Edward’s into a career blending business, marketing and design. As a student, he gained practical experience as a marketing coordinator for the Office of Recreation and Wellness, while faculty mentorship helped him prepare for the transition from college to career.
