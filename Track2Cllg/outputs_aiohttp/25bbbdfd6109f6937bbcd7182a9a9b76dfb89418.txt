Degree

If you're curious about how 
living systems
 function, adapt, and interact with the world around them, a degree in Biology can open the door to a wide range of scientific, environmental, healthcare, and research careers.

At 
LeTourneau University
, students do more than learn biology in the classroom. Through extensive laboratory and field experiences, faculty mentorship, and undergraduate research opportunities, they develop practical skills in scientific investigation, data analysis, and problem solving.

The Bachelor of Science degree in Biology provides a strong foundation in cell biology, genetics, microbiology, and ecology. Students gain both laboratory and field experience while preparing for careers, graduate study, and professional programs.

Biologists
 contribute to a wide variety of fields, including environmental science, biotechnology, laboratory sciences, ecological research, conservation, public health, and scientific education.

WHAT CAN YOU DO WITH THIS DEGREE?

The Biology degree prepares students for a variety of life science, graduate, and professional pathways, including:

Graduate study in biology, ecology, environmental science, genetics, microbiology, and related fields

Medical, dental, veterinary, and other professional programs

Environmental science and natural resource management

Biotechnology and laboratory sciences

Ecological and environmental research

Government and private research laboratories

Secondary science education

LEARN SCIENCE BY DOING SCIENCE

At LeTourneau University, Biology students gain hands-on experience through laboratory coursework, faculty-mentored research, internships, and professional conference participation. These opportunities allow students to apply classroom knowledge, develop technical skills, and strengthen their preparation for professional health programs and scientific careers.

SCIENCE & FAITH SEMINAR

As part of their educational experience, chemistry students are encouraged to participate in the 
Science & Faith Seminar series
, hosted by the Department of Natural Sciences. The seminar brings together students, faculty, and guest speakers to explore scientific discovery within a broader Christian worldview and to discuss important questions raised by advances in science and technology.

EXPLORE RELATED DEGREES

Biomedical Sciences, B.S.

Interested in a career in healthcare? The Biomedical Sciences degree provides focused preparation for medical, dental, pharmacy, veterinary, physician assistant, and other health-profession programs through advanced coursework in human biology, genetics, microbiology, and biochemistry.

Chemistry-Biological Concentration

Combine a strong chemistry foundation with advanced coursework in biochemistry, cell biology, genetics, and related life sciences. Well suited for students interested in biotechnology, biomedical research, and professional health programs.
