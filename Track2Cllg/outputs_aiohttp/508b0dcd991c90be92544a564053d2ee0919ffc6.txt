Why Study Global Latinx Studies?

Global Latinx Studies explores the ideas, histories, and lived experiences that shape Latinx communities across the Americas through migration, identity, language, politics, and culture.

Study Abroad and Immersion

Participate in language and cultural immersion programs in Spain and the Americas, or study ecosystems firsthand in places like Costa Rica.

Capstone Portfolio

In your senior year, you’ll complete a portfolio that integrates your work across multiple disciplines, demonstrating how your learning connects.

Community Partnerships

Work with organizations in San Antonio and beyond, building connections between academic study and community impact.

It’s a good fit for you if you:

Want to understand how culture, language, and history shape communities.
Are interested in issues of identity, migration, and social change.
Value learning across disciplines rather than within a single field.
Want to connect academic study with real-world communities and experiences.

What You'll Study

Build an interdisciplinary foundation through courses in history, literature, politics, sociology, anthropology, and the arts. You’ll learn to examine complex issues from multiple perspectives while connecting classroom learning to contemporary communities.

In Demand-Skills:
Analyze cultural and social systems across local and global contexts
Communicate effectively across languages and diverse communities
Apply interdisciplinary thinking to complex social and cultural issues

Comprehensive Curriculum
Students complete 34 credit hours across multiple disciplines, examining Latinx diasporas in regions shaped by Spanish and Portuguese histories.

Recent Graduate Careers

Global Latinx Studies graduates combine cultural knowledge, research, communication, and critical thinking to pursue careers in public service, education, nonprofit leadership, business, policy, and graduate study.

Community Advocacy and Public Service
Graduates work with nonprofit organizations, advocacy groups, and community initiatives focused on Latinx populations and broader social impact. They apply cultural knowledge and community-based experience to support meaningful change.

Education and Cultural Engagement
Graduates enter education, cultural institutions, and outreach roles where they teach, interpret, and share knowledge of Latinx histories, languages, and cultures with diverse audiences.

Policy, Government, and Public Affairs
Students pursue roles in public policy, government agencies, and civic leadership, bringing an understanding of cultural systems, migration, and social structures to decision-making and public service.

Business, Communication, and Global Organizations
With strong intercultural and multilingual skills, graduates contribute to roles in marketing, public relations, and international organizations that require communication across cultures and communities.
