College is an investment—and at Concordia Texas, we believe it should be a smart one.

                  admissions counselor is just a call, text, or email away—schedule your personalized
                  financial aid appointment today.

2027-2028 FASFA Information

Need-Based Aid

Aid awarded based on your financial situation, as determined by the FAFSA. This can
                              include grants, work-study, and loans.

Merit-Based Aid

Aid awarded based on your achievements—like academics, leadership, or athletics—regardless
                              of your financial need.

Institutional Aid

Funding provided directly by Concordia Texas, including scholarships, grants, and
                              tuition assistance aligned with our mission and values.
