Academic Excellence Rooted in Tradition
A Westmont education is grounded in the enduring strength of the liberal arts, forming habits of thought that endure across disciplines and throughout a lifetime. Here, students learn not simply what to think, but how to think with depth, clarity, and intellectual freedom.
