Why Study International Studies?

Understand how global issues shape communities, economies, and societies. Students will explore international challenges through multiple disciplines while building cultural awareness, analytical skills, and a global perspective. 

Connect Ideas Across Disciplines

Examine international issues through politics, economics, history, language, and culture to better understand an interconnected world.

Gain Global Experience

Study abroad, conduct research, and engage directly with international perspectives through immersive learning opportunities.

Learn From Global Scholars

Work closely with faculty whose expertise spans regions, languages, cultures, and global systems.

The International Studies Major (B.A.)

Develop the knowledge and perspective to navigate a globally connected world. The major combines interdisciplinary coursework with regional and thematic study, helping you understand how international systems, institutions, and communities interact.

In-Demand Skills
Cross-cultural communication
Global research and analysis
Critical thinking and problem-solving
International policy awareness
Adaptability in diverse environments

Curriculum:
You’ll complete at least 33 credit hours in the areas of international politics and global studies, with language proficiency and study abroad or experiential learning requirements.

The International Studies Minor

21 Credit Hours  |  1.5 to 2 semesters of coursework 
Add a global perspective to your primary field of study. 
The International Studies minor helps you understand how international forces influence local and global issues while strengthening your ability to work across cultures.
Why Add the Minor?
Whether you're majoring in business, political science, economics, language studies, or another discipline, the minor broadens your understanding of global systems and international challenges.

Recent Graduate Careers

Apply global knowledge, cultural understanding, and analytical thinking to challenges that cross borders. International Studies prepares you to work across sectors where communication, adaptability, and global awareness create impact.

Diplomacy, Government, and Public Service
Analyze international issues, evaluate policy decisions, and understand how governments and institutions interact on a global scale. Graduates pursue careers in diplomacy, government agencies, public affairs, and international policy.
International Development and Nonprofit Leadership
Work with organizations that address global challenges related to education, economic development, human rights, sustainability, and community engagement.

Global Business and International Organizations
Bring a global perspective to organizations operating across countries, markets, and cultures. International Studies complements careers in multinational corporations, consulting, international trade, and global strategy.
Research, Graduate Study, and Law
Build strong research, writing, and analytical skills through interdisciplinary coursework and faculty mentorship. These experiences provide excellent preparation for graduate study, law school, and academic research.
