Planning ahead and applying for financial aid can help students obtain an education that might otherwise be outside their financial reach. Getting started with your college journey includes understanding not just how to apply, but how to fund your education.

In this video, we walk you through key steps in the application and financial aid process, including tips on completing the FAFSA, exploring scholarships, and what to expect after you submit your information. Whether you’re new to the process or just need a refresher, this guide helps make applying for financial aid more manageable and clear.

Application –> Verification –> Award

Step 1: Application

To establish your eligibility for aid, you will be asked to complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA). The FAFSA serves as the initial application for most Federal Aid Programs and is used to establish your eligibility for financial assistance. To assure timely processing, please complete the FAFSA fully and accurately.

The federal processor will analyze your data and calculate your Expected Family Contribution (EFC). Once your EFC has been determined, the federal processor will email you a Student Aid Report (SAR), which you should review for accuracy. At that time, the University will also begin processing your award.

To receive financial aid each award year at the University, you must file the current year FAFSA and submit the requested verification documents if required. New FAFSA applications are available from the US Department of Education after October 1.

Step 2: Verification

The Financial Aid Office will receive a copy of your FAFSA application from the U.S Department of Education. An email will be sent notifying students who are selected by the Department of Education, that they are required to complete the verification process. This will direct you to the student portal and your Financial Aid checklist.
