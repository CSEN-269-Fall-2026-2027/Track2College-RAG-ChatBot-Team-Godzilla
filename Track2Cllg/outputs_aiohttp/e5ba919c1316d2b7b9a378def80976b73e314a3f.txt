Apply for Housing

Student Housing at Baptist University of the Américas is for 
full-time
 students. Full-time equals to 
12 hours
 of enrollment for the semester on the English as a Second Language program or the Associate or Bachelor degree programs. For Master degree students, full-time status equals 
9 hours
 of enrollment per semester.

Prospective students
 seeking to apply for campus housing, 
must first apply for admission to the university and be accepted as a student
. You can start this process by clicking on the “APPLY NOW” link at the top of this page. Upon acceptance, the student can apply for housing on their student portal. If their housing application is approved, they can proceed with paying their housing deposit to secure their space.

Current students
 seeking to apply for campus housing, must complete the housing application in the housing portal. If their housing application is approved, they can proceed with paying their housing deposit to secure their space.

Housing deposits
 are $300 for dormitories (single students); $770 and $935 respectively for one and two-bedroom apartments (married students). The deposit is refundable upon vacancy of the room or apartment, if no damage or unusual wear and tear is found during the facilities exit review. A student who moves into campus housing and subsequently moves out prior to the end of the semester forfeits their deposit.

Move-In Dates

Move-in to student housing begins the week before each semester starts and ends on 
September 15
 for the fall semester and 
February 15
 for the spring semester. These are the only times when students can move into campus housing. Please plan accordingly so that your transition to live in the university is a smooth one.

Background checks will be required for housing applicants and, where applicable, for all household members 18 and older.
 Negative background checks will not necessarily disqualify the applicant from studying at the University but may determine whether or not the applicant will be authorized to live on campus.

Pet Policy:
 Only service animals are allowed on campus. The University defines a 
Service Animal
 as a 
dog
 that has been individually trained to do work or perform tasks for the benefit of an individual with a disability. Other animals, whether domestic or wild, do not qualify as Service Animals and are not allowed in university property.
