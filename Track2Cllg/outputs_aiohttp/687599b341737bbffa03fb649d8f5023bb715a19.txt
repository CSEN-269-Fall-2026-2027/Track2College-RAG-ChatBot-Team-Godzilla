Sport Management Degree Program

A sport Management major prepares students for a variety of entry-level management and administrative positions in the exciting world of sports. The Sport Management degree helps students develop the skills needed to organize, administer, and facilitate sport programs at the corporate, agency, amateur, and professional levels. Graduates of the program are also prepared to pursue a graduate degree, if they so desire

Living the Dream

Daniel Davila’s dream was to just get his “foot in the door” and work for a professional sports organization. But this Sport Management graduate has already been able to work for two national teams in the short time since he graduated in 2017. He currently works for the Dallas Stars hockey team and prior to that worked for the Texas Rangers baseball team. “It’s been an amazing journey so far,” he said. He credits the Sport Management program in preparing him for his success by giving him insights on how the sports industry functions. “Our curriculum covered real-life situations and provided tools that could be used outside of the classroom and in my profession.”

What makes Sport Management at UMHB special?

The Bachelor of Sport Management provides students the opportunity to customize their educational experience to fit their desired career goals. 

 Students have opportunities to attend conferences where they can learn and network with people in the sports industry.

Majors complete a field experience in a professional setting to gain hands-on experience and learn about current trends and practices in the sports management arena. The program’s relationship with UMHB athletics also offers opportunities for students to practice skills and gain experience.
