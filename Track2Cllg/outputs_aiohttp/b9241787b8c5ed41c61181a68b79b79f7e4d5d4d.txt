Overview
Accounting is a complex, in-demand career — one that requires both a high level of technical skill and unwavering ethical standards. While technology and Artificial Intelligence (AI) are transforming the accounting profession, the demand for more qualified accountants with higher-level skills continues to grow. Biola University's accounting major curriculum is aligned with current industry needs, preparing graduates for successful careers in public accounting, private industry, and beyond.

In addition to its strong curriculum, expert faculty members and biblical integration, Biola’s accounting program offers several key advantages:

In addition to honing technical expertise, students in Biola’s Accounting program develop transferable skills in career-relevant areas, including financial analysis, management advising, valuation services and auditing, while building proficiency working with industry software such as AI, Tableau, Excel and data visualization platforms.
Biola accounting graduates have a very high job placement record — including placement with the “Big Four” national accounting firms — due in part to their reputation of being trustworthy, principled financial professionals.
The 
Biola Accounting Society
 is a great resource that hosts meetings with local professionals, offers career guidance and sponsors an annual “Meet the Firms” event featuring national, regional and local CPA firms.
Biola’s Crowell School of Business has been accredited by the Accreditation Council for Business Schools and Programs (ACBSP) since 1997.

Program-Specific Admissions Requirements

Admission to Biola University does not automatically guarantee admission as an accounting major within the Crowell School of Business, nor does it grant immediate permission to enroll in upper-division accounting courses.

To officially enter the program, students must meet the following criteria (applicable to the 2026 Catalog onwards):

Complete, with a minimum of a "C" (2.0) grade in each of the following courses, or the equivalent; with a cumulative grade point average of 2.50:
BUSN 211 — Principles of Accounting I
BUSN 212 — Principles of Accounting II
BUSN 240 — Principles of Management
Accomplish an overall cumulative GPA of 2.50 in all college level course work completed at the time of formal application to the Crowell School of Business.
Complete all portions of the application for admission to the Crowell School of Business. The application should be filed the semester in which the student is in progress of completing the prerequisite course requirements.
Meet approval of the academic advisor of the Crowell School of Business. The process may require an interview.
