St. Thomas Commitments

Automatic Consideration for a Scholarship

100-percent of first-time, first-year students are offered a St. Thomas scholarship. Outside aid does not reduce the St. Thomas scholarship for most students.

Merit-Based Scholarships Not Reduced During Program

The amount of your St. Thomas merit-based scholarship awards will not be reduced during your undergraduate program (for up to eight semesters) as long as you are consecutively enrolled as a full-time, degree-seeking student making Satisfactory Academic Progress, and the aid you receive from St. Thomas and outside sources does not exceed your cost of attendance.

Aid Packages Can Be Reevaluated

If your family experiences a substantial change in financial circumstances, we will reevaluate your aid package and provide additional financial resources if you qualify. Your financial aid counselor will help you navigate next steps.

Need-Based Options

Anyone applying for need-based aid needs to submit either the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) or the Minnesota (MN) Alternative State Financial Aid Application.

FAFSA

The Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) is the primary way most students gain access to need-based financial assistance to help fund their education.

FAFSA

Minnesota Alternative State Financial Aid Application

The Minnesota Alternative State Financial Aid Application allows undocumented students who are residents of Minnesota to be eligible for state financial aid and grant programs.
