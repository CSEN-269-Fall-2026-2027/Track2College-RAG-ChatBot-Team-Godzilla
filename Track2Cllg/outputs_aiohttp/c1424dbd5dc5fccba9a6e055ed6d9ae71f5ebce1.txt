Department of English
The Department of English equips students with the advanced critical thinking, rhetorical analysis, writing, and compelling communication skills essential for the 21st-century workplace. Our unique strengths shine through our diverse tracks in Writing and Rhetoric, Linguistics, Creative Writing, and Literature. 
Our graduates have gone on to successful careers as 
educators, legal analysts, authors, grant writers, advertising executives, content strategists, journalists, editors, novelists and much more. 
Get the degree. Build the network. Change everything.

Creative Writing
Our MFA in Creative Writing is an internationally recognized program that has produced New York Times bestsellers, poet laureates and Hollywood screenwriters. Our dedicated faculty include award-winning writers, a Pulitzer Prize finalist, Guggenheim Fellows and Humanities Medal recipient.
