A Place to Grow as a Musician, and More

Whether you’re pursuing a degree or simply want to continue performing, creating, or exploring music alongside another major, Trinity offers an inclusive, high-level musical experience within a liberal arts environment.
With more than twenty ensembles, scholarship opportunities, and variety of major options, the Department of Music is committed to assuring that meaningful and rewarding participation with music is achievable throughout your time at Trinity.

Music is open to all students

You don’t have to major in music to be part of it.  Unlike some larger Schools of Music or conservatories, the Music Department, like all departments at Trinity, are open to all interested and qualified students, irrespective of one’s major.
Students across disciplines, science, business, humanities, and more, perform in ensembles, take lessons, and participate fully in the musical life of campus.

Music Majors

Trinity offers both a B.A. in Music (conducive for a double major) and a B.M. in performance, composition, or music education. The fifth-year Master of Arts in Teaching allows music education majors to receive both the B.M. and M.A.T. in five years.

Music (B.A., Minor)

Explore music through performance, analysis, composition, and cultural study while building creative, collaborative, and communication skills.
