FSU earns No. 1 national ranking among kinesiology doctoral programs

            Florida State University’s doctoral program in kinesiology has earned the No. 1 spot in the National Academy of Kinesiology’s (NAK) 2026 rankings. Housed in the Anne Spencer Daves College of Education, Health, and Human Sciences (Anne’s College), the kinesiology program, which explores human movement and physical activity, rose from No. 3 in the 2023 rankings. Released every three years, the evaluation examined data from 2023 through 2025 across 39 participating universities.
