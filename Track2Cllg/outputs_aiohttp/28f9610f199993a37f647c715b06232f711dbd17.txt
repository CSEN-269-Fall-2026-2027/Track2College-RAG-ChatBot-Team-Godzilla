ABOUT THE SOCIOLOGY BA

FAU's Sociology major is distinctive as a flexible skills based major. The major lets students choose a plan of study that suits their career interests, focuses on topics that intrigue and challenge them, and helps them to build their own "sociological tool kit" for addressing personal and social problems. Every course includes coverage of core skills and basic knowledge of the discipline in addition to material that is distinctive to the topics. Because of how the major is designed, students who major in Sociology will have many opportunities to develop the skills necessary to ask and answer questions about social behavior, social structure and change, and social trends as well as to read skillfully, write and speak clearly and conduct effective presentations.

Requirements

The B.A. in Sociology requires a minimum of 120 credits.

The major requires that at least 33 credits be taken in sociology including:  

SYG 1000 Sociological Perspectives*

SYA 3010 Sociological Theory

SYA 3300 Sociological Analysis: Survey of Research Methods

18 credits of 3000-level electives in sociology

6 credits of 4000-level electives in sociology**

*SYG 1000 is a prerequisite for both SYA 3010 and SYA 3300.  SYD 3792 may be substituted for SYG 1000 to fulfill the prerequisite requirement. Students who take SYD 3792 will require only 30 credits in sociology to complete the BA in Sociology.

**SYA 3010 and SYA 3300 are prerequisites for 4000-level sociology courses. Students are encouraged to take SYA 3010 and SYA 3300 fairly early in their program but no later than the term prior to the term when they expect to graduate.  4000-level courses differ from 3000-level by engaging students in original sociological research projects; students may elect to take more than two 4000-level sociology courses, and these additional credits may substitute for 3000-level credits.

Students majoring in Sociology are required to earn a grade of "C" or better in all sociology courses that are counted towards the Sociology major's minimum requirement of 30 credits at the 3000 and 4000 levels.

All majors at FAU require a minimum of 45 upper-division credits (including the 30 required for the Sociology major). Sociology majors are strongly encouraged to take more than 10 upper division courses in Sociology and/or to pursue a second major, minor or certificate program in another field. As long as all University and College requirements are met, the Sociology major allows students to reach their required number of credits in any way they choose.

Prerequisite Coursework for Transfer Students

Students transferring to Florida Atlantic University must complete both lower-division requirements (including the requirements of the Intellectual Foundations Program) and requirements for the college and major. Lower-division requirements may be completed through the A.A. degree from any Florida public college, university or community college or through equivalent coursework at another regionally accredited institution. Foreign Language

Sociology majors are required to take eight credits of appropriate college-level courses in one modern foreign language. College-level transfer credits or credits earned through CLEP or Advanced Placement Examination may satisfy or be applied toward the foreign language requirement for graduation.
