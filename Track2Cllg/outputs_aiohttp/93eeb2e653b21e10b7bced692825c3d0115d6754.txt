Why should I study sociology?

UT Permian Basin offers two paths to earning a BA in Sociology—face-to-face and online. In our program, you explore social institutions, cultural dynamics, and the different processes of social interaction.

Learn to step outside of yourself and make connections between the lives and environments of people in various cultures. 

WHY SOCIOLOGY?

What are my job prospects?

Everything people do has social implications. Studying sociology offers valuable preparation for careers in diverse fields:

Business

Social services

Journalism

Politics

Public relations

Criminal justice

Health care

Human resources

Public administration (civil service, nonprofits)

Employers are looking for people who can think creatively about human problems, understand diversity, and navigate their group to success. As of 2021, the median pay for sociologists was nearly $93K and over $122K for political scientists. 

What industry-specific skills will I learn?

As a graduate with a sociology degree, you apply analytical and communication skills to real-world situations. In our increasingly diverse world, the study of sociology equips you with 21st century marketable skills, including:

Critical and analytical thinking

Working skillfully as part of a high-functioning team

Gathering and interpreting data

Problem-solving

Conveying complex information and ideas
