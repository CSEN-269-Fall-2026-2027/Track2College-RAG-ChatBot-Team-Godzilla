welcome to the engineering school of sustainable infrastructure & environment
The Engineering School of Sustainable Infrastructure & Environment (ESSIE) continues to improve and advance sustainability through a nexus of the civil, coastal, environmental and oceanographic programs. ESSIE is comprised of the Department of Civil and Coastal Engineering (CCE) and the Department of Environmental Engineering Sciences (EES).

ESSIE faculty members earn Wason Medal for Materials Research
Christopher Ferraro, Ph.D., P.E., associate director and associate professor in the Engineering School of Sustainable Infrastructure & Environment, and Kyle Riding, Ph.D., P.E., department head and professor of Civil and Coastal Engineering, are among the researchers recognized with the American Concrete Institute’s Wason Medal for Materials Research.

Bombarded by dangerous e-waste? Bring on the bots

Back to the bog: UF researchers lead wetlands assessments
