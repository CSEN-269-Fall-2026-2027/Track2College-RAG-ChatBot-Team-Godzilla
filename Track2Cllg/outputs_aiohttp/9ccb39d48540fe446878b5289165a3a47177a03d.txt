DEGREE OVERVIEW 

Management is creating goals and strategies for an organization and coordinating employees to achieve the objectives with available resources. It involves planning, organizing, directing, coordinating, and monitoring progress. The primary role of a manager is a decision maker. Managers must be alert to social, political, economic, and technology issues in the business environment.

ABOUT THE PROGRAM

The AACSB-accredited 120-credit-hour degree program includes 12 to 15 hours of additional management classes built around the core business curriculum. UTA’s BBA in management has four optional tracks: Human Resource Management, Entrepreneurship/Small Business, International Management, and General Management. Management undergraduate courses include:

 Human Resource Management

 Entrepreneurship and Venture Management

 International Management

 Diversity in Organizations

 HR Staffing and Performance

 Team Management

 Negotiations and Conflict Resolution

 Training and Development

CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

A management degree can lead to many types of jobs across the full range of industries and every sector of the economy, including financial, government, hospitality, nonprofit, tech, transportation, and energy fields. Many pursue entrepreneurial options of working for a start-up or starting their own business. Some of the typical career paths are:

 Banker

 Business Owner

 Health Care Management

 Human Resource Generalist or Specialist

 Human Resource Manager

 Management Consultant

 Nonprofit Manager

 Project Manager

DEGREE OPTIONS

BBA - Bachelor of Business Administration in Management

The BBA in Management has three optional tracks: Human Resource Management, Entrepreneurship/Small Business, and International Management. In addition to the BBA, the department also offers two undergraduate certificates: Certificate in Entrepreneurship and Certificate in Managing Diversity and Inclusion in Organizations.
