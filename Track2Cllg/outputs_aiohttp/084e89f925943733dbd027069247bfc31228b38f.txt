Management Information Systems B.S.

Bachelor of Science in Management Information Systems

Develop Key Creative, Analytical and Communications Skills

Interested in a career where you can use your aptitude with computer systems to tackle
                              complex business challenges? Pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Management Information
                              Systems from University of Houston-Clear Lake will equip you with the skills to combine
                              business theory and technology in the modern world. You’ll be able to fill the growing
                              need for professionals with oversight and technical expertise in advanced information
                              systems technology. Job opportunities for graduates include:

Database architect

Computer programmer

Computer or I/S manager

Systems or data analyst

Web or business applications developer

Computer network designer or administrator

Database developer and administrator

Learn from Leading Minds and Build Career Relationships

As a student in the Management Information Systems (MIS) B.S. program, you'll work
                              with professionals committed to creating the most relevant and effective learning
                              experiences for you. UHCL's MIS faculty bring real-world business expertise to the
                              classroom. You'll also have a variety of networking opportunities within the program.
                              Internships and professional organizations, such as the Management Information Systems
                              Organization student chapter, offer you the chance to build professional and personal
                              connections before you graduate.

Why Choose the MIS B.S. at UHCL?

STEM Designation:
 UHCL's MIS degree is STEM-designated and resides within the College of Business,
                              which is accredited by AACSB International.  

Extended OPT for International Students:
 International students graduating from this program are eligible for 36 –months of
                              OPT work authorization in the IT field in the US.   

State-of-the-Art MIS Labs:
 UHCL's MIS degree program is equipped with state-of-the-art labs featuring the latest
                              hardware and software. This immersive experience ensures you gain hands-on proficiency
                              with the tools and technologies that drive the dynamic IT field.

Expert Faculty:
 UHCL faculty consists of scholar-leaders and industry professionals, who hold advanced
                              degrees and professional certifications sought by industry professionals. This ensures
                              that you are being taught relevant and emerging technologies. As part of your coursework,
                              you'll also have the opportunity to complete some professional IT industry-recognized
                              certifications. 

Accreditations and Distinctions

 The College of Business at the University of Houston-Clear Lake holds both business
                           and separate accounting accreditation with The Association to Advance Collegiate Schools
                           of Business (AACSB) International. Recognition by AACSB symbolizes the commitment
                           to continuous improvement of educational quality, regional engagement with business
                           partners, and the effectiveness of our societal impact. AACSB holds its member institutions
                           to standards of excellence as interpreted by peer review teams, accreditation committees,
                           and Board of Directors. Fewer than 1 percent of the business schools in the world,
                           and only one other public university in the Houston region, have earned both business
                           and separate accounting accreditation.
