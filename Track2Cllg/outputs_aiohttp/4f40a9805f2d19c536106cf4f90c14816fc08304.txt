                     About the Sociology Degree at Troy University

                     Are you fascinated by society and human relationships? Do you want to use your education
                     to make a difference in our world? If so, gain a deep understanding of human relationships
                     and interactions, societal changes, and social causes and their consequences with
                     the sociology Bachelor of Science at Troy University. As a sociology major at TROY,
                     you'll develop a broad knowledge base and skills that you can apply to a variety of
                     careers positively impacting society.

                  “My time studying sociology and social science at TROY was a valuable and enlightening
                  experience. This program both provided a foundation for me to pursue further education
                  and taught me about diverse perspectives and paradigms for thinking about the larger
                  social world around me. To this day, I hold this experience close in my teaching and
                  research.”

Ryan Howard
 | PhD Student and Teaching Assistant, University of Florida | 2023 graduate, Sociology

                     Build Analysis and Research Skills for Sociology Careers

The 
best universities for sociology
 are those that help you develop the skills you need for career success. In TROY's
                        program, you'll gain a comprehensive understanding of various research methodologies
                        including creating effective tools for data collection, conducting qualitative and
                        quantitative analyses and honing your overall research abilities. Through robust 
sociology courses
, expert faculty and hands-on learning opportunities, you'll be well prepared to tackle
                        social issues such as food insecurity and wage inequality.

                     Apply Your Sociology Courses to Real-World Situations

Sociology degrees
 are not all created equal. At TROY, our 
sociology Bachelor of Science
 degree offers an exceptional internship program that provides you with hands-on experiences
                        in diverse settings, from prisons to mental health agencies. As a 
sociology major
 at TROY, you can also engage in research and public good projects to gain experience
                        putting what you learn into practice.

                  			Faculty Profiles

Sharon Everhardt, Ph.D.

Dr. Everhardt's main research interests include the study of race, class, and gender,
                                 especially disenfranchised populations of women. In 2020, Dr. Everhardt earned certification
                                 as an AACS Clinical Sociologist who helps organizations and institutions resolve social
                                 issues related to the human condition.  She is currently researching the impact of
                                 horticultural programs on nutritional knowledge and food insecurity among female inmates
                                 in women's correctional facilities. She has secured over $75,000 in USDA funding to
                                 support her gardens projects.  In 2020, she spearheaded the pursuit of a 1.9 mil Department
                                 of Education's Strengthening Institutions Title III grant to improve student achievement
                                 and retention at Troy University.

Andrew Tatch, Ph.D.

Dr. Tatch is interested in research related to social stratification, substance use
                                 and abuse, impaired driving, and health disparities. He has led or co-authored manuscripts
                                 related to college student drinking, gender-specific predictors of impaired driving,
                                 mental illness, adverse childhood experiences, and employment outcomes for individuals
                                 with blindness or low vision. Dr. Tatch is currently working on several manuscripts
                                 considering the utility and applicability of data from digital online spaces to extend
                                 current research on identity work and impression management.

FAQ About the Sociology Major

Learn more about the field of sociology and our program.

What is a sociology major?

A 
sociology majo
r involves studying human relationships, societal changes, and social causes and consequences.
                        At TROY, you'll explore topics like crime and deviance, social inequality, and social
                        institutions such as the criminal justice and healthcare systems. Our program equips
                        you with critical analysis, research skills and practical experience through internships
                        so you can earn a 
sociology salary
.

Is sociology a good major?

Yes, sociology is a good major if you want to understand and address social issues.
                        It provides a comprehensive understanding of human behavior and societal structures,
                        which are valuable in various professional fields. As a 
sociology major
 at TROY, you'll develop critical thinking, communication, and other skills that will
                        make you a well-rounded and adaptable professional.

What jobs can you get with a sociology degree?

With a 
sociology degree
 from TROY, you can pursue careers in:

Corrections

Court administration

Family intervention

Case management

Victim advocacy

Social research

Community development

Public policy

Human resources

Are there job opportunities for sociology majors?

Yes, by earning a
 sociology Bachelor of Science degree
 at TROY you'll have numerous job opportunities. Through your 
sociology courses
 and hands-on learning experiences, you'll gain skills in research, data analysis,
                        and understanding social dynamics that are in demand in government agencies, nonprofits,
                        educational institutions and private companies.
