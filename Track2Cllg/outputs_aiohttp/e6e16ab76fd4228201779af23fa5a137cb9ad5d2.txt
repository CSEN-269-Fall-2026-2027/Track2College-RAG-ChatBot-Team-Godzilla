The richness of the experience is predicated upon a body of motivated, intellectually curious students ready to find new forms and expressions, coupled with an outstanding faculty of professional artists and technicians who share their artistry and knowledge with passion and generosity. The stimulating curriculum allows every student to expand their cultural experience and, in the process, develop into a better artist, with a well-informed, highly articulated personal vision.
Students work in all modes of moving image and sound based artwork, including story- and character-based work in live-action and animated cinema; essayistic, sociopolitical and observational documentary projects in live-action, animation, installation, and hybrid forms; and poetic, lyrical, structural, and other modes of experimentation with the materials and forms of the moving image.
