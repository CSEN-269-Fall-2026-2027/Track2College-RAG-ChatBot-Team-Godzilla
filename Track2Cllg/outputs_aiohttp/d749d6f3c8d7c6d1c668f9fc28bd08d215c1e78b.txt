2025 Cohort

Tuition

Master of Athletic Training Tuition:
 $500 per credit hour
Year One: 31 credit hours
Year Two: 25 credit hours

Master of Athletic Training Fees

Fees, Year One (payable in the summer term):

MAT Year 1 Fee: $600 ($300 of this fee is covered by deposit that is paid when accepted into program. The remaining $300 is due the week of orientation). This fee covers the following:
Background Check—$20
Drug Screen—$30
CPR Certification—$40
Name Badges—$3
Apparel—$150
NATA Membership Dues—$80
Field/Lab Kit—$150
Typhon Program Software Fee—$125 
MAT Book Fees: Approximately $800 first year
Athletic Training Conference Registration and Travel (ALATA, SEATA, or NATA)—Travel to conference is optional, but strongly encouraged; registration fees range from $40 to $100 and conference location sites vary.
Some clinical experiences will be off-campus and will require student-provided transportation and parking fees.

Fees, Year Two (payable in the fall term):

MAT Fee, Year Two: $600, which covers the following: 
NATA Dues—$80
Field Kit Supplies—$150
Apparel—$70
ACES Boards Prep Workshop
—$300
Book Fee, Year Two: Approximately $400
Athletic Training conference travel (ALATA, SEATA, NATA)—Travel to conference is optional, but strongly encouraged; registration fees range from $40-100 and conference location sites vary. 
Travel and parking at off-campus clinical internship sites 
BOC Exam—$475

Estimated Master of Athletic Training (M.A.T.) Cost of Attendance
