AFFORDABLE EDUCATION

The decision to attend college is the beginning of a path to future success. North American University strives to provide affordable access to higher education by keeping tuition and fees as low as possible for students.

Our tuition is a flat rate, allowing students to take as many or as little credits as they need each semester. In addition to our low tuition, we offer many scholarship opportunities for students to offset tuition costs. North American University believes in providing affordable access to higher education and within that belief, we aim to keep our tuition and fees low and transparent.
