Mission Statement

The CSUB Department of Nursing provides high-quality nursing education to undergraduate
                           and graduate students pursuing careers in professional nursing and advance practice
                           nursing roles. The faculty provides a collaborative, inclusive, and interactive learning
                           environment committed to excellence in teaching, scholarship, service and practice.
                           Faculty are dedicated to empowering students with essential life-long learning skills,
                           emphasizing evidence-based practices, quality and safe patient care across the lifespan
                           for a diverse and global society. The Department of Nursing accomplishes the mission
                           through collaboration with the communities of interest.
