Discover Your Future at The University of Texas at Tyler

Thrive in a supportive, driven community of innovators and strivers, where instructors
                        are invested in your academic, personal and professional success, no matter where
                        you are along your journey. Think ahead and embrace your strengths through undergraduate,
                        graduate and health professional programs emphasizing hands-on learning, research
                        and mentorship.

We continue to think about how we serve others — from engaging degree programs to
                        innovation, research and our partnership with UT Health East Texas. Join us and discover
                        your future as we look toward what’s next.

A big reason why I chose UT Tyler was because of the business school. I'm very focused

                              comfortable knowing that I would graduate with the knowledge to get a good job in
                              the future.
