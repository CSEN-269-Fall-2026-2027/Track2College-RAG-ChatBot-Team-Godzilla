At Southwestern Adventist University, our Theology program is more than academic studyâit is a calling to lead with knowledge, faith, and compassion. Designed to prepare future pastors and church leaders, this program equips students with a strong biblical foundation and hands-on ministry experience through intensive coursework, field evangelism, and pastoral mentorship.

Students engage deeply in biblical understanding and personal application while gaining essential skills in church leadership, administration, biblical preaching, and pastoral service. They also study biblical languages to enhance scriptural interpretation. With key experiential opportunities like the Pastoral Internship Program and Field School of Evangelism, students graduate prepared to serve faithfully and effectively in diverse ministry settings.

Curriculum and Courses

The Theology curriculum fosters both intellectual and spiritual growth. Students explore key areas such as biblical exegesis, systematic theology, church history, practical ministry, and pastoral counseling. Courses also focus on communication and preaching, helping students learn to craft and deliver sermons rooted in sound theology.

Field-based Learning Plays an Essential Role

In the Pastoral Internship Program, students are paired with experienced pastors who provide direct mentorship and support. 
The Field School of Evangelism 
offers real-world training in outreach and evangelistic strategies, further reinforcing the connection between classroom learning and spiritual leadership in action.
