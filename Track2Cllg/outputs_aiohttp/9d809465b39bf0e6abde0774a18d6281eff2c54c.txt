Discover Unique Paths Supported by Community
On the hilltop of St. Edward’s, you’ll be part of a close-knit community of smart, globally-minded, critical thinkers and problem-solvers passionate about making the world a better place. Together, you’ll learn in small classes taught by professors (not TAs) who get to know you and mentor you.
Plus, there’s nowhere else quite like our hometown — Austin, Texas — a magnet for innovation and opportunities and ranked the #1 Best College City in the U.S.
