Choosing an Application Plan

Early Decision I and Early Decision II are for students who know LMU is their first-choice university and are ready to commit to enrolling if admitted. Early Action and Regular Decision have a longer timeline to make your commitment.

What You Need to Apply

Application Requirements

1. Submit your application.

Choose to submit your application either through the Common Application, the LMU Application, or the Coalition Application.

2. Pay the application fee.

Pay the one-time $75 application fee.

3. Unofficial and official transcripts.

Report your high school grades through the application you choose, or upload a copy of your unofficial transcript. When you enroll at LMU, you will need to submit your official final high school transcript before classes begin.

4. Test scores optional.

SAT and ACT test scores are not required. However, submitted scores will be considered as part of our holistic review of your application and may strengthen it. If you choose not to submit your test scores, this will not negatively impact our review.

5. Submit your letter of recommendation.

Submit at least one letter of recommendation from a school official. A letter from a teacher and a letter from your counselor are recommended.

6. Submit any required audition or portfolio.

Some LMU programs require you to submit an audition or portfolio to be considered.

Asking for a Letter of Recommendation

Ask early. 
Give your recommender plenty of time before the application deadline.

Give them context.
 Share helpful context, such as your activities, accomplishments, goals, and application deadlines.

Make the ask personal.
 Explain why you are asking them specifically.

Not sure what to say? Try: 
"Would you feel comfortable writing a strong recommendation for my LMU application?"

What Happens Next

How We Review Your Application

Your academic record is the primary factor in LMU’s admission review. We also consider other parts of your application that help us understand your preparation, experiences, and potential.

We consider:

Grades and academic rigor

Activities and accomplishments

Leadership and service

Recommendations

ACT or SAT scores, if submitted

There is no minimum GPA or test score required for admission. LMU reviews each application individually for evidence of academic achievement, preparation, and potential.

After You Apply

1. Check your email.

LMU will send instructions on how to access your Application Status Portal.

2. Review your status.

Confirm that we have received all your required documents and check whether anything is still needed in your Application Status Portal.

3. File your FAFSA.

Submit your Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) early to be considered for all financial assistance that may be available.

4. Stay connected.

Engage with us by 
visiting campus
 or attending our virtual events, and join our 
Zeemee community
.

5. Watch for your decision.

Your admission decision will be posted to your Application Status Portal.

Application FAQs

Find answers to common questions about applying to LMU, submitting materials, admission decisions, and next steps.

        	Does LMU offer an application fee waiver?

Yes
. To request a fee waiver, please email us at 
admission@lmu.edu
.

        	Is LMU test optional?

Yes. However, while LMU is test-optional, and ACT or SAT scores are not required for admission, submitted scores will still be considered as part of our holistic review of your application. 

For some, test scores may help their application. However, students who choose not to submit test scores will not negatively impact our review or your scholarship eligibility.

        	How do I submit an audition or portfolio

You will be able to submit your audition or portfolio after you submit your application. Once you submit your application, you will receive an email with further instructions on how to access your Application Status Portal, which, after you log in, will allow you to upload and submit your materials.

        	How do I update or correct information after I submit my application?

In order to update or change information on your application after you have submitted it, you should email 
admission@lmu.edu
 with any information that needs to be changed. Our undergraduate admission team can make these updates for you.

        	How do I know if my application is complete?

You can check your Application Status Portal to see whether your application is complete, review the materials we have received, and determine whether anything is still needed to complete your application.

        	Where can I see my admission decision?

Your admission decision will be posted in your Application Status Portal. Watch your email for instructions on how to access it after you submit your application.

        	Am I automatically considered for academic scholarships?

Yes. All first-year applicants are automatically considered for LMU academic scholarships. No additional scholarship application is required.

        	If I am deferred from Early Action, can I switch to Early Decision II?

Yes, you can switch your application from Early Action to Early Decision II. 

Switching to Early Decision II shows us that LMU is your first choice, and communicates your commitment to enrolling if you are admitted. Choosing this option reflects a meaningful level of commitment, and we consider the enthusiasm and intentionality behind that choice in our review process. 

To change your application from Early Action to Early Decision II, email 
admission@lmu.edu
.
