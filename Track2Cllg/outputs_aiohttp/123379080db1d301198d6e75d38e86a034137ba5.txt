Malibu and Los Angeles

Living in Malibu means waking up to ocean views, hiking trails in the hills behind campus, and beaches a few minutes from your dorm. And with Los Angeles just 30 miles away, you can spend a semester interning in entertainment, tech, or business, then catch a Lakers game or find the city's best tacos on the way home. Most schools make you choose between a beautiful campus and a big city. Here you get both.

Global Experience

More of our students study abroad than at almost any other university in the country. With residential campuses in Florence, London, Buenos Aires, Heidelberg, and more, going abroad is part of the main adventure here, not just a side quest. Spend a semester, a summer, or a whole year taking classes with Pepperdine professors and traveling on the weekends.
