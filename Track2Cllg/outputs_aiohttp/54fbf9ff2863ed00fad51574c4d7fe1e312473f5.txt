Psychological Science

The BCLA Department of Psychological Science offers a supportive and engaging curriculum where students dive into human and animal behavior, exploring how biology, psychology, and culture shape us. Along the way, students sharpen their critical thinking skills and apply what they learn to real-life situations, focusing on diverse perspectives. Students also get to work closely with faculty on research, preparing for graduate studies or careers in psychology and beyond.
