The Psychology program at Southwestern Adventist University empowers students to understand the complexities of human behavior while preparing them for a range of professional paths.

With a strong emphasis on theoretical foundations, career mentorship, and experiential learning, this program offers a holistic and transformative academic journey. Students develop practical skills through individualized guidance and internship opportunities, allowing them to apply classroom concepts to real-world scenarios.

Curriculum and Courses

Our Psychology curriculum includes courses in developmental psychology, abnormal psychology, social psychology, cognitive psychology, and research methods. The program emphasizes hands-on learning through internships and applied projects, helping students gain the skills necessary to succeed in both professional and graduate-level settings.

Admission Requirements
