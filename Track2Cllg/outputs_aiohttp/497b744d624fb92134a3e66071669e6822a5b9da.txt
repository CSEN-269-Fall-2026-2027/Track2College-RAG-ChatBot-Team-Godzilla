A degree in Social Science at Southwestern Adventist University equips you with strong analytical, research, and critical thinking skills.

By examining governments, cultural practices, and social structures, you will gain a broad knowledge base spanning multiple fields. This interdisciplinary approach fosters a deep understanding of human behavior and prepares you for a wide range of professional or graduate-level opportunities.

Curriculum and Courses

Courses in the Social Science program cover topics such as cultural anthropology, comparative government, historical analysis, principles of sociology, and foundational psychology.
