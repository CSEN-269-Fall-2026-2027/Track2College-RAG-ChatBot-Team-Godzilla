Are you intrigued by the intricate dance of currencies and markets across the globe? Ready to unravel the mysteries of international economics and finance? At Austin College, our International Economics and Finance major invites you on a journey into the dynamic world of global finance and trade. Dive deep into the complexities of international markets, trade policies, and financial systems, and gain the skills needed to navigate the interconnected world of global finance.

Finish in Four*

with the skills to launch your career 

Working in a field that spans

international borders will require you to hone your communication, critical thinking, quantitative, and collaboration skills. As a graduate with a degree in International Economics & Finance, you will be poised to take on your first job with the knowledge that you can confidently:

describe and illustrate economic phenomenon effectively in written and oral presentations;

have the breadth and depth in understanding the foundations of international financial markets; and

utilize quantitative methods to address international economic and finance problems.
