Healthy movement is the goal.

The Kinesiology program teaches the science behind the responses and adaptations to exercise. It includes components of nutrition, health and fitness, and cardiovascular wellness. You will learn how the body adapts to exercise, how to tailor programs to individual patients needs, and how to use learned skills as medicine for your patients and customers.

Why study Kinesiology?

Kinesiology is focused on how the human body moves, develops, and heals. You'll learn the skills necessary to work in clinical rehabilitation, sports science, and nutrition. Popular career fields include sports medicine, physical and occupational therapy, and other health professionals. 

What are my job prospects?

Kinesiologists are able to work in many areas including:

Wellness Programs

Clinical Rehabilitation

Conditioning Coaching

Cardiac Rehabilitation

Exercise Testing and Specialization

Performance Enhancement Testing

Personal Training Sports Coaching

What industry-specific skills will I learn?

Gain understanding of sports performance and the factors that affect behavior during movement. A degree from UT Permian Basin is a perfect academic combination of anatomy, physiology, and modern technology.

Acquire knowledge for coaching, problem solving, communication and client relations alongside opportunity for hands-on experience in state-of-the-art facilities.
