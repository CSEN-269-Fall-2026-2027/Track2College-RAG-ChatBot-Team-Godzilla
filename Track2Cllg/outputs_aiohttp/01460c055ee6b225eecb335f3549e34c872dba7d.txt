                              Contact the Office of Admission

The Offices of Admission and Financial Aid are here to answer questions you might have regarding your enrollment at Whittier College.

There is a full staff of 
admission counselors
, 
financial aid counselors
, and student ambassadors available to work with you and your family. Each admission counselor is assigned to work with students from specific territories. We travel to many states, a few countries, and nearly 600 high schools, spreading the Whittier word. Your admission counselors will be the "first reader" of your file and will assist you throughout the application process. So we encourage you to contact your counselor and become his or her positive partner throughout the Whittier admission process.

And if you have questions or concerns about how to afford a Whittier education, our financial aid staff is similarly available to help you navigate the possibilities for financial assistance.
