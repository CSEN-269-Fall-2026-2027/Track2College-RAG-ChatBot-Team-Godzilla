Degree Maps

Investigate the Intersection of the Environment and People

With a B.S. in Environmental Science from University of Houston-Clear Lake, you’ll
                                 study the effects of natural and unnatural processes and its interactions of the physical
                                 components of the planet on the environment. You will enjoy small classes and award-winning
                                 faculty members with a pulse on the industry. 
You can also specialize in one of the following areas of study:

Environmental Biology

Environmental Chemistry

Environmental Geology

Environmental Technology

Physical Geology

Expand Your Career Opportunities

Using an interdisciplinary approach that crosses science and cultural studies, this
                                 degree will prepare you for any number of jobs where environmental issues are addressed,
                                 whether in the private sector or governmental agencies. And depending on your specialization,
                                 you can become an environmental scientist, biologist, chemist, geologist, or industrial
                                 hygiene manager.
