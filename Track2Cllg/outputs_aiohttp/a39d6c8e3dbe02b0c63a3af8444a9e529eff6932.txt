Management is the art of leading people and projects, and our program helps you master both.

At Southwestern Adventist University, students in the Management program develop a strong foundation in leadership, organizational strategy, and practical decision-making to prepare for success across industries.

Curriculum and Courses

The Management curriculum combines theoretical insight with real-world application. You will study leadership, organizational behavior, ethics, strategic planning, and team dynamics while gaining hands-on experience through projects and internships that simulate real business challenges.

Specialize Your Management Degree

Enhance your management education with one of our career-focused emphasis programs:

Project Management
: Learn how to lead projects from start to finish with skills in planning, execution, and oversight.

Operations and Supply Chain Management
: Focus on logistics, efficiency, and systems that drive productivity.

Human Resource Management
: Develop expertise in managing people, building company culture, and optimizing talent.

These emphasis options let you tailor your degree to align with your career goals and emerging business fields.
