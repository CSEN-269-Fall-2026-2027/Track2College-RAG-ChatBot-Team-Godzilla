Chemistry and Biochemistry

Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry
The FIU Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry is at the heart of groundbreaking research and scientific innovation. As part of a Carnegie R1 research university, we are leading in forensic science, nuclear chemistry, environmental and biomedical solutions, pharmaceutical innovations, chemistry education and more. Whether you want to work in a corporate lab, health care, a government agency, law enforcement or education, we offer rigorous academics and hands-on experiences to prepare you for a successful career.

Life as an FIU Chemistry and Biochemistry Major
As a student, you will be part of the traditions and experiences that unite our department and build connections including:
BSI Research Symposium
 —
 showcasing the latest research in biomolecular sciences by FIU students, faculty and invited scientists.
Seasonal Department Socials 
— building community through festive gathering like the Holiday Party and Halloween Party, featuring food, games and spirited fun.

— celebrating our graduates' achievements and marking their transition in the next chapter with a dinner.
