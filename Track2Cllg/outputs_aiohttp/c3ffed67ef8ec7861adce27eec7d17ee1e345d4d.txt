Department of Communication Majors

Students pursuing a Bachelor of Arts degree in the Department of Communication may
                        select from one of four majors. Additionally, students are required to have a minor
                        outside of their major.

The department offers four undergraduate majors:

Bachelor of Arts in Advertising and Public Relations

Bachelor of Arts in Communication Studies

Bachelor of Arts in Digital Film and Television Production

Bachelor of Arts in Journalism (Broadcast and Multimedia)

To qualify for an undergraduate degree a student must complete a minimum of 120 approved
                        semester hours, meet the University's general education requirements, meet the requirements
                        of the major, and meet any specific requirements of the college or school.

Advertising and Public Relations

The advertising and public relations major is an integrated approach allowing students
                                 to choose a focus in either and courses are taken in both for a strong foundation
                                 in the field. Graduates to pursue careers as account executives, media planners, marketing
                                 and communication specialists, social media managers, public relations directors,
                                 brand managers and digital strategists.

Communication Studies

Communication Studies allows graduates to pursue careers as human resources managers,
                                 recruiters, sales directors, healthcare counselors and non-profit directors. Students
                                 can choose to study human and organizational communication or rhetoric and culture.

Digital Film and Television Production

Digital film and television production allows graduates to pursue careers as editors,
                                 videographers, producers, scriptwriters and technical directors. Students produce
                                 their own short films and television programs using high-definition equipment, and
                                 in the process, learn a broad understanding of the moving image.

Journalism (Broadcast and Multimedia)

A degree in journalism prepares students to tell stories making a difference. From
                                 online to on TV, a journalism degree allows students to accelerate their careers as
                                 news editors, radio broadcasters, TV reporters, multimedia journalists and editors
                                 in the exciting world of both digital and broadcasting journalism.
