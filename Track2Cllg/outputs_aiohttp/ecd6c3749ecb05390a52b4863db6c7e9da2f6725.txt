Tuition Protection Insurance

The University of South Alabama has partnered with 
GradGuard
 to offer a tuition insurance plan as added protection for students and families.
                        This plan can reimburse your non-refundable tuition and housing costs after an unexpected
                        withdrawal for a covered illness, injury, mental health condition and more. Please
                        visit 
GradGuard
 to learn more.
