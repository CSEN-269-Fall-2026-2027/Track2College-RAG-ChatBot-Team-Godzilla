Complete the  2027-2028 FAFSA

Students must apply or reapply for financial aid every year.

Completing the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) allows you to be considered for federal, state and university financial aid programs. The 2027-2028 FAFSA is scheduled to become available by October 1, 2026. Filling out the FAFSA is free. Find out more at our 
FAFSA information page

Centsible Knights – Top 50 Financial Literacy Program in 2020

The University of Central Florida’s 
Centsible Knights
 program has daily office hours for students to attend in case of any need for financial literacy and education counseling. The university has plenty of online articles and events helping students understand their financial situation, and also offers GradReady, “a free interactive website with online videos and resources to better assist students in making sound financial decisions.”
