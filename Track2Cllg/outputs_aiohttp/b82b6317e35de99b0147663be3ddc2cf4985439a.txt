                     Elevate Your Talent as a Dance Major at TROY

                     Do you have the passion and dedication to push yourself as a dancer? Are you ready
                     to lead in the arts and give back to your community? At Troy University, the Bachelor
                     of Fine Arts in Dance challenges you to uncover your full potential through rigorous
                     training and performance in all dance genres and styles. If you're ready to commit
                     to the journey, we invite you to audition for this selective dance major. With opportunities
                     in choreography, pedagogy, student-driven creative projects, study abroad and collaborations
                     with guest artists, the dance degree at TROY will shape you into the performer, choreographer,
                     teacher, administrator and artist you aspire to be.

                  “When I came for my first day of dance class, I felt an immediate sense of family,
                  that they genuinely cared about what you were doing, how you were doing in your classes
                  and that you were going to excel. They were looking at how far you could go and how
                  they could help you grow.”

Erin Smith
 | Dancer, Choreographer & Instructor, Nashville, TN | 2020 graduate, B.F.A. in Dance

A B.F.A. in Dance Designed for Your Success

                     				As you explore colleges with dance majors, make sure the dance program you choose
                     will strengthen your technique and career potential. At TROY, you'll build a strong
                     foundation in ballet and contemporary styles while developing skills in choreography,
                     pedagogy and the commercial dance industry. Courses in dance composition, improvisation
                     and anatomy refine your artistry, while history and pedagogy expand your perspective.
                     This well-rounded curriculum prepares you to perform, create and lead in your future
                     career.

Dance Careers with a B.F.A. from TROY 

                     				What can you do with a dance degree? At TROY, you'll train for careers in performance,
                     choreography, arts administration and so much more. Our B.F.A. in dance builds your
                     skills in ballet, contemporary dance, pedagogy and the commercial dance industry.
                     With a strong foundation in technique and teaching, you'll also be prepared for dance
                     teacher jobs in studios, schools and community programs. Whether you want to perform,
                     create or lead, TROY gives you the experience and connections to excel in your future
                     career. 

                  			Faculty Profiles

Dominique Angel

Dominique Angel, a lecturer of dance at TROY, began her ballet training with Ballet
                                 Russe premier danseur Oleg Tupine and his wife, Tania Rousseau. She continued her
                                 studies at the Joffrey Ballet School before joining the Joffrey II Dancers under the
                                 direction of Sally Brayley Bliss and later, the Joffrey Ballet under Robert Joffrey.
                                 Throughout her career, she has performed works by renowned choreographers, including
                                 Paul Taylor, Frederick Ashton and Mark Morris, among others.

FAQ About the Dance Program

Learn more about the field of dance and our program.

What are related jobs to a dancer?

                     	A dance degree from TROY prepares you for a variety of careers beyond performing.
                     With your training, you can pursue dance instructor jobs, choreographer careers and
                     unique positions such as arts administrator, dance critic, studio owner or movement
                     therapist. You'll also be equipped to work in the commercial dance industry in roles
                     such as dance director, film or television performer, dance agent — or in dance-related
                     marketing and promotion jobs.

What can you do with a dance degree from TROY?

                     	With your B.F.A. in dance from TROY, you'll be equipped to perform, choreograph,
                     teach and lead in dance studios, schools, fitness programs or arts organizations.
                     Whether you want to focus on education, performance or entrepreneurship, your TROY
                     dance degree provides the foundation to build your artistic future.

Can I pursue dance teacher jobs with a B.F.A. in dance?

                     	Absolutely! TROY will prepare you to pursue teacher positions in K-12 schools, private
                     studios, community programs and colleges. You can teach ballet, contemporary dance
                     or other dance styles while also offering guidance in choreography, performance and
                     technique. 

Is dance a major in college and why should I choose it?

                     	Yes, you can major in dance in college. With intensive training, hands-on experience
                     and the opportunity to explore a wide range of dance careers, TROY's program stands
                     out among colleges with dance majors. If you have a passion for dance and want to
                     develop your artistry while preparing for a professional career, choosing TROY is
                     a wise decision.

How does TROY's program compare to other colleges with dance majors? 

                     	TROY provides a unique combination of traditional dance techniques, choreography
                     and emerging opportunities in commercial dance. Unlike many colleges with dance majors,
                     TROY offers access to study abroad programs, guest artists and individualized mentorship,
                     ensuring you graduate ready for a wide array of dance careers.
