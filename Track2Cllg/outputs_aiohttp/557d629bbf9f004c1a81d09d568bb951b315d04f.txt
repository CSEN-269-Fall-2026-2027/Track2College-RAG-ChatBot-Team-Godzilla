Professional Studies Certificate in Voice

SFCM's Voice Department builds on a deep commitment to operatic voice training, one that has produced industry stars like Elza van den Heever, Amina Edris, and Efraín Solís. From there, SFCM makes sure our Voice students are able to use that foundation to explore wherever their voice wants to go, whether that's musical theatre, pop and contemporary vocals, or improvisation.
SFCM offers a wide range of vocal performance experiences, from fully staged operas with the SFCM Orchestra to Historical Performance and Baroque productions with chamber ensembles, as well as workshops, scene showcases, and a dedicated undergraduate opera each winter term.
SFCM's largest venue, the Caroline H. Hume Concert Hall, boasts cutting-edge projection technology that allows for an immersive experience for both performers and audiences.
Many of our faculty members maintain active performance careers and have ties to not only the San Francisco Opera, but companies across the Bay Area and beyond. And SFCM singers—like all Conservatory students—benefit from SFCM's Global Creative Network with arts management companies Askonas Holt and Opus 3 Artists, with industry luminaries from their rosters like Joyce DiDonato, Meow Meow, Patricia Racette, and Craig Terry visiting the Conservatory for performances and masterclasses.

"At SFCM we ground things in classical vocal technique. With the non-technical side, the artistry, we continue to develop that from whichever genre it has grown."—Richard Cox, Executive Chair and Professor of Voice.

Joyce DiDonato (represented by SFCM Global Creative Alliance member Askonas Holt) sings at SFCM's commencement following a week of activities at the Conservatory, including a masterclass for students.

Opera and Musical Theatre Department Chair and SFCM time alumna Heather Mathews guides students to finding the true essence of their characters while delving into music and text.

Each year, SFCM produces seven fully-staged opera and musical theatre productions: Three Opera productions (two mainstage, one chamber), two Musical Theatre productions, an undergraduate opera, and a semi-staged Historical Performance opera.

SFCM voice students perform in rehearsals and opera performances throughout the year, including Voice Department recitals, masterclasses, and residencies.
