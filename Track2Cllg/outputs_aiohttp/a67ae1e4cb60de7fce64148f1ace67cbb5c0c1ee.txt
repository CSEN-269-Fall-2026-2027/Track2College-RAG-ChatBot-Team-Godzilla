                              Department of Theatre, Film, and Communication Arts

Whittier College's Theatre, Film, and Communications Arts programs empower students to explore their creative expression.

The 
Theatre and Communication Arts
 major guides students through the rich history of theatre while preparing them to take the stage as performers or bring worlds to life with hands-on experience in stage design and technology.

The 
Film
 major immerses students in both film scholarship and content creation as they craft short films to screen at the annual film festival and enjoy networking opportunities with industry professionals.
