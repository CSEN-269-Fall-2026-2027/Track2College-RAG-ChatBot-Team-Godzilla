About

The Bachelor of Science in Pharmaceutical Sciences is housed in the UC Irvine Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences, a pioneering program designed to train undergraduates in the art of discovering new medicines and deploying them into the clinic. After completing comprehensive background training in basic biology, chemistry, and mathematics, majors continue on to advanced training in organic chemistry, molecular and cellular biology, human genetics and physiology, and biochemistry. Starting in their third year, majors take very deep dives into the topics that define contemporary drug discovery and clinical implementation: medicinal chemistry, molecular pharmacology, and biologics. Supplementing these core areas are a variety of electives and experiential learning opportunities to acquire advanced knowledge in high-throughput experimentation, structure-activity relationship studies, cellular analytics, and cutting-edge OMIC technologies. Most graduates of this program pursue advanced degrees, such as the PhD, MD, or PharmD, and pursue careers in biomedical research and professional healthcare, while others seek employment in the public and private sectors after graduation.

Graduates emerge from the program with knowledge of:

the development, characterization, and delivery of drugs

physical, organic, and medicinal chemistry

biology

engineering

pharmacology and toxicology

healthcare

drug interactions in the human body

cost effectiveness of medicines

ethics and access

compliance

clinical care/patient care

Future opportunities for BS in Pharmaceutical Sciences graduates:

Advanced scientific degrees (PhD, PharmD, MSP, MD)

Careers in the biotechnology and pharmaceutical industries

Healthcare (pharmacist, physician, dentist, physician’s associate, etc.)

Pharmaceutical administration and sales

Government regulatory positions

Degree Pathways

Our undergraduate program offers two degree pathways. Both pathways earn their BS in Pharmaceutical Sciences. The first two years of a students’ undergraduate program are identical for both pathways. The curriculum changes beginning in the junior year. Students will work with their academic advisor to select the path that is best for them prior to their junior year. 

Pharmaceutical Sciences

Focuses on drug discovery & drug development

Answers the question: “How do we create and develop drugs?”

Best for students interested in pursuing research, academia, PhD, working in Biotech or Pharma industries

Pharmaceutical Sciences with Medicinal Pharmacology Specialization

Focuses on how drugs work in a medical context

Answers the question: “How are drugs applied to healthcare and patients?”

Best for students interested in pursuing healthcare (Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, Optometry, etc.)

Research and Internship Opportunities

UC Irvine is an R1 Research University! 
Undergraduate students in the UC Irvine School of Pharmacy & Pharmaceutical Sciences have extensive research opportunities, including working in faculty members’ labs, writing journal articles and getting published, presenting at conferences, and participating in the Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP)!

Research interests that our students are able to explore include synthetic biology, RNA and disease, medicinal chemistry, molecular evolution, therapeutics for childhood cancer, bioengineered tools for drug delivery, and anti-aging and health.

First-Generation Students

Thirty-seven percent (37%) of all enrolled undergraduates in the UC Irvine School of Pharmacy & Pharmaceutical Sciences are first-generation students. The BS in Pharmaceutical Sciences program offers a catalyst program for first-generation, low-income freshman students.
