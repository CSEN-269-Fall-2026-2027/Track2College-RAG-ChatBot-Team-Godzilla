Featured Classes
MKTG 300
Fundamentals of Marketing
Learn necessary marketing terms, competitive strategies and innovative marketing concepts to understand the industry.
MKTG 420
Consumer Behavior
When you get down to it, marketing is influenced by behaviors. Develop a solid marketing strategy by learning how consumers process information and make decisions.

Marketing Strategy
In this capstone course, you’ll apply your marketing knowledge in a semester-long simulation. Compete with classmates to get the most market share.

Digital Marketing
Digital marketing utilizes a variety of platforms to deliver marketing messages to consumers. In this course, build your own website and run real-life Google advertising campaigns.
