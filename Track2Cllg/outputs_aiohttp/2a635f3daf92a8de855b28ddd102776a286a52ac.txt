International Relations
Major & Minor

Bachelor of Arts

As an international relations major, you will explore the great issues of politics in a rapidly changing, global environment. You will explore different approaches to the study of politics, employing historical, comparative, philosophical, legal, and experiential approaches to discipline.

Our international relations degree program at Austin College combines rigorous academics with real-world application. This foundation is strengthened through a departmental emphasis on mastering core knowledge, sharpening critical thinking, and developing clear, persuasive communication. These are key skills that together prepare you for careers in law, policy research, teaching, journalism, management, and government service.

international relations field will require you to hone your communication, critical thinking, quantitative and collaboration skills. As a graduate you will be poised to take on your first job with the knowledge that you can confidently:

describe and illustrate international relations issues effectively in written and oral presentations.

evaluate the effectiveness of international institutions and organizations in various contemporary and historical contexts.
