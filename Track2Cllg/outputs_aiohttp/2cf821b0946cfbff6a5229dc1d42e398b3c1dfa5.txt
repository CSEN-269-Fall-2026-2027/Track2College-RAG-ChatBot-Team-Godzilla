Explore the resources below to learn more about fees, deadlines, methods of payment, and more!
Reminder:
 All students enrolled at a CSU campus pay a systemwide tuition fee, in addition to campus mandatory fees. The CSU makes every effort to keep student costs to a minimum. All listed fees, other than mandatory system-wide fees, are subject to change without notice, until the date when instruction for a particular semester begins. All CSU listed fees should be regarded as 
estimates that are subject to change
 upon approval by the Board of Trustees, Chancellor, or the President, as appropriate.

Student Involvement and Representation Fee (SIRF)
: On January 28, 2015 the CSU Board of Trustees established a new voluntary student fee, the Student Involvement and Representation Fee (SIRF). 
This fee is charged to all students unless they 
choose to opt out of the fee
, which can be done via MyCSULB Student Center.
 The fee amount is $2 per fall and spring term, and is used to support the student leadership, involvement, governance, and advocacy programs managed by the California State Student Association (CSSA). For more information, see 
the CSSA website
.

If you are not a resident of California, review the
 Non-Resident Tuition

                        Non-Resident Tuition

If you are 
not a resident of California
, 
you must add $471.00 for each unit of credit

you take 
to your tuition total.
Example Non-Resident Tuition for an undergraduate who wishes to register for 12 units:
Total California Resident Tuition and Mandatory Fees: $4,365.00
Multiply the number of units (12) by $471.00 = $5,652.00
Add the two amounts together for total non-resident tuition: $4,365.00 + $5,652.00 = $10,017.00
Students may be exempt from non-resident classification if they are a teacher, military service member, California high school graduate who has attended a California high school at least three years, or a dependent child of California residents. If so, you must apply for an exemption every semester through Enrollment Services.
Learn more via Residency for Tuition Purposes.

                        Refunds

The university automatically generates refunds two weeks after census, which is approximately 6 weeks after the start of the semester. If you drop all classes prior to the first day of instruction or drop from full time to part time on or prior to the last day of self-service registration you may submit an 
Application for Refund of Fees
.
Explore the refund schedule, learn about university tuition policies, and more via 
Tuition and Fees: Refunds
.

                        Financial Aid and Scholarships

There are various forms of financial aid opportunities that are available to students depending on eligibility. Remember, your financial aid package will always 
align with your unit load
 (for example, if you drop a class and have 6 units of coursework vs. 9 units, this may impact your financial aid amount as you will move from being considered a full-time-student to a part-time student). If your financial or educational situation changes after you have applied for aid, you may 
apply for an appeal
.

                        About Cost of Attendance

Tuition and fees represent only a part of your total education costs, which include items such as books, supplies, and living expenses. At Cal State Long Beach, the following elements are included in standard Cost of Attendance estimates:  
Tuition and Mandatory Fees
Living Expenses (Housing and Food)
Books, Course Materials, Supplies, and Equipment
Transportation
Miscellaneous Personal Expenses
Federal Student Loan Fees 
Except for tuition, mandatory fees, and on-campus housing, all other components of Cost of Attendance are based on the data from the Student Expenses and Resources Survey administered by the California Student Aid Commission.

                        Understanding CSULB Mandatory Fees

Supporting Your Student Experience, Success, and Campus Life
Your mandatory fees at CSULB help fund essential services, programs, and facilities that support your academic journey and enhance campus life. Here’s how your fees work for you:
Health Facility Fee:

Supports the construction, maintenance, and operations of the Student Health Center, ensuring you have access to a safe, modern space for your healthcare needs.
Associated Students, Inc. (ASI) Fee: 
Funds Associated Students, Inc. (ASI), a nonprofit organization that enhances student life through programs, services, events, facilities, and student-led initiatives across campus. For more information, please visit the 
ASI
 website.
Instructionally Related Activities (IRA) Fee: 
Provides funding for hands-on learning experiences beyond the classroom, including academic competitions, performances, research opportunities, athletics, and support programs tied to your coursework.
Student Recreation & Wellness Center (SRWC) & University Student Union (USU) Fee: 
Supports the construction, operations, services, and ongoing improvements of campus facilities where you can stay active, connect with others, and access resources. This includes recreation programs, student events, study spaces, and wellness services. For more information, please visit the 
Student Recreation & Wellness Center
 and 
University Student Union
 websites.
Student ID Card Fee: 
Supports the operation of ID Card Services which issues the official CSULB student ID card. This ID card provides access to various campus services, programs, and resources. For more information, please visit the 
Beach Shops
 website.
Student Excellence Fee: 
Invests in your success by supporting academic advising, technology upgrades, cultural and resource centers, campus transportation, and athletics—enhancing your overall student experience.
Student Health Center Fee: 
Funds a wide range of health, wellness, and preventative services to help you stay healthy and succeed. For more information about the services offered, please visit the 
Student Health Services
 website.
