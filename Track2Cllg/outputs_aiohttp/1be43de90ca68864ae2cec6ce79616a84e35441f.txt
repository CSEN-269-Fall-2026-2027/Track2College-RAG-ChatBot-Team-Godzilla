About the Mathematics Department

Trinity's Mathematics department blends pure and applied study, covering everything from algebra and analysis to statistics and financial mathematics. Tigers build strong analytical and problem-solving skills through a flexible curriculum, interdisciplinary connections, and a capstone Majors' Seminar, with plenty of research and internship opportunities along the way.

Take on the challenge

I loved the way math made me think. It requires hours of logical thinking and is very applicable to most social science questions. After taking enough mathematics classes, I decided, though I struggled with it, to major in it because I really loved the challenge.

Grace Etzel ’26

Areas of Study

Trinity's Mathematics department offers a B.A. in Mathematics and a minor. The program covers both pure mathematics and applied areas, giving Tigers the flexibility to tailor their studies to their interests and goals.
