Mechanical & Materials Engineering 

Department of Mechanical & Materials Engineering
The Department of Mechanical & Materials Engineering leads in innovative research and education, preparing students to become skilled engineers and future industry leaders.
Our faculty and students take on major challenges in space exploration, environmental control, defense, energy, transportation and bioengineering, developing technologies that move the field forward.
Through hands-on learning and collaborative research, we create a dynamic environment where students build the practical skills and experience needed to shape the future of engineering.
