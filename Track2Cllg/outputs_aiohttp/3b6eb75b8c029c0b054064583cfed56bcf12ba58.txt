A Finance Curriculum That Makes a Difference
Our expert team of award-winning research professors, business leaders, and investment analysts will teach you everything you need to know — from investments to corporate and international finance. You’ll get to crunch actual financial and economic data and apply your analysis to a 
real $200,000 donor fund
. The finance curriculum at the Knauss School of Business will also prepare you to take the first two levels of the Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) exam, a certification that lead to more job opportunities and a higher salary in the financial field.
Featured Classes
FINA 300
Financial Management
You can have $5,000 now or in three years — which do you choose? Learn about time value of money, ratio analysis, stock and bond valuation, and other topics that guide financial managers in making crucial business decisions.
FINA 402
Investments
What actually goes into analyzing investments? Where does analytical information come from, and how is it used? Learn different methods of analyzing markets, stocks, fixed-income portfolios and other derivatives.
FINA 404
Advanced Corporate Finance
Apply financial management concepts you’ve studied to actual business situations using case studies. In this course, you’ll learn about topics like risk and return, capital structure, dividend policy, mergers and more.
FINA 408
Financial Statement Analysis
Develop a core set of skills essential to analyzing financial statements: strategic ratio analysis, financial modeling and cash flow analysis, among others.
