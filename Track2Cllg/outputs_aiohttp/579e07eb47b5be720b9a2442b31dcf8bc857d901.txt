Biology is the study of life, and a degree in Biology from Southwestern Adventist University opens the door to diverse and exciting paths. Whether your passion lies in ecology, genetics, microbiology, or molecular biology, our programs are designed to give you a rigorous scientific foundation while nurturing your curiosity for the natural world.

You will engage with faculty who bring real-world experience as researchers and educators and who guide you in developing your critical thinking and research skills. Our hands-on approach includes lab experiments, field studies, and research projects that prepare you for graduate or professional school, teaching, or a wide array of science-based careers.

Curriculum and Courses

Our Biology curriculum emphasizes both theoretical knowledge and practical application. Courses span from foundational biological principles to specialized topics like microbiology, genetics, and environmental biology. Lab work, field studies, and student research projects ensure experiential learning. Whether you are planning to pursue a career in healthcare, research, or science education, our courses will give you the tools to succeed.
