Middle Class Scholarships

Initial awarding of the 2026-27 Middle Class Scholarship (MCS) will begin mid to late Fall. The California Student Aid Commission (CSAC) requires that we continuously monitor awards for changes to eligibility. Adjustments will be made based on changes to enrollment, cost of attendance or any other factor impacting award calculation. For these reasons, initial awarding will begin no sooner than after the fall financial aid freeze date. Other sources of funding should be used to meet expenses until the awards are posted to your financial aid package.
