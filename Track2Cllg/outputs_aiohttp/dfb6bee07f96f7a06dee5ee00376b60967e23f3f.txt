Contact Us

There are several ways to get in touch with USC admission. Find what you need below.

          Ask the Office of Admission

The 
askUSC
 portal will help you find answers to our most commonly asked questions — and even some you may not have thought about. If you can’t find your answers in the portal, you can email the Office of Admission at 
askUSC
.

          Find Your Admission Counselor

Search for the 
USC Admission Counselor
 who works with your school. They will serve as your primary point of contact in the Office of Undergraduate Admission.

          Learn More about USC

Submit a 
Request for Information
 to join our email list.

          Visit Your Applicant Portal/Check Your Status Online

Once you have applied to USC, you will receive a confirmation email from our office with instructions on how to set up your account in the 
USC Applicant Portal
.

Check your portal on a regular basis to make sure you have submitted all necessary materials.

          Submit Follow-Up Application Materials

Students are encouraged to submit their transcripts electronically. Information about sending e-transcripts can be found at 
askUSC
.

Letters of recommendation that cannot be submitted through the Common App can be emailed to 
admlors@usc.edu
. Please do not send any other materials or information to this address.

Materials that cannot be submitted electronically should be mailed to the following address:

USC Office of Admission

Undergraduate

University Park Campus

Los Angeles, CA 90089-0911

          Join a Campus Tour or Information Session

For a full list of in-person and virtual tour options, please visit our 
Campus Tours
 page.

          Gain Insights from Community Engagement Leaders 

Community Engagement Leaders are current USC students dedicated to connecting with students from all backgrounds. They are here to offer insight into the University of Southern California and their own experiences finding community on campus. You can contact them 
USCCEL@usc.edu
.

          Connect with a Transfer Ambassador

Meet with a member of the USC Admission Transfer Ambassador Program. Our Transfer Ambassadors are current USC students serving as peer mentors who can share their firsthand experiences as transfer students at USC. Visit our 
registration page
 for more information.
