Get to Know Us
Your college
experience
begins with a 
visit
.
Florida State students can look forward to a genuine college town experience, both on and off campus. We are located on almost 500 acres in the heart of Tallahassee, just miles from the Capitol and downtown areas.
Whether you are walking along the red-brick pathways of our beautiful campus or studying on Landis Green, you immediately feel connected to the rich history and traditions of FSU.
Check out our Visitor Center website to schedule your campus visit, register for a live virtual information session, or enjoy a virtual campus tour.
