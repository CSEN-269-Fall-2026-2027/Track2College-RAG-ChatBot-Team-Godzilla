Office of Financial Aid

Invest in Your Future with Confidence

Achieving academic success requires dedication, time, and effort. It also takes the support of mentors, family, and friends to help you grow and reach your full potential. Just as importantly, having access to the right financial resources ensures you can start and complete your education without unnecessary barriers.

We’re here to help you navigate your financial aid options so you can make a smart investment in your future. Let us show you how funding your education can open doors to meaningful career opportunities and a fulfilling calling.
