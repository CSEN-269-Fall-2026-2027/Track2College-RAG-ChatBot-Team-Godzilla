Illustration Minor
Minor in Illustration
15 Units

The Illustration Minor at LCAD is designed to transform students into versatile visual storytellers with the technical skills and professional knowledge needed to thrive in today’s diverse illustration marketplace. This comprehensive program develops both artistic excellence and business acumen, preparing students to create compelling imagery across multiple industries and applications.

Career Applications
The Illustration Minor prepares students for careers across multiple industries. Graduates work in publishing creating book covers, magazine illustrations, and educational materials. Commercial opportunities include brand illustration, advertising, packaging design, and product visualization. The entertainment industry offers roles in concept art, storyboarding, character design, and visual development for film, TV, animation, and gaming. Digital careers span web illustration, social media content, app design, and user interface graphics. Many pursue fine art through gallery shows, commissions, and art licensing. The minor also leads to freelance work, art direction positions, and teaching opportunities, allowing graduates to specialize in specific areas or maintain diverse client bases.
