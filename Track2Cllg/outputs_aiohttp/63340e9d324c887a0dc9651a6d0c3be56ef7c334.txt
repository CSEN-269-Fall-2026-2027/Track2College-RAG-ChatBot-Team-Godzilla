Bachelor of Science

Austin College is known for producing some of the finest educators in the field. Are you ready to join their ranks?

Education majors at Austin College can pursue one of two pathways, leading to certification in either elementary or secondary education. Both pathways are designed to combine a strong liberal arts foundation with focused professional preparation for teaching.

Many future teachers participate in the 
Austin Teacher Program (ATP)
, which allows you to complete a bachelor's degree and master's degree in teaching in 4.5-5 years. Take your position at the head of class and achieve your goal of becoming a dedicated and creative teacher.

Being a great teacher 

requires you to strengthen and refine your skills in communication, critical thinking, and collaboration. At graduation with a degree in education you will be prepared to:

clearly communicate the results of research into the complex, ethical, political, or economic forces that influence educational policy in oral, written and electronic formats;

utilize technology to plan, present, and reflect/receive feedback on instruction; and

design and carry out effective and coherent instruction and assessment based on appropriate learning goals and objective.

Bachelor of Science in Elementary Education

Pre-K through 6th grade teaching certification

Gain the skills and hands‑on experience you need to become a confident, innovative 21st‑century elementary school teacher.

In this four‑year degree, you’ll build a strong foundation in teaching essentials while gaining real classroom experience. Together, these prepare you to step into your first classroom ready to lead, inspire, and make an impact from day one.

Bachelor of Science in Secondary Education

Middle or high school teaching certification (7th - 12th)

Be ready to lead your classroom with the power of dual degrees.

Earn a Bachelor of Science in Secondary Education to build a strong foundation in effective, impactful teaching. Pair it with a second major in your certification area—such as history, PE, Spanish, math, and more—to graduate fully prepared to teach your subject with confidence and expertise.

Education Studies Minor

Explore education from classroom to culture

Interested in how students learn, how schools operate, or how education shapes communities—even if you don’t plan to become a classroom teacher?

The Education Studies minor lets you explore the ideas and practices behind modern education while gaining insights into learning, development, and the role of schools in society.

Master of Arts in Teaching

With an Elementary or Secondary Teacher Certificate

This five-year master of arts program, with a four and half year* option, allows you to receive your bachelor’s degree in any of the liberal arts majors or minors offered by Austin College and continue your studies with graduate education courses, resulting in both a master’s degree and a teaching certification. Our program gives you hands-on experience in local classrooms as early as your first year and continues through your final semester.
