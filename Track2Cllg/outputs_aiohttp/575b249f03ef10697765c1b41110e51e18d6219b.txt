Value, Tuition, and Aid

At Vanguard, we keep costs focused on what matters—Christ-centered academics, caring professors, and a close-knit community—rather than luxury extras. Since 1950, God’s guidance and wise leadership have made a Vanguard education financially accessible, with 98% of students receiving aid to pursue callings in ministry, education, healthcare, and beyond.
