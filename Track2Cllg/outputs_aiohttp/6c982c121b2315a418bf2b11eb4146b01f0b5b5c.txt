ADMISSIONS

ARE YOU READY TO PURSUE YOUR CALLING? 

You’ve been called to do something with your life. Now it’s time to take that next step by applying to Dallas Christian College. Whether you need help figuring out your calling or you already know it and you need help to aggressively pursue it, DCC will help you get to where you want to go!

Whether you’re pursuing your degree on-campus or online, whether you’re a first-time freshman or transferring in to finish your degree, we’ve got a variety of academic programs, flexibility of class times, and a variety of learning formats to allow you to achieve your goals. Our academic programs and your experience at DCC will prepare you for both a career and for your life. College shouldn’t be just about getting you a degree. It’s more than that. It’s about preparing you for a life of influence.

We encourage you to browse our website to learn more about the variety of DCC’s academic programs, athletic teams, student life opportunities, and our different learning platforms (traditional & DCC|Online).
 If you have any questions, don’t hesitate to contact us at 
admissions@dallas.edu
 or call (214) 453-8155. We’d love to chat and partner with you as you pursue the next step in your life!
