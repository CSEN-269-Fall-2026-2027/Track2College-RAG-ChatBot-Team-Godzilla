Why should I study chemistry?

Chemistry is fundamental to everything we do, see, and are. Understanding chemistry is critical to our attempts to feed and clothe the world, to tapping new sources of energy, to improving health, and to protecting our environment.

What are my job prospects?

The U.S. chemical industry employs more than a million people. As a chemist you have your choice of professions including:

Forensic scientist

Research chemist

Biochemist/ Pharmaceutical chemist

Chemical analyst

What industry-specific skills will I learn?

Use UT Permian Basin’s modern lab facilities to deepen your understanding of chemical concepts. Develop your practical abilities by designing experiments with our equipment.
