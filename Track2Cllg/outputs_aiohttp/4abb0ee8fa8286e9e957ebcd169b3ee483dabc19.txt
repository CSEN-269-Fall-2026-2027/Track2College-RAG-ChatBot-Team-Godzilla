What You’ll Learn about Business Administration
Have you ever wondered how Amazon Prime works on the backend?  We will teach you the frameworks of successful supply chain management that make same-day shipping possible. Or perhaps you want to better understand how a trend, idea or product goes viral? We will teach you the mechanics behind a great marketing campaign... and then you’ll build one yourself. The business administration major will teach you the foundations of business and introduce you to disciplines like supply chain, marketing, accounting and more.
Featured Classes
ECON 101
Principles of Microeconomics
Why is food more expensive in some places than in others? Learn how the world decides how to best allocate its scarce resources through the principle of supply and demand.
MGMT 303
Interpersonal Relations
In this interactive course, you will increase self-awareness while also building your communication and leadership skills. Topics discussed include diversity, politicking, influence conflict and negotiating.
ETLW 302
Business and Society
This course considers individual versus societal interests, labor and employment issues, consumer protection, discrimination and diversity, among other topics.
DSCI 303
Operations Management	
Get into the nitty gritty of operations management, taking a strategic look at processes and supply chain management, all while accounting for corporate social responsibility.
