The Accelerated Bachelor of Science in Nursing program offers the BSN program to students already holding a non-nursing bachelor's degree. The ABSN program uses the same curriculum design as the Baccalaureate Nursing Program, follows the stated philosophy and students meet the same terminal objectives as in the regular BSN program. The program is completed within one year, in three consecutive semesters; it is a rigorous program of study. Approved by the California Board of Registered Nurses and accredited by the Collegiate Commission on Nursing Education.

MSMU’s MBA program is recognized for its innovative, theme-based modular approach to learning. The approach helps managers and executive integrate various business disciplines (including management, marketing, accounting, finance and economics) for effective decision-making and innovation. This 20-month, weekend-only MBA program offers classes on alternate weekends, for a total of six weekends per semester. Students can choose from three concentrations: Entrepreneurship, organization leadership and project management.
