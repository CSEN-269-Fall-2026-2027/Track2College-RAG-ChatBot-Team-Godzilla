Mechanical Engineering B.S.

Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering

Learn to Design the Future, Today

Prepare for an interdisciplinary problem-solving career in a broad industry with a
                              Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering from University of Houston-Clear Lake's
                              (UHCL). This program takes a hands-on approach to learning, incorporating numerous
                              laboratory and project-based courses, and with small class sizes, you'll have greater
                              access to faculty who know you by name.

Gain proficiency in fundamental engineering principles, advanced computational techniques,
                              and practical experience with cutting-edge technology and machinery. These skills
                              will prepare you to adapt to future trends, ensuring that you'll be well-equipped
                              for the evolving demands of the engineering sector. 

Graduates benefit from global job prospects, the chance to innovate and solve societal
                              challenges, and the development of valuable problem-solving and teamwork skills. Continuous
                              learning and professional growth are integral, making UH-Clear Lake's Mechanical Engineering
                              B.S. both rewarding and impactful.

Why Study Mechanical Engineering at UHCL?

State-of-The-Art Learning Environment

Earn your degree in UHCL's cutting-edge STEM and Classroom Building, featuring 15
                                 teaching labs for mechanical engineering, physics, chemistry, geology, industrial
                                 hygiene and environmental safety. Gain first-hand experience with 12 specialty rooms
                                 designed for mechanical-engineering projects, computer-aided drafting, 3-D printing
                                 and more.

Enhanced Learning and Career Prospects

UHCL's Mechanical Engineering B.S. provides a solid foundation in core engineering
                                 principles, with opportunities for hands-on projects and internships at prestigious
                                 companies like NASA, Boeing, and KBR. This versatile degree opens diverse career opportunities
                                 across industries like aerospace, oil and gas, energy, and manufacturing, often with
                                 competitive salaries. 

Competitive Salary Potential

According to U.S. News & World Report, mechanical engineers earn a median salary of
                                 $96,310. The Bureau of Labor Statistics projects a 10% employment growth for this
                                 position through 2032. In addition, the average salary has steadily increased since
                                 2010 and is expected to continue on an upward trajectory.

Reputable Employment Opportunities

Alumni of UHCL's mechanical engineering degree have gone on to land careers as area
                                 managers, engineering technicians, West Coast fuel oil schedulers, engineers, and
                                 project module leads, to name a few. Some of the renowned employers include:

Philips 66

Nationally Accredited Program

The Mechanical Engineering BS program is accredited by the Engineering Accreditation
                           Commission of ABET, 
https://www.abet.org
, under the commission's General Criteria and Program Criteria for Mechanical and
                           Similarly Named Engineering Programs.

The program is also accredited by the Southern Association of Colleges and Schools
                           Commission on Colleges (SACSCOC). This means that the university has complied with
                           the standards contained in the Principles of Accreditation: Foundations for Quality
                           Enhancement and with the policies and procedures of the Commission on Colleges.

No Class Rank / No Test Scores Available

Students without a class rank or test scores will be reviewed based on CGPA:

High School CGPA of 3.25 and higher – Direct Admission into Mechanical Engineering,
                                                         BS program.

High School CGPA of below 3.24 – Students who meet UHCL regular admission criteria
                                                         can be admitted as Pre-Engineering.

Recommended preparation courses for Mechanical Engineering include completion of the
                                 following either in high school or through dual credit:

Precalculus (MATH 2412) and/or Calculus (MATH 2413)
