About the Department

Trinity's computer science department provides broad training in design, systems, applications, and the theory of computing. Students receive substantial laboratory experience with computers, programming, and other related areas, which provide the knowledge and tools to be successful in both theoretical understanding and applied computing. ​​​​​​

Areas of Study

Trinity offers a Bachelor of Science and a Bachelor of Arts in computer science. The curriculum reflects the significant changes that have occurred in the discipline in recent years, and follows specific guidelines from the Association for Computing

 Computer Science (B.A., B.S., Minor)

The major and minor provide practical computing skills for careers and graduate study. The major is designed to complement another discipline, making it ideal for students whose primary field relies on computing.

Program Details

Scientific Computing (Minor)

Turn data, models, and code into scientific insight. Scientific Computing helps students apply computational tools to real-world challenges in science, engineering, and mathematics.
