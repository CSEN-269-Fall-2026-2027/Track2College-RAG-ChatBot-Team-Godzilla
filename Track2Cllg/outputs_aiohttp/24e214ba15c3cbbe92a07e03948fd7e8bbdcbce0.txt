Bring UHD to Your School

The University of Houston-Downtown is a student-centered institution committed to
                              helping students realize their potential as they achieve their personal and professional
                              goals. With 49 bachelor and 13 master programs, UHD welcomes and supports a diverse
                              student body as they pursue their dreams of a higher education. Whether you are a
                              freshman entering straight from high school, a transfer student completing your degree,
                              or a professional looking to gain a competitive edge, you can accomplish your goals!
                              The Office of Admissions at UHD will be your resource and guide as you transition
                              to the University. Find your student type to the left to learn more and apply.
