FAFSA is now available

The 2027-28 FAFSA form is available to compete on studentaid.gov. This application will apply you for financial aid for the summer 2027, fall 2027, and spring 2028 semesters. UTA's 2027-28 priority date is March 1, 2027. Once our office receives your completed form, we will notify you with next steps via your UTA email address.

Blaze Forward Program

The Blaze Forward Program makes it even easier for eligible students to get a world-class education here at UTA! Using a combination of grants, tuition waiver and scholarships, the Blaze Forward Program provides students meeting all eligibility requirements with funding that will cover 100% of their tuition and course fees for fall and spring semesters. The Blaze Forward Program does not cover other expenses including but not limited to housing, meals, and textbooks. The deadline to be considered for the 2026-27 aid year was March 1, 2026. The deadline to be considered for the 2027-28 aid year is March 1, 2027.
