Step 1: Get Ready!

Before you start your application:

Check the 
admission requirements
 for your major and level

If you're feeling unsure, 
schedule a virtual appointment
 with an Admissions representative, call us (760-750-4848), or email us at 
apply@csusm.edu

Step 2: Submit Your Application on Cal State Apply

During the application period for Spring or Fall admission (see 
When to Apply
), go to 
Cal State Apply
 to submit your application for admission. If you are a freshmen, you can also launch
                              your application from your 
CaliforniaColleges
 account. 

Need help completing your application? 
Follow-along with one of our instructional videos!

Freshmen: Click Here

Step 3: Check Your Email and Follow Next Steps
Within a day or two of submitting your application in Cal State Apply, you will begin
                           to receive emails from CSUSM with instructions for next steps. Be sure to check your
                           inbox regularly!
