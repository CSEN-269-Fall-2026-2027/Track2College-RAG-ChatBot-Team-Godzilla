Enrolling in college is a considerable financial commitment, but an invaluable investment.
                           The mission of the Office of Financial Aid and Scholarships is to ensure students
                           can study at Texas Tech regardless of their ability to pay. Striving to maximize funds,
                           exercise good stewardship, and support student success, we work together to:

Assist in the recruitment, retention, and success of our students

Uphold federal, state, and institutional policies and practices

Recognize academic success of our students

Support the University’s enrollment and campus community goals

We are here to support your success.
