Steps to Apply

Submit Application

Complete and submit your application online

Submit 1 personal reference

Forms are included in the Application Form page

Submit Pastor Recommendation Letter

Only for Biblical/Theological Degree applicants.

Submit Proof of Meningitis Vaccination

For students under 22 taking classes on campus. Form is included in the Application Form page

Submit Official Transcript

Submit an official transcript from each high school attended and/ or each college attended. Transcripts will only be considered official when sent directly from the institution to BUA via the address above. Final official transcripts must be submitted upon graduation and must include your graduation date.
