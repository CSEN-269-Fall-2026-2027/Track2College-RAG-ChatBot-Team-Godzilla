Why Study Sport Management?

Sports are a business and a big part of community life. In this minor, you learn how teams and sports organizations work, and you practice skills in leadership, communication, and ethics, all in the city of San Antonio. 

San Antonio as Your Classroom

Students regularly engage with SAFC at Toyota Field, Spurs at the Frost Bank Center, and Valero Alamo Bowl staff at the Alamodome. 

Leadership Experience That Employers Notice

Students complete a 50-hour service requirement connected to teamwork, leadership, and civic engagement.

What Sets the Experience Apart

Small class sizes create opportunities to work closely with faculty, participate in research, and study abroad.

Sport Management is a good fit for you if:
Want to lead teams or projects in fast-moving environments
Are interested in how sport connects to community and culture
Want experience that strengthens communication and leadership
Are considering careers connected to sports, nonprofits, education, or media

What You’ll Study

Courses focus on understanding how sports organizations operate and how sports influence society. Students learn through discussion, projects, and real-world experience.

Sport in England

Students explore the cultural and political forces shaping British athletics, reflecting Trinity’s commitment to global perspective and critical inquiry.

Sport and Sponsorship Marketing

Study the changing sports marketing industry through hands-on analysis of campaigns from global sports properties.

Introduction to Sport Philanthropy

Experience sport philanthropy through a “Living Classroom” model featuring guest lectures from well-known leaders in sports, nonprofit, and government sectors.

Sport Management Internship

Faculty-guided internships connect classroom learning to professional practice.
