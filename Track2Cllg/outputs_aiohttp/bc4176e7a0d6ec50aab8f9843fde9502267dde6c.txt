The Computer Science program at Southwestern Adventist University prepares students to excel in the ever-evolving field of technology.

Our curriculum emphasizes critical problem-solving skills, ethical decision-making, and multidisciplinary cooperation, all within a framework of Christian values. Students gain hands-on experience in software development and work closely with experienced faculty dedicated to their academic and professional growth.

Curriculum and Courses

Our Computer Science curriculum combines theoretical knowledge with practical applications. Courses cover topics such as algorithms, data structures, software engineering, network security, and database systems. Students participate in collaborative projects and real-world simulations, building a solid foundation in both individual and team-based work environments.
