Undergraduate Application

As a rolling admissions college, we accept applications year-round and are available anytime to answer questions you may have about academics, campus life, financial aid, or anything else. Having rolling admissions also means we’ll be able to make admissions decisions promptly after an application is submitted. We recommend you apply during the fall of your senior year for maximum scholarship consideration.

Undergraduate Application

We also have a 
test-optional policy
 that allows students to be reviewed holistically, rather than just on test scores. This is vital to our mission as a college: “Cura Personalis”, the care for the whole person, intellectually, socially, and spiritually.

Once your application file is complete, you can expect to receive an admission decision within 10 days.

A complete admission file consists of:

A transcript of high school grades covering at least six semesters

A copy of your 
ACT
 or 
SAT 
scores if you would like them to be considered as a part of your application

All prospective students are encouraged to 
visit the campus
.
