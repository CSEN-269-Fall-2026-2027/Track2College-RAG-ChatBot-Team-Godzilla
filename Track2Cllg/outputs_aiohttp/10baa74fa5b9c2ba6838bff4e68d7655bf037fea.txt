                     Help Safeguard the Planet with an Environmental Science Degree

                     Do you have a passion for protecting the environment and preserving the earth for
                     future generations? With a Bachelor of Science in Environmental Science degree from
                     TROY, you can combat threats to the environment like climate change, resource consumption,
                     biodiversity loss, ocean acidification and soil degradation. Offering five concentrations,
                     hands-on learning and guidance from expert faculty, TROY's environmental science program
                     aims to prepare you to develop innovative, sustainable solutions for our most pressing
                     environmental challenges.

                     Choose from Five Environmental Science Major Concentrations

Whether you want to work with marine life, land, numbers or policy, TROY's environmental
                           science program allows you to tailor your degree to your interests and strengths.
                           The 
environmental science major 
offers five concentrations to help meet your career goals: Biology, Marine Biology,
                           Chemistry, Geography or Mathematics. Each of these concentrations equips you with
                           the expertise and specialized skills you need to live out your passion while preparing
                           you to be competitive in the job market.

                     Gain Experience in the Sciences and Research

At TROY, you'll build an understanding of the sciences and critical environmental
                           issues, practicing research and practical skills as you go. You'll learn from skilled
                           faculty who can draw from extensive research and field experiences as they mentor
                           you in the classroom. And, you'll practice what you learn in projects, labs and hands-on
                           classroom activities to ensure you're well-prepared for 
environmental science careers
.

Environmental Science Degree Concentrations

Tailor your environmental science degree to your interests and future career with
                  one of the following concentrations. 

Biology

In the Biology concentration, you'll explore additional topics in ecology, genetics
                           and wildlife biology. This focus prepares you for careers in wildlife conservation,
                           environmental research and ecological consulting. You can pursue roles like wildlife
                           biologist, conservation scientist or research technician, where you'll contribute
                           to protecting and managing natural resources and biodiversity.

Marine Biology

In the Marine Biology concentration, you'll study marine ecosystems, oceanography
                           and marine life. This focus prepares you for careers in marine conservation, such
                           as marine biologist, oceanographer or marine conservationist, where you'll work to
                           protect and preserve ocean environments and marine species.

Chemistry 

In the Chemistry emphasis, you'll focus on environmental chemistry, toxicology and
                           chemical analysis. This emphasis prepares you for careers in environmental testing,
                           pollution control and regulatory compliance. You can pursue roles like environmental
                           chemist, toxicologist or lab analyst, where you'll analyze pollutants, develop solutions
                           for environmental issues and ensure safety and compliance with environmental regulations.

Geography

In the Geography emphasis, you'll study geographic information systems (GIS), cartography
                           and spatial analysis. This emphasis prepares you for careers in environmental planning,
                           GIS analysis and resource management. You can pursue roles like GIS analyst, environmental
                           planner or cartographer, where you'll map and analyze environmental data to support
                           sustainable development and resource management efforts.

Mathematics

In the Mathematics concentration, you'll focus on statistical analysis, modeling and
                        quantitative methods. This emphasis prepares you for careers in environmental data
                        analysis, climate modeling and resource management. You can pursue roles like environmental
                        data analyst, climate modeler or risk assessor, where you'll use mathematical tools
                        to solve complex environmental problems and support decision-making in sustainability
                        and conservation efforts.

FAQ About the Undergraduate Environmental Science Program

Learn more about the field of environmental science and our program.

What is environmental science

Environmental science is the study of how natural and human-made environments interact.
                           It combines biology, chemistry, geology and social sciences to understand and address
                           environmental challenges like pollution, climate change and conservation. 

Why is environmental science important?

Environmental science is important because it helps us understand our impact on the
                           planet and learn to protect the environment. By studying ecosystems, natural resources
                           and environmental challenges, environmental science guides efforts to combat climate
                           change, conserve biodiversity and ensure sustainable living. Environmental science
                           helps us work toward a sustainable future, providing the information and tools to
                           address pollution, resource depletion and environmental health. Through environmental
                           science, we can make informed decisions to create a healthier and more sustainable
                           world.

What do you learn in environmental science?

Environmental science encompasses topics and research from biology, chemistry, geology
                        and ecology. It also includes studies in social science, policy and conservation.
                        As an 
environmental science major
 at TROY, you'll study how to assess environmental problems, such as pollution and
                        climate change, and explore sustainable solutions. Our curriculum includes fieldwork
                        and lab research that trains you to apply scientific methods to real environmental
                        issues. This multidisciplinary approach equips you with the knowledge and skills needed
                        to pursue diverse careers in environmental science and make a positive impact on the
                        planet.

What can you do with a bachelor's in environmental science?

With a 
bachelor's in environmental science
, you can pursue various careers, including conservation, wildlife biology, sustainability
                           coordination and environmental policy. TROY graduates go on to work in roles that
                           involve protecting natural resources, conducting research, managing environmental
                           projects and developing policies to address environmental issues. You might find opportunities
                           in government agencies, nonprofit organizations, private companies or research institutions. 

Specific 
jobs for environmental science majors
 include sustainability specialist, conservation scientist, wildlife biologist, marine
                           biologist, geospatial analyst, environmental policy analyst, water resources specialist
                           and environmental health and safety specialist. With your undergraduate degree from
                           TROY, you can also go on to pursue 
graduate study in environmental and biological sciences
. 

Is environmental science a good major?

If you are interested in the sciences and making a positive impact on the environment,
                           environmental science is a good major. This field offers diverse career opportunities
                           in areas like conservation, environmental consulting, sustainability and research.
                           As environmental challenges like climate change and resource depletion become more
                           pressing, the demand for professionals with expertise in environmental science will
                           continue to grow. A 
degree in environmental science
 provides you with a strong foundation in science and problem-solving skills, preparing
                           you for meaningful and impactful careers that contribute to a sustainable future.
