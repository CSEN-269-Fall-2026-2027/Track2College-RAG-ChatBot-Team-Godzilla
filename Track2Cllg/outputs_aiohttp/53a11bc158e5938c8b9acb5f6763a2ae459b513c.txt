Welcome to Stillman College Office of Admissions

Welcome future Tigers! Throughout our site, we provide insight and answers to make your college decision a little easier. Our staff is dedicated to assisting you during your college selection process. We are here to assist you in any way that we can.  For questions about the campus, admissions and enrollment processes, do not hesitate to call!  We have an Admissions Specialist waiting to assist you in your journey to becoming a future Scholar at Stillman College!

Why you should Choose Stillman!

Small Class Sizes
At Stillman College, we believe that the advantages of small class sizes extend far beyond numbers – it's a testament to our commitment to your success and academic development. Experience these benefits firsthand as you embark on your educational journey with us.

Leader in Online Education
Whether you're a working professional seeking to advance your career, a busy parent juggling multiple responsibilities, or simply someone looking for the convenience of online education, Stillman Online is here to provide you with a world-class educational experience that fits seamlessly into your life.

Family Atmosphere
Discover the warmth and close-knit community at Stillman College, where our family atmosphere fosters an environment of support, belonging, and academic excellence.

Here is what you need!

In order for the Office of Undergraduate Admission to make an admission decision, Stillman must receive a completed application. This includes the online application and supporting material (discover.stillman.edu).

						Application 					

						You can apply via futuretiger.stillman.edu.					

						Apply Now					

						Transcripts					

						Please request that your high school send an official high school transcript electronically to Stillman College. This transcript should reflect grades through at least six semesters of high school work. 

Upon graduation, submit a complete official transcript including the date of graduation.

						Test Scores					

						Scores from the ACT or the SAT I are not required for admission purposes but students may submit them for consideration in illustrating their academic journey. Test scores are optional.					

Tools For Success

Incoming freshmen should have the high school preparation necessary for academic success at the college level. To be eligible for consideration at Stillman College, an applicant must have successfully completed the following high school courses as a minimum:

English/Language Arts – 4 credits
English I, II, III, IV
Mathematics – 3 credits
Algebra I, Algebra II and Geometry (or more rigorous courses in mathematics)
Science – 3 credits
Biology I, Chemistry I, Physics I or life science, physical science, and earth/space science (at least one lab course)
Social Studies – 3 credits
From U.S. History, Economics, Government, World Geography and World Civilization (or comparable courses)
Foreign Language – 2 credits
Two credits in the same foreign language or demonstrated competency
Health – 1/2 credit
Physical Education – 1/2 credit
History and Appreciation of Visual, Performing Arts – 1 credit
History and appreciation of visual and performing arts or another arts course that incorporates such content
Electives – 7 credits
Recommended strongly: one or more courses that develop computer literacy

Additionally, high school students are encouraged to complete at least one year of mathematics beyond Algebra II.
A total of 24 credits or more must be completed in high school. Applicants should contact the Office of Admission and Recruitment for information concerning exceptions to this policy. Admissions@stillman.edu

Transfer Requirements

									Transferring to Stillman College is made possible with any number of college credit hours.								

24 Credit Hours or More

2.0 cumulative GPA
Official transcripts from all previous higher learning institutions
Recommended but not required: Official high school transcripts
Successfully completing two years of the same foreign language in high school will complete the university-level foreign language requirement.

23 Credit Hours or Fewer

2.0 cumulative GPA
Official college transcripts
Official high school transcripts
