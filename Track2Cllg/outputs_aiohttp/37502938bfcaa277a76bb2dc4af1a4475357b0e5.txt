                                                                        JSU's Emergency Management Program: Educating EM Leaders

With a degree in Emergency Management from JSU, you will be ready for 
any community incident,
 from minor emergencies to major disasters.  You will possess the knowledge and skills to lead the way in helping your region, your state, and your country prepare 
and
 recover from major events.  You will have the degree to propel your career forward.   

 Top Three Reasons to Choose JSU Emergency Management

An Accredited Program Taught by Experienced Staff

 What sets JSU’s Emergency Management program apart is its distinguished faculty with extensive experience in the field and the classroom, an excellent reputation in
 academics, and a full complement of required courses and electives from an accredited university.  

Comprehensive Degree Options

 The JSU program produces graduates at the bachelor’s, master’s, and doctorate level, making JSU an excellent choice for individuals wanting to advance their education and careers in emergency management fields.  More than 1200 students have graduated from one of these Emergency Management degree programs.

Bachelor of Science in Emergency Management

Master of Science in Emergency Management

Doctor of Science in Emergency Management

Convenient Online Courses for Working Professionals

 The JSU Emergency Management programs are comprised of online courses that take place in virtual classrooms, meaning students may complete their coursework at the time and place of their choosing so long as they meet deadlines for assignments and exams.  Although the degrees can be earned online, students are still earning a degree in an accredited program.
