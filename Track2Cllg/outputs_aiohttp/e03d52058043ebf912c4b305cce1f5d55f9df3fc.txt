                                Financial Aid                            

Westcliff University’s Financial Aid Department operates on the foundation of Three C’s: Compliance, Customer Service, and Confidentiality. Following the University’s motto, Educate. Inspire. Empower, Financial Aid personnel strive to Educate students in all aspects of their financial aid eligibility, Inspire students to be responsible loan borrowers, and Empower students to be self-sufficient and in control of their financial aid.

                                        Understanding the Financial Aid Process                                    

Apply to Westcliff University

File the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (
FAFSA
)

Westcliff University FAFSA Code: 042496

Review and Accept Your Financial Aid Package

Your financial aid package will be ready within four business days after we receive your FAFSA. You will be notified via email for review and acceptance. Check your personal and Westcliff student emails. This will include instructions for logging into the financial aid system.

Scholarships are a separate application process. Your FA advisor will integrate any Westcliff scholarships into your financial aid package. Please inform your Financial Aid Advisor If you have been awarded an external scholarship.
