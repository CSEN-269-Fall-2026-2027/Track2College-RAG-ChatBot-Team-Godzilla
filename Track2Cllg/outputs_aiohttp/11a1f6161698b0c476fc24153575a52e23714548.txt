Frequently Asked Questions
What are the general requirements for undergraduate admission?
Freshman applicants will need to submit a completed application, high school transcripts, and short answer responses. Biola is test-optional, so SAT/ACT/CLT scores are not required but may be submitted if you feel they strengthen your application. Specific requirements may vary by program, so check with your admissions counselor for any major-specific requirements.
Is there on-campus housing, and do I have to live on campus?
Yes — Biola has a residential campus with housing available for undergraduate students. Freshmen are generally required to live on campus for their first year. Housing options range from traditional residence halls to apartment-style living for upperclassmen. Applying early improves your housing placement options.
When should I apply, and are there application deadlines I need to know?
Biola uses a rolling admissions process, so we review applications as they are received — but applying early gives you the best chance at scholarship consideration and housing priority. We recommend submitting your application by November 30 for priority review for fall enrollment. Contact your admissions counselor for specific deadlines relevant to your applicant type.
Is Biola test-optional? What does that mean for my application?
Yes, Biola is test-optional — you are not required to submit SAT, ACT or CLT scores to be considered for admission. If you feel your scores reflect your academic ability and would strengthen your application, you're welcome to include them. If you choose not to submit scores, your application will be reviewed holistically based on your transcript and short answer responses. Test scores are not required to be considered for merit scholarships.
What GPA do I need to be admitted to Biola?
Biola looks for students who demonstrate strong academic preparation and genuine readiness for university-level work. While there is no single cutoff GPA, competitive applicants typically have a cumulative high school GPA of 3.0 or higher. Some programs — particularly in nursing, science and other high-demand areas — may have additional academic requirements. We encourage you to apply and let our admissions team review your full academic story.
What is chapel, and is it required?
Chapel is one of the most distinctive elements of a Biola education — a regular, community-wide gathering for worship, prayer and reflection. Undergraduate students are asked to attend chapel as part of the Biola experience. It's a place where the entire campus community comes together and many students describe it as one of the highlights of their time at Biola. You can learn more about chapel life at 
biola.edu/chapel
.
