Why should you study Special Education?

As a special education teacher you will have the opportunity to not only impact a student but also leave a life-long impact on their family.

Courses in the Master of Arts in Special Education are offered at an accelerated rate. Each course is eight weeks long versus 16 weeks in a traditional semester. 

What are my job prospects?

The Special Education Program offers a master's degree with specialization in one of the three areas: Autism and Developmental Disabilities, Educational Diagnostician and General Specialist Education. The route you choose determines your job prospects.

Special education teacher

Early interventionist specialist

Special education advocate

Educational diagnostician

Curriculum specialist

Supervisory positions

Manager/Director of special education programs

What industry-specific skills will I learn?

Our Special Education Program will prepare candidates to serve students with developmental learning disabilities. This affordable 36-credit hour online program can be completed in as little as one year and will equip you with the specialized skills and knowledge you need to:

provide accessibility to diverse groups of students

integrate technology and create an equal opportunity for students to succeed 

provide students with as many ways to learn as possible
