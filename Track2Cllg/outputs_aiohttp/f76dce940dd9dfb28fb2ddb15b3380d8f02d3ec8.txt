Standardized Test Scores & Test-Optional Philosophy

Southwestern has a test-optional admission policy for all first-year candidates. At the time of application, candidates must choose whether or not they wish SAT or ACT scores to be considered as a factor in their admission and merit scholarship review process. Our middle 50% of test-scores is 1160-1320 for SAT and 24-30 for ACT.

Students who choose to have their SAT or ACT scores considered may order reports on the College Board or ACT websites. Southwestern will also accept official scores that appear on a student’s high school transcript. In addition, Southwestern will accept a student Score Report that an applicant has downloaded from the College Board or ACT website. College Board SAT Subject Tests are not required. 
Southwestern will Superscore your submitted test-scores for you.
