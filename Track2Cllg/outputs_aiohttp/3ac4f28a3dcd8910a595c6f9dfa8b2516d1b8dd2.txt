IMPORTANT NOTICE: Loan disbursement delay for loans accepted on or after October 6.
 Due to a technical issue, students who accepted Federal Direct Loans (subsidized, unsubsidized, Graduate PLUS, Parent PLUS) on or after October 6, 2026 will experience a delay in their loan disbursement. The Financial Aid & Scholarships Office is currently working to fix this and expect a resolution within 10 business days.

Who Gets Aid?

Nearly two-thirds of undergraduate students qualify for financial aid. Eligible graduate students may be awarded federal student loans and work-study.

More than 60% of Berkeley Undergraduate students receive aid.
