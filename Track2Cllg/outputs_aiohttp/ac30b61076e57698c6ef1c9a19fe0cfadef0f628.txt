Mathematics and Statistics

Department of Mathematics and Statistics
The FIU Department of Mathematics and Statistics is a dedicated community of scholars, educators, and data scientists turning raw information into actionable knowledge. We equip students with essential quantitative and statistical skills. Our research and applied programs prepare graduates to solve complex, real-world problems, driving innovation in technology, business and finance, national security, health care, education and more.
Get the degree. Build the network. Change everything.

Want to become a Learning Assistant?
Learning Assistants are undergraduate students who facilitate collaborative peer learning during group work in STEM classes including Mathematics and Statistics. FIU features one of the largest LA programs in the country, transforming classroom experiences for greater student success.
