Program Overview

The Bachelor of Sports Science (BSS) in Sports Coaching is a 121-credit hour program that combines the science of human performance with the leadership, communication, and practical skills needed to become an effective coach in today’s evolving sports industry. Students study coaching methodology, sport psychology, strength and conditioning, sports medicine, leadership, and sport administration while gaining hands-on experience through High Impact Professional Experience (HIPE) opportunities such as internships, research assistantships, applied projects, and study abroad.

Designed to develop scholar-coaches, the program prepares graduates to lead athletes and teams across youth, scholastic, collegiate, and club sport settings using evidence-based coaching and athlete development strategies. By integrating classroom learning with real-world application, students graduate prepared to mentor, innovate, and thrive in the modern sports performance environment.

Why Choose This Program

The Bachelor of Sports Science (BSS) in Sports Coaching prepares future coaches to lead athletes and teams through a powerful combination of sports science, coaching methodology, leadership, and real-world experience. Students gain a strong foundation in human anatomy and physiology, sport psychology, strength and conditioning, sports medicine, and athlete development while learning how to apply evidence-based coaching practices in competitive sport environments. The program also emphasizes leadership, ethics, communication, and sport management—skills that today’s coaches need to succeed both on and off the field.

What sets this sports coaching degree apart is its focus on High Impact Professional Experience (HIPE). Through internships, undergraduate research, applied projects, and study abroad opportunities, students build professional experience and industry connections before graduation. Graduates leave prepared for coaching careers in youth, scholastic, collegiate, club, and performance sport settings, as well as advanced study in sport science and related fields.

What You’ll Learn

As a Sports Coaching major at USSU, you will learn how athletes develop physically, psychologically, and technically while mastering the principles of effective coaching and athlete performance. Coursework explores motor learning and development, sports coaching methodology, sport psychology, applied sports mechanics, sports nutrition, sports medicine, and strength and conditioning, giving you the knowledge to design safe, effective training programs and support long-term athlete success.

You will also develop skills in leadership, sport administration, ethical decision-making, research, and performance analysis. Through hands-on coaching practica, research projects, and experiential learning opportunities, you’ll learn how to evaluate performance, communicate with athletes and stakeholders, leverage technology in sport, and create positive, high-performing team cultures.

Who This Program Is For

This program is ideal for students who are passionate about sports, athlete development, leadership, and helping others reach their full potential. Whether you aspire to become a youth, high school, collegiate, or club coach, work in strength and conditioning, athlete development, sport performance, or pursue graduate study in sport science, this degree provides the academic and practical foundation to achieve your goals.

The BSS in Sports Coaching is especially suited for individuals who want to combine a love of sport with science-based training methods, leadership skills, and hands-on coaching experience. If you are motivated to lead, teach, mentor, and make a lasting impact on athletes and teams, this program can help you turn that passion into a rewarding career.
