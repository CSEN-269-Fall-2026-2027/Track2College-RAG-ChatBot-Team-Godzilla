Federal Assistance Programs
Federal Pell Grant

Federal Pell Grants are generally available to undergraduate students with exceptional financial need and usually do not have to be repaid.

For the 2026–27 award year (July 1, 2026–June 30, 2027), the scheduled award ranges from $740 to $7,395.

A student’s actual award depends on federal eligibility calculations, FAFSA information, cost of attendance, enrollment intensity and time enrolled during the award year.

Some eligible students may receive up to 150% of their scheduled award through year-round Pell when attending an additional term.

Federal Direct Student Loan Program
A Direct Student Loan is money authorized by the Federal Government to help pay for your college costs. These funds must be paid back to your lender upon your withdrawal from school or graduation.

Direct Loans may be subsidized or unsubsidized. Subsidized Loan eligibility is based on financial need, and interest generally does not accrue while the student is enrolled at least half-time. Unsubsidized Loans are not based on financial need, and interest accrues from the date of first disbursement.

Direct Subsidized and Direct Unsubsidized Loans generally have a six-month grace period after a student graduates, leaves school or drops below half-time enrollment. Repayment terms depend on the borrower’s repayment plan, and federal student loans may be prepaid without penalty.

Federal Loan Changes for 2026–27
The new federal limits apply to borrowers who do not qualify for the limited transition exception. A borrower may qualify for the exception only if the student was enrolled in the same program by June 30, 2026, received a Direct Loan for that program before July 1, 2026, and remains continuously enrolled in the same program at the same school.

Graduate and professional students who do not qualify for the exception are no longer eligible for new Grad PLUS Loans. Review the 
Federal Student Aid updates
 and contact the USSU Financial Aid Office for help determining which limits apply.

Students who received loans before July 1, 2026, may qualify for “Legacy Provision.” Students are encouraged to contact the Financial Aid office to determine if they qualify for this provision.

Schedule of Reduction (SOR)
: Beginning in 2026-2027 and the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, Federal Student Aid Loan limits are now proportional to your enrollment status. Half-time students are only eligible for 50% of the loan limits. Also, if you add, drop or withdraw from any class after loans are disbursed, it will affect your next semester of eligibility.

Federal Parent Loan Program (PLUS)
Parent PLUS Loans are federal loans available to eligible parents of dependent undergraduate students to help pay education expenses not covered by other financial aid.

For borrowers not covered by the limited transition exception, total Parent PLUS borrowing by all parents for one dependent student is limited to $20,000 per academic year and $65,000 in total.

Interest accrues from disbursement. Repayment generally begins after the final disbursement unless the parent requests an eligible deferment.

The student must complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) before a parent applies.

The parent borrower must meet the federal credit requirements. Applications are completed online at 
StudentAid.gov
.

If a parent is unable to obtain a Parent PLUS Loan because of adverse credit, the dependent student may be eligible for additional Direct Unsubsidized Loan funds. Contact the USSU Financial Aid Office for an eligibility review.
