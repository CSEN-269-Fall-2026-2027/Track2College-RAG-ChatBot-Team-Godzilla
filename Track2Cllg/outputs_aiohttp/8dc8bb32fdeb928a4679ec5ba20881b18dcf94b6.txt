Department of Chemistry and Physics

At the University of North Alabama, the Department of Chemistry and Physics offers a robust foundation for students eager to explore the fundamental forces of nature and prepare for exciting careers in science, technology, health, and education. Our mission is to deliver a transformative collegiate experience through a balance of exceptional teaching, innovative research, and meaningful service to the university and community.

Housed in the state-of-the-art Science and Technology (SET) building, our department is home to dedicated faculty with global research experience and a passion for mentoring students. From hands-on learning in the lab to stargazing in our on-campus planetarium and observatory, students gain the theoretical knowledge and real-world experience needed to thrive in diverse scientific fields.

We offer Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Science degrees in Chemistry and Physics, with flexible specialized tracks, including ACS-certified chemistry, biochemistry options, and professional health concentrations. This allows students to tailor their academic journey for medical, engineering, pharmaceutical, forensic, geographical, and other career fields.

Whether preparing for medical school, a research lab, or the classroom, UNA's Department of Chemistry and Physics will help you discover your path and reach your potential.

Start your journey—connect with us today to learn more!
