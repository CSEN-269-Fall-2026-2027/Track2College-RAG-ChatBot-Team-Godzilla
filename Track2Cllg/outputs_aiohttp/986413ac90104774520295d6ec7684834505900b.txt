Researchers See Molecular Order in Plastic at the Nanoscale

Researchers at the FAMU-FSU College of Engineering have directly observed how molecular bonds organize at the nanoscale inside amorphous polystyrene, a common plastic. Understanding order at this scale could help engineers design plastic films that are lighter and stronger for uses such as packaging or medical devices.
