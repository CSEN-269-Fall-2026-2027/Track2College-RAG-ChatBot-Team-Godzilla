The Physical Education program at Southwestern Adventist University prepares future educators and coaches to lead individuals toward healthier, more active lives.

You will learn how to develop and implement exercise routines, sports programming, and wellness activities for children, adolescents, and adults. Whether you plan to work in schools, community organizations, or health-focused programs, our degree equips you with the tools to make a positive impact.

Curriculum and Courses

Courses in the Physical Education program cover key areas such as exercise science, sports management, coaching methods, kinesiology, and health education. You will participate in classroom learning and real-world teaching opportunities that prepare you to design developmentally appropriate physical activity programs for diverse populations.

Certification Options

Students pursuing Physical Education may seek teaching certification for Kindergarten through 12th grade. This pathway allows graduates to work in school systems, teach physical education, or pursue additional roles in wellness or coaching.
