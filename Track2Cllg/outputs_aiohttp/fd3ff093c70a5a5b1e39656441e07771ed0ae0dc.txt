About the Chemistry Department

Trinity University’s Department of Chemistry provides a challenging and supportive learning environment that gives students a comprehensive and rigorous foundation in the chemical sciences.  We engage students as collaborators in our research laboratories, and we bring our research expertise to the classroom.

Areas of Study

Chemistry connects the physical, life, and applied sciences — and at Trinity, you'll graduate ready for medical school, graduate study, or a career driving industries like pharmaceuticals, engineering, and beyond.

Chemistry (B.S.)

The ACS-certified Bachelor of Science degree offers rigorous preparation across all major areas of chemistry and is designed for students pursuing research-intensive careers or advanced study.

View Program

Biochemistry (B.S)

Build a comprehensive understanding of the molecules and processes that sustain life through coursework in chemistry, biology, physics, and mathematics.

View Program

Biochemistry and Molecular Biology B.S.

Students learn to connect concepts from biology and chemistry to answer questions about health, disease, biotechnology, and the natural world.

View Program

arrow_right_alt

Chemistry (B.A.)

Designed for students seeking a strong foundation in chemistry while maintaining flexibility to pursue additional interests, the B.A. is well suited for interdisciplinary study and many pre-professional pathways.

View Program

Chemistry (Minor)

Strengthen your primary study with a foundational chemistry understanding. The minor offers lab and problem-solving skills complementing majors in sciences, health, engineering, and related fields.
