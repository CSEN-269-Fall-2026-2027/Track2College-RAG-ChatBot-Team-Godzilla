Curriculum That’s Built to Last
The real estate market is constantly changing, but the education you’ll receive will serve as the foundation for your lifelong career in real estate. Consider the real estate program your blueprint for success in the industry.
Featured Classes
REAL 320
Principles of Real Estate
To succeed in real estate, you first need to understand the principles and practices around real estate assets in U.S. financial markets. Here, you’ll learn the factors that impact market efficiency.
REAL 324
Real Estate Market Analysis
Markets are constantly changing so you need to know how to analyze the market to fit your goals. Learn more about the process of market analysis in real estate.
REAL 326
Commercial RE Fin & Investment
Learn about the basics of real estate investments and gain critical real estate decision making skills. This class will teach you how to apply those skills to investment opportunities.
REAL 329
Real Estate Development
Ever wondered what goes through the minds of real estate developers on HGTV? Learn tricks of the trade and the real estate development process — from land acquisition to building design.
