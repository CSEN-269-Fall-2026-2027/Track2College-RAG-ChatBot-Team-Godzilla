Cost of Attending UC Riverside

Financing your UC Riverside Education

Paying for college can feel like a lot - but you don't have to figure it out all by yourself. At UC Riverside, we're here to support you every step of the way!

Nearly 80% of UC Riverside undergraduates are awarded financial aid, and 99% of those students receive need-based scholarships and/or grants, money you don't need to pay back.

UC Riverside undergraduates receive a mix of support - grants, scholarships, loans, and work study - to make college affordable. And with Scholarship Universe, you can get matched with scholarships just by answering questions and building your own scholarship profile.

At UC Riverside, the Financial Aid team is ready to help you make college happen. Check out some helpful resources below.

UCR is Ranked High for Value

The Cost of Attendance, also known as a budget, is used to determine how much financial aid students receive. UC establishes these budgets using data from a variety of sources including surveys of UC students. Students are assigned one of the following budgets based on their choice on their aid application, or based on notification from the campus housing office of your signed contract. 

Standard budgets include basic expenses a student will have each year including the following:

UC tuition

Campus Fees

Housing and meals (includes room, groceries, and utilities)

Books and supplies (such as books, supplies, course fees, data services, software, etc.)

Personal expenses (such as toiletries, laundry, clothing, and entertainment)

Transportation (includes an allowance for traveling to and from home and a pro-rated allowance for vehicle insurance and registration)

UC Student Health Insurance (an allowance for health insurance)

University of California Tuition Stability Plan

Beginning fall 2022, tuition will be adjusted for each incoming undergraduate class but will subsequently remain flat until the student graduates, for up to six years.

At the time of admission, the tuition and fees reflected on your financial aid offer will be based on the preliminary information provided in your admission and/or financial aid application. Residence status for tuition purposes is determined by the UCR Office of the Registrar. The Statement of Legal Residence (SLR) indicates your resident non-resident status. You can only submit your SLR after you have submitted your Statement of Intent to Register (SIR). See 
Residency for Tuition Purposes
 for more details. If your residence status changes after you submit your SIR, your financial aid offers will be revised accordingly.
