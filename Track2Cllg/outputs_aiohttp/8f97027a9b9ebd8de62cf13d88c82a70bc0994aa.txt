Do you have an interest in engineering and manufacturing all while improving the production and function of technology? Then this major is right for you. 
Industrial Technology is a field of study designed to prepare technical and management individuals for careers in mechanical, industrial, manufacturing, and process technology fields. 
Students with this degree are prepared to creatively solve problems, become productive innovators, and enter the workforce with advanced knowledge and practical skills.

Why should I study Industrial Technology?

Technology is critical in the workplace and world.

The field of Industrial Technology is designed to prepare students to manage the technical portions of a business across all industries.

Individuals working in industrial technology are creative and technically savvy, and
 learn the expertise needed for a career in a broad range of sectors
.

What are my job prospects?

The Industrial Technology program provides individuals with knowledge and skills for a broad range of careers in fields such as: 

Supply Chain manager

Manufacturing or Industrial Engineer

Operations Manager

Quality Supervisor

Director of Operations

Project Engineer

What kind of skills will I learn?

Industrial Technology skills focus on the practices and processes required to manufacture products in a variety of industries. 
Graduates of the program will be equipped to meet the new and emerging technology in areas such as:

Process technology management

Manufacturing processes and technology

Human relations and management 

Maintenance & operations

Quality control

Our business programs have once again been extended accreditation by the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB). These programs include Management, Marketing, Accounting, Finance, and our graduate programs
