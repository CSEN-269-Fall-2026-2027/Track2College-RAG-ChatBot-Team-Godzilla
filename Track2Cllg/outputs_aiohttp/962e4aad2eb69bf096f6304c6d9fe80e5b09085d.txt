What is a religious studies degree?

In the Bachelor of Arts in Religious Studies program, you will consider the many aspects of religions around the world. Through meaningful study of religions in an academic context, you will dig into the spiritual, historical, literary, ethical, practical, contemplative, and social dimensions of religious people and cultures.

Why should you major in religious studies at Redlands?

As a student in the Religious Studies program, you will work alongside engaged, award-winning faculty and become part of an inclusive community that seeks to promote understanding and generate respect for lived religion across traditions. Through thought-provoking coursework, you will be prepared to continue your education at the graduate level, through pursuing seminary, doctoral studies, or to begin a career in a variety of fields, including chaplaincy, law, medicine, education, global leadership, and public policy. You will have the opportunity to experience and explore local religious communities as well as to travel and study abroad. Each will sharpen your understanding of religion and equip you to positively impact both your local and global community.

Graduates pursue careers as

        Public Policy, Government, and International Relations

With a deep understanding of cultural values, ethics, and belief systems, graduates are equipped to navigate complex global issues and foster more inclusive, effective policymaking with a sensitivity to various religious communities across transnational relations.

        Religious Leadership, Diplomacy, Interfaith Relations

Understanding diverse religious traditions and ethical frameworks will allow graduates to promote dialogue and mutual understanding for peace building and collaboration.

        Global Business and Media

By utilizing deep insights into cultural diversity, ethics, and human values, graduates in religious studies can foster responsible leadership, cross-cultural communication, and meaningful global engagement.

        Education

Religious Studies graduates can inspire critical thinking and empathy while helping a diverse student body explore the world’s diverse cultures and spiritual traditions.
