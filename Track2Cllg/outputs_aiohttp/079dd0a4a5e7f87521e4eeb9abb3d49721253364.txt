A versatile business degree designed for maximum flexibility and opportunity.

The Bachelor of Science in Business at Southwestern Adventist University offers a strong foundation in core business principles while allowing students to tailor their education through electives and interdisciplinary exploration.

Curriculum and Courses

Courses cover essential topics in business administration, including accounting, marketing, management, finance, and business ethics. With a focus on critical thinking and effective communication, students graduate ready for leadership roles in any industry. The program is also available online through 
SWAUonline
 for added convenience.
