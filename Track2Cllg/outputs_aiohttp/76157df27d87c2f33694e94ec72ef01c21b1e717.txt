Social Work Program

Helping people lead better, more fulfilling lives
.

Do you have an interest in helping and serving others? Then a degree in social work is right for you.

The Social Work Program at UT Permian Basin prepares students to promote human and social well-being. Social Work students receive training in a number of areas including interviewing, assessment, problem solving, crisis intervention, community outreach, case management, and client advocacy.

What are my job prospects?

Social workers are employed in various professional agencies and organizations that advocate for human rights and social and economic justice. 
Our graduates fill positions in a variety of roles including:

Case worker

Mental health advocate

Social and community service manager

Clinical coordinator

Family and child advocate

Juvenile court liaison

Marriage or family therapist

Guidance counselor

What kind of skills will I learn?

Students in the Social Work program learn a variety of skillsets that will carry them not only through the workforce, but through their personal lives including: 

Communication

Advocacy 

Leadership

Assessment

Social Work Field Education

Field practicum is an exciting time for the student to explore their interest in the social work profession! Students will be matched with the agency of their interest as they apply skills and knowledge from the classroom into their professional roles.

Core Values

The UTPB Social Work Program is guided by the National Association of Social Workers (NASW) Code of Ethics, and the Council of Social Work Education educational polices and standards.
