become your best self

Philosophy, the “love of wisdom,” is the original academic degree.

Since it established the foundations of mathematics, science, theology, and ethics through its proclivity to wonder at and question life, philosophy is the perfect path for SHSU’s inquiring minds intent on having rewarding careers and living fully engaged lives.

You can find people who studied philosophy in all careers and fields because philosophy:
• teaches excellent written and verbal communication,
• develops reading and public speaking skills,
• highlights moral and ethical reasoning that strengthens your capacity to make good choices,
• analyzes arguments both for critique and for envisioning how to develop better ones,
• and focuses on how to understand and fairly consider others’ worldviews, helping you to work across differences and engage with people of all backgrounds and experiences.

Where and How Is This Program Available?

The Bachelor of Arts in Philosophy is offered in person on our Huntsville campus or online. The online option allows you to complete your four-year degree with flexibility.

Develop Your Skills

    Not convinced you can answer the perennial question: What are you going to do with a degree in Philosophy? Remember: philosophy students routinely outperform nearly all other majors on the LSAT, GRE, and GMAT, but philosophy students also have among the highest lifetime earnings of all humanities fields. People who study philosophy have gone on to be successful tech leaders, entrepreneurs, journalists, professors, politicians, clergy, Supreme Court justices, award-winning writers, actors, comedians, filmmakers, activists, and even Presidents of the United States.

Where Can Philosophy Take You

"What do Pope John Paul II, Alex Trebek (TV game show host), Peter Thiel (co-founder of PayPal), Phil Jackson (basketball coach), Wes Anderson (film director), Stephen Breyer (Supreme Court justice), Matt Groening (“Simpsons” creator), Philip Glass (composer), Carl Icahn (financier and investor), Mary Higgins Clark (mystery author), and Vaclav Havel (former president of the Czech Republic) all have in common?"

They all received a college degree in philosophy.  To hear more from philosophy grads about how their degree served their career success and personal growth, take a deeper look at David Boersema's "What Can I Do with a Degree in Philosophy?"

Philosophy Club

Our mission is to make discussions about philosophy enjoyable and accessible for everyone. We aim to enhance your college experience while helping you connect with students and faculty who share similar interests.

We explore how philosophy applies to everyday life, engage in thought-provoking discussions, and share ideas. We want to have fun while learning together.

Phi Sigma Tau

Phi Sigma Tau is the International Honor Society in Philosophy, established in 1930 and incorporated as a nonprofit in 1955. With over 200 chapters in the U.S. and Canada, it promotes student engagement and connections among philosophy departments.

The Society publishes the journal *Dialogue* for student research contributions and the *PST Newsletter* for chapter updates.
