Unlimited possibility at the tip of your baton.

The exclusive nature of SFCM’s conducting program guarantees students the guidance and opportunities required to become a successful and well-rounded conductor. The core of the program is private, individualized instruction, and public-facing podium opportunities. SFCM's location in the heart of SF's Arts District ensures that conducting students are immersed in the world of the San Francisco Symphony, Opera, and Ballet.

SFCM Music Director Edwin Outwater leads the conducting program at SFCM.

The personalized conducting program has only a few students enrolled at any time

SFCM conducting students are often invited to guest-conduct for large ensemble performances and premiere new works.

Curriculum includes private instruction, rehearsals, and performance, in conjunction with the Conservatory Orchestra and other Conservatory ensembles.
