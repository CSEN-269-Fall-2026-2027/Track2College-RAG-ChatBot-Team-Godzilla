Scholarships & Financial Aid

Public Price Promise Scholarship

Awarding $34,000

The Public Price Promise scholarship awards you the difference in average cost of
attendance between Cal Lutheran and UC schools. 

Visual & Performing Arts

Awarding up to full tuition

Incoming first-year and transfer applicants with superior creative talent in the areas
of music, theatre arts, or visual art can apply and audition for a VAPA Scholarship.

Presidential Scholarship

Awarding up to full tuition

Presidential Scholarships recognize graduating high school seniors in the top tier
of our applicant pool who embody Cal Lutheran's educational and leadership goals.

Steven Dorfman Scholarship

Awarding 75% up to full tuition

The Steven Dorfman Scholarship is awarded to first-year students who will pursue their
studies in the School of Management with an interest in entrepreneurship and innovation.
