DEGREE OVERVIEW 

Marketing is a very broad field of study that examines conception, pricing, promotion, and distribution of ideas, goods, and services. Increasing worldwide competition, expanding global markets, and accelerating technological advancements provide business challenges and demand organizations place greater emphasis on effective marketing as a means of achieving their goals.

ABOUT THE PROGRAM

The AACSB-accredited 120-credit-hour degree program includes 18 hours of marketing courses built around the core business curriculum. Marketing undergraduate courses include:

Professional Selling

Integrated Marketing Communication/Advertising

Consumer Behavior

Social Media and Digital Marketing

Retail Marketing

Marketing Research

Product and Brand Strategy

International and Multicultural Marketing
