Why Study Environmental Studies?

Study environmental challenges through hands-on, interdisciplinary learning in San Antonio and beyond. Build the knowledge and experience to pursue careers in policy, industry, nonprofits, law, or graduate study.

Learn Beyond the Classroom

Study abroad with faculty-led programs in Costa Rica and China.

Turn Learning Into Action

Environmental studies internships are available in city and state government entities and at local volunteer organizations, public institutions, and private firms.

Discover, Research, Present

Take advantage of a wide range of faculty-led research opportunities and opportunities to present research at regional or national academic conferences.

It’s a good fit for you if you:
Are passionate about understanding and addressing environmental challenges at local, national, or global scales
Enjoy exploring the connections between the natural world, society, policy, and sustainability
Like approaching complex problems from multiple perspectives, including science, economics, and public policy
Want hands-on learning opportunities through research, internships, fieldwork, or community engagement
Are interested in careers related to conservation, sustainability, environmental policy, advocacy, or resource management

The Environmental Studies (B.A.)

Environmental Studies majors explore the relationship between human societies and the natural world through an interdisciplinary lens. Students will analyze environmental challenges and develop sustainable solutions, graduating as informed problem-solvers ready to make an impact across sectors.

In-Demand Skills
You’ll gain skills in:
Interdisciplinary research and analysis
Environmental communication and advocacy
Project design and sustainability strategy

Curriculum:
You’ll complete at least 42 credit hours in the areas of environmental science and policy, with interdisciplinary electives and hands-on experiential learning opportunities.
View Degree Requirements

The Environmental Studies Minor

21 Credit Hours  |  1.5 to 2 semesters of coursework
Explore Earth’s environment and how human beings interact with it. 
Beyond the natural sciences, Environmental Studies minors can examine environmental issues from perspectives of social policy, art, urban studies, and the humanities, fulfilling Pathways requirements while maintaining flexibility with their other academic interests.
Graduates leave Trinity with versatile knowledge stemming from the humanities, social analysis, and natural sciences, launching careers in academics, nonprofit public interest groups, private industry, and state and local policymaking.
