Why Study Business Administration at Trinity?

We develop ethical, well-rounded leaders through an interdisciplinary, integrated business education. 
Our students learn business by doing business. They research alongside faculty mentors, intern with local organizations, study abroad, serve the San Antonio community, attend industry conferences, participate in consulting projects, and enter case competitions — all while gaining valuable skills and experience. 
We designed our programs with flexibility at the forefront. Many business students at Trinity create their own double-major by combining interests and passions.
