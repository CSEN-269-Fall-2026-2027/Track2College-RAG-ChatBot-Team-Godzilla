Why should I study spanish?

A Spanish degree at UTPB prepares students to be culturally literate professionals. Students will develop their critical thinking skills, communicate effectively, conduct research, and have an overall understanding of target cultures while obtaining their degree. 

What are my job prospects?

Students with a spanish degree are able to explore career opportunities in a variety of fields such as: 

Government

International business

News/Journalism

Social work

Law enforcement

Translation

Foreign services

Travel and tourism

Teaching

What if I want to teach?

Students interested in obtaining a Spanish degree can also have the opportunity to get their teaching certification to teach Spanish at any level.
