Why Study Political Science at Trinity?

Politics is the most powerful force shaping society — and at Trinity, you'll learn to understand it, analyze it, and engage with it. Through a curriculum spanning American politics, international relations, public law, and political theory, you'll build the critical thinking and research skills to navigate complex issues at every level. With faculty who bring diverse real-world expertise, opportunities to study abroad in cities like Berlin and The Hague, and a track record of graduates excelling in law, government, advocacy, and public service, Trinity prepares you to be more than an observer — but a participant in the world around you.

Political Science (B.A., Minor)

Examine the forces that shape society through a modern political science curriculum and hands-on experiences at the local, state, national, and global levels. Build the knowledge and skills to lead and make an impact.
