Civil & Environmental Engineering

Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering
At the Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering, you won’t just learn how structures are built. You’ll learn how to make them smarter, safer and more resilient. Our students gain a solid foundation in core engineering skills while working with new tools and technologies that are shaping the future of the field.
Here, you’ll study the forces that shape our world, take on real environmental challenges and prepare to design infrastructure that lasts.
Whether you want to create buildings that withstand Category 5 winds, use AI to predict flooding or strengthen bridges with new spray-on concrete, Civil & Environmental Engineering gives you the skills and experience to make a lasting impact.
