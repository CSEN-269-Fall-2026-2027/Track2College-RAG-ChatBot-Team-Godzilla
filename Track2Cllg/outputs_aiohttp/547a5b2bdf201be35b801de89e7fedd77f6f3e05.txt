Admissions

We are thrilled that you are considering an education from Arlington Career Institute. Rest assured that our knowledgeable admissions representatives will guide you through the admissions and enrollment process.

Prior to enrolling, we encourage prospective students to visit and tour Arlington Career Institute so that we can take the opportunity to get to know you as well as your goals. During your visit we can answer any questions you may have about Arlington Career Institute and the programs we offer.

It’s important to us to ensure we are the right fit for you!

To Apply for Admission

Complete the Initial Application for Admission 
here
!

Schedule an appointment to visit Arlington Career Institute or call our admissions office at (972) 647-1607.

To enroll, please provide:

High school diploma, high school transcripts, or GED certificate.

Enrollment fee $100. (Please contact Admissions as not all programs require an Enrollment fee of $100).

A completed Enrollment Agreement.

ACI does not discriminate on the basis of age, race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, creed, color, disability, or national origin.

What are you waiting for? Your future starts now!
