Your Urban Advantage

UNT Dallas offers high-quality academic programs in small classroom settings, where
                              students really get to know their instructors, while earning their bachelor’s or master’s
                              degree.

We are dedicated to putting students on their own, unique career pathways and gaining
                              on-the-job experience as part of high-impact internships and practical learning. We
                              also have a dedicated career services team to help our students and alumni learn more
                              about the DFW job market.
