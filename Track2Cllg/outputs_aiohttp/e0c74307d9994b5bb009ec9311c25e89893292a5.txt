Are you fascinated by the chemistry of life and curious about how biological systems function at a molecular level? Our Bachelor of Science (BS) in Biochemistry bridges the worlds of biology and chemistry, providing a powerful foundation for understanding living organisms and the chemical processes that enable life.

The Biochemistry program at Southwestern Adventist University merges core principles from biology, chemistry, mathematics, and physics.

Through rigorous courses and hands-on laboratory experiences, you will learn to analyze and interpret the chemical behavior of biological systems. This degree opens doors to the biochemical and biomedical fields, emphasizing practical skills that prepare you for immediate employment or further studies.

Curriculum and Courses

Our Biochemistry curriculum integrates core courses in biology and chemistry with advanced electives in math, physics, and related areas. Students engage in hands-on lab work, exploring molecular structures, metabolic pathways, and experimental techniques. This comprehensive academic path ensures you develop the theoretical knowledge and technical skills valued by employers and graduate schools alike.

What can you do with a degree in Biochemistry?

A BS in Biochemistry from Southwestern Adventist University equips you for success in numerous industries and advanced programs. Graduates find opportunities in biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, clinical chemistry, and related fields. The program also provides a strong foundation for entry into medical, dental, pharmacy, and other allied health programs.
