The Finance program at Southwestern Adventist University is designed to equip students with the tools needed to manage resources wisely and make informed financial decisions.

By blending theory with real-world experience, this degree opens doors to a wide range of impactful careers in business and finance.

Curriculum and Courses

Our comprehensive Finance curriculum emphasizes financial analysis, investment strategies, risk management, and portfolio development. Students engage in case studies, simulations, and internships that prepare them to navigate the complexities of global financial systems.
