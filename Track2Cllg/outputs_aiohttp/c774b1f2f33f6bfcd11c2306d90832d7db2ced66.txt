DEGREE OVERVIEW 

The 36-credit hour BA program is designed to meet the needs of students with or without previous experience in the degree. Featuring award-winning, dynamic faculty, our program will provide students with the knowledge and skills they need to be competitive in a changing world.

ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Our program prepares future leaders and public servants for careers across a broad range of sectors, from government to business to nonprofits. Graduates of the program will learn from experts in their field and will have the opportunity to apply their knowledge to real-world situations. Here is just a sample of the courses:

Legislative Organization and Procedure 

Public Opinion 

Government in Urban America 

Health Politics, Policy, and Advocacy 

Introduction to Middle East Politics 

Politics in Popular Culture. 

Civic Engagement, Civil Society, and Community 

U.S. Constitutional Law
