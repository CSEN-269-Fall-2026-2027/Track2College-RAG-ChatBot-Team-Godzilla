Economics, Department & Faculty

Economics is the study of how human communities provide for their material needs and
                              wants.  We explore what motivates human action and thus the choices of individuals,
                              firms, and policymakers regarding production, consumption, and allocation of scarce
                              resources and output.  Institutions, policy, and historical change in the local and
                              global marketplace impact people's daily lives and so we also study the choices people
                              make in their historical, social, environmental, and political contexts.

Economics is the study of how human communities provide for their material needs and wants. We explore what motivates human action and thus the choices of individuals, firms, and policymakers regarding production, consumption, and allocation of scarce resources and output. Institutions, policy, and historical change in the local and global marketplace impact people's daily lives and so we also study the choices people make in their historical, social, environmental, and political contexts.

How will we help face scarcity in the global marketplace?

The curriculum is designed to enable students to achieve a thorough grounding in economic
                              theory; to understand the methods and insights of the economic way of thinking; to
                              explore the intellectual history of economics; to learn the economic history of western
                              civilization; and, to gain a specialized understanding of those aspects of contemporary
                              economics that are of most interest to them.
