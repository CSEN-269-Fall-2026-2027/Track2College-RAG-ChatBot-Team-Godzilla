Why Study Education at Trinity?

At Trinity, we prepare educational leaders and learners to transform schools and communities through equity, excellence, and collaboration. Our nationally recognized programs combine personalized learning, dedicated faculty mentorship, and real-world experiences to prepare students for careers in teaching, school psychology, and educational leadership.
Rooted in the San Antonio community, we believe education is a pathway to meaningful change. Through partnerships, scholarship, and practice, our students, alumni, faculty, and community partners work together to improve educational outcomes and create lasting impact locally and beyond.
