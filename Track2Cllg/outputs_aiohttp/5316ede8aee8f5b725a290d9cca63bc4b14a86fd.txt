General Financial Aid Information

To Top Button

Financial aid is provided through a collaborative process involving the student, the student’s family, and Texas A&M International University. This process utilizes federal, state, and local programs to help meet the financial needs of students attending college.

The mission of the Office of Financial Aid is to support both students and parents. Our office offers guidance and information about the financial aid programs available to help students achieve their educational goals.

To ensure a fair assessment of a family’s financial resources and their ability to cover the cost of education, an application must be submitted annually. This application covers the long semesters (Fall and Spring) as well as the Summer sessions.

This website is designed to help students understand the application process, the different types of aid available, how to review financial aid offers, maintain eligibility, and navigate important policies related to financial aid. Please note that this information is subject to change. For the most current details, we encourage you to contact our office directly.

Texas A&M International University is an equal opportunity, affirmative action, coeducational institution. We do not discriminate based on race, creed, color, sex, disability, or national or ethnic origin.

Commitment to Ethical Practices in Financial Aid

Texas A&M International University and the Office of Financial Aid are committed to conducting business in an ethical manner and with the highest level of integrity. To this end, our office adheres to the Texas A&M University System 
Ethics Policy 07.01
 and 
Texas Higher Education Fair Lending Practices 07.02
 as indicated in our 
Code of Conduct for Financial Aid Professionals at Texas A&M International University.
 In addition, as a member of the National Association of Student Financial Aid Administrators, we comply with their 
Statement of Ethical Principles and Code of Conduct for Institutional Financial Aid Professionals
.
