DEGREE OVERVIEW 

The BA in communication offers specializations in advertising, broadcasting, communication studies (organizational and speech communication), communication technology (web design and development), journalism, and public relations.
Our courses and certificates prepare students to become competent, skillful communication and media professionals in organizations and media industries.

ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Our highly engaged BA program connects theory, practice, and experiential learning. Students can expect to be part of several innovative experiences as a major.
Our students and alumni stand out through their demonstration of:

oral and written communication

teamwork and leadership skills

digital media literacy

intercultural communication competency

community engagement and outreach

critical thinking and analytical skills

Career development opportunities are available through internships, client and service-learning projects, communication campaigns, and study abroad opportunities.
