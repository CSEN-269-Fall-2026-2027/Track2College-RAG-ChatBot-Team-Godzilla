                    Experience Southern California living in Redlands

University of Redlands is in the welcoming city of Redlands (population 70,000). In about one hour you can visit Los Angeles, relax on the beach, hike in the mountains, or explore the desert. Our partner school, Woodbury University, is in Los Angeles, giving students even more chances to enjoy city life, culture, and career opportunities. Redlands is a great place to live, study, and experience Southern California.
