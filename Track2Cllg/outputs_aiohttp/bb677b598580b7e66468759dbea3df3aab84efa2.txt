APU welcomes students who are committed to personal, intellectual, and spiritual growth.
                     Applicants must evidence appreciation for the standards and spirit of the university
                     and exhibit moral character in harmony with its purpose. The university invites applications
                     from students who will contribute to, as well as benefit from, the university experience.
                     In assessing the applicant’s potential for success, academic capabilities, as well
                     as involvement in church, school, and community activities, are reviewed.
