Overview

The mission of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSCE) is to develop the human and intellectual resources needed to meet the future technological challenges in the field of computing. This includes developing computer scientists and computer engineers for positions of leadership in industry, government and academia. It also means performing basic, applied and multidisciplinary research as well as coordinating and cooperating with other parts of The Texas A&M University System to enhance the effectiveness of educational resources. Likewise, our department provides the intellectual leadership and technology in the computing field required by the university, the state and the nation.

Advising Information

				The Department of Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate advising office offers both appointments (scheduled through Navigate) and drop-in sessions to meet with an advisor. You can choose to attend in person (EABA 100C) or on Zoom. You can also contact our office with questions through email at undergraduate-advisors@cse.tamu.edu.

For specific requirements and the official listings of available courses, please visit the 
Texas A&M Undergraduate Catalog
.

Drop-In Times

You can attend drop-in times either in person (EABA 102-107) or via Zoom. 
All students must sign in at 
tx.ag/cscedropinsignin
. 
Entering your information and submitting the form will reserve your place in line. Please note that the form will only open a few minutes before drop-ins begin. During especially busy times, we may have to shut the form down before the end of the drop-in session, but we will avoid that as much as possible.

We offer the following drop-in sessions days and times (unless the university is closed):

Mondays, 3:00-4:30 p.m.

Tuesdays, 9:00-10:30 a.m.

Wednesdays, 9:00-10:30 a.m.

Thursdays, 2:00-3:30 p.m.

All drop-in times are on a first-come, first-served basis and are designed for quick questions (5-10 minutes). Please keep in mind that there may be a longer wait if there are many people ahead of you, especially during peak advising times. For a dedicated time or for longer questions, schedule an individual appointment through 
Navigate
.

Change of Major

A request for a change of major to computer science (BS-CPSC), computer engineering–CS track (BS-CECN) and computing (BA-COMP) 
needs to be submitted via Howdy. You will find links to submit requests for a change of major under My Records at the bottom left of the page. Submission deadlines are early in the semester and decisions are made after semester grades are posted. See the section on 
change of major on the undergraduate studies page

for additional details. 

We recommend that students interested in a change of major review the general information that we provide for undergraduate transfers.

Undergraduates Enrolling for Graduate Courses

Undergraduates wishing to apply to take a 
graduate CSCE course
 should see our 
CSCE Grad Course Petitions Form
. Please complete the required information by the end of preregistration for full consideration. Department policy is that CSCE 331 should be completed before graduate courses are taken.

Some students are eligible to take up to three specified graduate courses on Fast Track, which provides credit for both graduate and undergraduate courses. If you are interested, see the information 
about our Fast Track program
.

Students wishing to take a 
graduate class in another department
 first should obtain permission from that department’s advising office. If their advising office agrees, the student should email 
undergraduate-advisors@cse.tamu.edu
 to get the university's required form. Students will then need to obtain signatures from the course’s instructor and from that department's college. The form should then be emailed back to 
undergraduate-advisors@cse.tamu.edu.
 We will obtain the needed approvals from the CSCE department and the College of Engineering.
