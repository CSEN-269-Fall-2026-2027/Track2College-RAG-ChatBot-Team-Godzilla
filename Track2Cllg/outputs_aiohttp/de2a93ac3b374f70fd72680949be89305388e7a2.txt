Music occupies a prominent place at Mount Saint Mary’s University. Established in 1925, our Department of Music builds on a nearly century-long tradition of excellence, offering emphases in music performance, music history, music education, music industry, pre-music therapy and the opportunity to study in Los Angeles with some of our city’s finest musicians.

Small class sizes, world class performance venues and access to a rich variety of masterclasses and internships makes MSMU an ideal place to follow your musical passion.

Explore the Music Program

Undergraduate programs
﻿

Mount Saint Mary’s University offers students the opportunity to earn a Bachelor of Arts or a minor in Music. Our degree program offers emphases in Music Education, Music Performance, Music Industry, Music History and Pre-Music Therapy.

Scholarships
﻿

MSMU gives Music majors the opportunity to earn full-tuition scholarships each year. Other generous music scholarships are available to all Mount students, regardless of their major.

High School Ensemble Competition

﻿

MSMU Music introduces the Solo & Chamber Ensemble High School Music Competition! Participants must submit an audition video and application for adjudication by Bridge to Everywhere, MSMU’s ensemble in residence.

Interested in joining an ensemble, or enrolling in a music class? Take advantage of the many opportunities available to non-music majors within the Music department! 

Music + Healing Initiative

Join us

The Music + Healing Initiative brings Mount Saint Mary's University's Music and Nursing communities together around a shared question: How does sound shape the way we heal? This interdisciplinary Initiative brings together experts in Environmental Music Therapy, the Roy Adaptation model, musical artists, music therapists, and nurses in conversation together to share and develop knowledge around Music + Healing.
