Biological Sciences B.S.

Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences

Gain the skills you need to become a respected biological scientist.

The Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences from University of Houston-Clear Lake
                              (UHCL) encompasses various areas of biology, including cell and molecular biology,
                              ecology, pre-health, and physiology. As a student in the program, you’ll gain a better
                              understanding of life processes and the environmental connections with living organisms
                              while engaging in problem-oriented research.

Request Info
How to Apply
Visit

Knowledge of biological sciences will equip you for advanced studies or a career in
                              biomedical research, zoology, and environmental fields, to name a few, depending on
                              your selected specialization area. Specialization options include:

Cell/Molecular/Biotechnology.

Ecology/Microbiology/Aquatic/Marine.

Physiology/Pre-Health.

Plant Science.

According to the recent data available from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, biological
                                 scientists earn an average salary of $99,000. Scientific research, government, and
                                 higher education are among the top-earning industries.
