                                                                        STEP- Go from RN to BSN in One Year!

Do you have an Associate’s Degree in nursing? Advance your career quickly through the 
STEP RN to BSN
 program! This 100% online program can be completed in one year and will prepare you to be a leader in healthcare, even as it readies you for advanced nursing education.

                                                                        Master of Science in Nursing

JSU's 
Master of Science in Nursing
 degree is offered in three concentrations and can be completed 100% online! 

The 
MSN in Population Health Nursing
 is designed to prepare advanced population health nurses to address the needs of your community and beyond in the 21st Century.

The 
MSN in Nursing Education
 prepares advanced community health nurses to teach in schools and colleges of nursing or clinical agencies and hospitals.

Today's healthcare organizations need to be ready for anything. The 
MSN in Emergency Management
 offers advanced knowledge and skills in emergency preparedness, planning and management from the nurse coordinator perspective.

 Doctor of Nursing Practice

JSU Nursing offers three 
Doctor of Nursing Practice
 tracks. These include the BSN-DNP Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP) track, Adult-Gerontology Acute Care Nurse Practitioner (AGACNP) track, and the Post-Master’s Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP) track. The Doctor of Nursing Practice is a terminal degree in nursing that prepares Registered Nurses (RNs) and Nurse Practitioners (NPs) to practice at the highest level of the profession.
