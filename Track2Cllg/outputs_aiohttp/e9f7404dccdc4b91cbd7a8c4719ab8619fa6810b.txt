Welcome to Financial Aid

On behalf of the Financial Aid Office, 
welcome to East Texas Baptist University!

More than 
90% of ETBU students receive financial aid
 through federal or state grants, ETBU scholarships, Work-Study and student employment, or student loans.

We’re here to help you understand your financial aid options and navigate the process with confidence. 
Follow the steps on the 
Financial Aid Resources
 page to complete your financial aid requirements
.
