Why Study Economics at Trinity?

Trinity’s Department of Economics helps students understand how individuals, firms, and governments make decisions in a complex global economy. You’ll study economic theory, data analysis, and real-world issues like markets, inequality, and public policy. You leave with strong analytical, quantitative, and problem-solving skills for careers or graduate study.

Economics Major (B.A., Minor)
