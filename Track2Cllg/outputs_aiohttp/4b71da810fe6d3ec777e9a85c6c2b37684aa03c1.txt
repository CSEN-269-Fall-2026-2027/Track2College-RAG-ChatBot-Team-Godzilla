First Year Experience

Creative Action

Creative Action is an interdisciplinary, project-based learning program that partners
                        students with diverse community organizations, both locally and globally. Through
                        this program, students collaborate with nonprofits, cultural institutions, and businesses
                        to develop and implement creative solutions to real-world challenges. The program
                        is designed to foster critical thinking, leadership, and teamwork, providing students
                        with invaluable experience that extends beyond the classroom.

Join Creative Action at Otis College

Minors

At Otis College of Art and Design, students have the opportunity to enrich their educational
                        experience by pursuing a variety of minors that complement their major studies. The
                        Interdisciplinary Studies program offers optional minors in Art & Design Education,
                        Art History, Concept Art, Contemporary Clay, Creative Writing, Digital Media, Graphic
                        Design, Illustration, Motion Design, Painting, Photography, Product Design, Sculpture/New
                        Genres, and Sustainability. Students in this program hone a broad range of skills
                        and knowledge, developing an art and design practice that is grounded in two or more
                        disciplines or situated uniquely in between. 

Otis College Minors

Summer of Art

Summer of Art is Otis College’s premier pre-college program. Designed for students
                           14.5 years and older, this four-week program offers and immersive art and design experience
                           and an opportunity to earn college credit.
 Courses include painting, digital media, fashion design, and more, taught by professionals.
                        The program concludes with a final exhibition where students showcase their work.
                        It’s a great opportunity to develop creativity and prepare for college.

Explore Summer of Art

Art and Design Education

The Art and Design Education minor at Otis College prepares students for diverse professional
                        roles as socially engaged artists, designers, and educators. This program equips students
                        to work in K-12 schools, museum education departments, community art centers, and
                        other educational and therapeutic settings. Through coursework and internships, students
                        gain practical experience and develop skills in teaching, community engagement, and
                        creative problem-solving.
