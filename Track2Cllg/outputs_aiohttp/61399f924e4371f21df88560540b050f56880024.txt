Start your flight path today. Choose the type of admission you are seeking, and learn about the admissions requirements.

Freshman

Apply as a freshman if you are a high school senior or if you graduated high school/GED but never attended a college or university.

Freshman

Transfer

Apply as a transfer student if you have previously enrolled at another college or university after graduation from high school.

Transfer

Graduate

Apply as a graduate student if you have already earned a bachelor's and are seeking a certificate, master's or doctoral degree.

Graduate 

International

Apply as an international student if you want to attend Lamar University with citizenship from a country outside the U.S.

International

Online

Select this option if you are seeking admission for a program offered in a fully-online format.
