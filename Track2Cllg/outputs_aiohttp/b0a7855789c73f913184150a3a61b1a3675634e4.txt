Department of Physics
The FIU Department of Physics brings together diverse minds to explore the universe's biggest questions. As part of a top-ranked Carnegie R1 university, we're driven by discovery and powered by opportunity. Our students work alongside faculty on cutting-edge projects in quantum materials, space science, health care, data science, physics education and more. FIU offers the facilities, mentorship and momentum to launch your career.
