Why Study Neuroscience?

Neuroscience is a good fit for you if you are interested in how the nervous system shapes movement, perception, decision-making, and well-being, and how disorders like stroke, dementia, Parkinson’s disease, and mental health conditions arise.

Learn By Doing

Join faculty-led research projects early in your academic career and contribute to ongoing discoveries in neuroscience.

Study the Brain From Multiple Perspectives

Examine neuroscience through biology, chemistry, psychology, and cognitive science to understand behavior and brain function.

Prepare for Your Next Step

Build the research experience, quantitative skills, scientific communication skills, and professional preparation needed for graduate school, medical school, and research careers in academia or industry.

What You Will Study

Learn how the nervous system functions from the cellular level to complex human behavior. Through coursework, laboratory experiences, and research opportunities, you'll investigate the biological foundations of cognition, health, and disease.

In Demand-Skills:
Experimental design and laboratory methods
Scientific data analysis
Critical evaluation of research
Scientific communication
Quantitative reasoning and problem-solving

Curriculum
Students complete core courses in biology, chemistry, and neuroscience, with electives across psychology and computer science, along with advanced laboratory and research experiences.

Where This Can Take You

Neuroscience graduates combine scientific knowledge, research experience, and analytical thinking to pursue careers in healthcare, research, biotechnology, and academia.

Medicine and Health Professions
Build a strong foundation for careers in medicine, dentistry, physician assistant studies, physical therapy, and other health professions.

Biomedical and Clinical Research
Investigate questions related to brain function, disease, behavior, and human health through laboratory and translational research.

Biotechnology and Pharmaceutical Sciences
Apply scientific and analytical expertise to drug development, biotechnology innovation, and healthcare technologies.

Graduate Study and Scientific Leadership
Continue developing expertise through advanced study in neuroscience, biomedical sciences, psychology, public health, and related disciplines.
