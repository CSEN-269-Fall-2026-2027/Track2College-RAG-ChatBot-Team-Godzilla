The Religion program at Southwestern Adventist University offers students a rich foundation in biblical knowledge while preparing them to serve in a variety of ministry and mission-oriented roles. Whether your calling is full-time ministry or incorporating faith-based principles into another profession, this program provides the flexibility and depth needed to make a meaningful impact.

Courses explore religious traditions, biblical teachings, and contemporary issues, all through the lens of Seventh-day Adventist beliefs. Many students pair a major or minor in Religion with other fields like education or nursing to expand their influence and service potential.

Curriculum and Courses

The Religion curriculum invites students to explore Adventist beliefs, biblical interpretation, and the history of Christianity in depth. Through coursework and guided research, students investigate the mission and teachings of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, examine other religious traditions, and learn to communicate faith-based insights clearly and effectively. The program emphasizes the development of both spiritual understanding and critical thinking, providing a well-rounded preparation for life and service.
