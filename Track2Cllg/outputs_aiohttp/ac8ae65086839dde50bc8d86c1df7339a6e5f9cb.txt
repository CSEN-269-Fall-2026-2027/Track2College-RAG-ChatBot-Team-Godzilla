Freshman

Applicants applying to college for the first time.

Transfer

                                 			Applicants previously enrolled at another college or university after graduating
                                 high school.

Graduate

Applicants with a bachelor's degree or equivalent and are pursuing further studies.

International

                                 			Applicants on a temporary visa, who do not have a U.S. permanent resident card
                                 (green card).

Non-Degree Seeking

                                 			Applicants who wish to take courses but do not plan to earn a degree at UNT.

Former/Returning

Applicants previously enrolled at UNT and have not attended the prior two long semesters.
