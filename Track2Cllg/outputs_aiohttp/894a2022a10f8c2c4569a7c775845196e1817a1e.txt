Obtain an Extremely Diverse Degree

A B.A. in Biological Sciences from the University of Houston-Clear Lake gives students
                                 the opportunity to pursue a career in a health-related profession or those interested
                                 in training in an allied health field. This program is most beneficial for students
                                 on the pre-medical track, though you can also combine it with another area of study,
                                 such as business, environmental studies, law, or science writing. You can also earn
                                 a specialization in General Biology.

Go Beyond the Classroom

Our College of Science and Engineering creates opportunities to put your knowledge
                                 to use in private industry and corporations, nonprofit organizations, and local, state
                                 and federal agencies. As a student, you can get involved by engaging in applied research
                                 in medicine, aerospace, biotechnology and chemistry.
