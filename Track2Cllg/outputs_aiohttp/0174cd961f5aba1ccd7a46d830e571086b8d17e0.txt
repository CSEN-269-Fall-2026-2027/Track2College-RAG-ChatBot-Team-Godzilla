Turn your passion for fitness and teaching into a rewarding career through JSU’s Health and Physical Education Teacher Education (HPETE) program.

Program Overview:

Prepares you to teach:

Physical Education (PreK–12)

Health Education (Grades 6–12)

Aligned with Alabama Department of Education and SHAPE America standards

Coursework includes:

Motor development, movement concepts, kinesiology, exercise physiology, biomechanics

Lesson planning, classroom management, and instructional design

Hands-On Experience:

“Early and often” fieldwork in:

Adapted physical education

Practicum and pre-internship teaching

Full-semester internship in PE and health settings

Certification & Outcomes:

Pass the Physical Education Praxis, Health Education Praxis, and edTPA

Earn dual Alabama certifications:

Physical Education (P–12)

Health Education (6–12)

Graduate ready to lead and inspire active, healthy lifestyles in schools.

                                                                        Degree Requirements

This degree requires 120 hours of coursework, including practicum and internship placements.
