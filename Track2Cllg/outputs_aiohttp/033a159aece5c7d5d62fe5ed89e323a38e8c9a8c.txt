EXPERIENCE UA

Life in

T-Town

Tuscaloosa combines a lively downtown, a scenic riverfront and the energy of a classic college town. With boutique shops, restaurants, and music and arts festivals nearby, there is much to explore.

While there’s plenty to do in T-Town, Tuscaloosa is a short drive from the beautiful beaches of the Gulf Coast, the adventurous trails of the Appalachian Mountains and the major metropolitan areas of Atlanta, Georgia, and Nashville, Tennessee.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You

Need to Know

Frequently asked questions from students and families.

How do I apply to The University of Alabama?
Students can apply to The University of Alabama through the UA Undergraduate App. Freshman students applying for main campus admission may also choose to apply via the Common App. Visit our 
Apply webpage
 for more information.

What are the admissions requirements for freshmen?
Freshman admission is reviewed holistically and may consider factors such as GPA, standardized test scores (if provided), and the strength of your academic coursework. View full requirements on the 
Freshman Requirements page
.

What scholarships are available at UA?
The University of Alabama offers automatic merit and competitive scholarships. Visit 
UA’s Scholarships website
 to learn more about the qualifications for each scholarship.

When are the application deadlines?
Visit our 
steps to enrollment page
 to see the application deadlines for your desired entry term.

Does UA have an Honors College?
Yes. The University of Alabama has an Honors College for high-achieving students. The Honors College offers enriched academic experiences through small seminars, faculty mentorship, research opportunities, and a variety of distinctive programs designed to enhance the undergraduate experience. Students apply after being admitted to UA. Learn more about eligibility and programs on the 
Honors College page
.

Can I transfer AP, IB, or dual enrollment credits?
Yes. AP, IB, and dual enrollment credits may transfer to The University of Alabama. Credits are reviewed after admission and applied based on official policies and course equivalencies. Some credits may fulfill degree requirements, while others may count as elective credit. You can review specific course equivalencies on the 
Transfer Credit Equivalency page
.

How do I visit the UA campus?
The University of Alabama offers student-guided tours Monday through Friday. Visit our 
Campus Tours webpage
 to see what tour dates are available or contact your 
admissions advisor
 to schedule a personalized tour based on your interests.

What undergraduate research opportunities exist?
UA offers undergraduate research opportunities across all majors through the Office for Undergraduate Research and individual colleges and departments. Students can work with faculty mentors on campus-based projects or participate in nationally funded programs. Visit the 
Office for Undergraduate Research website
 for more information.
