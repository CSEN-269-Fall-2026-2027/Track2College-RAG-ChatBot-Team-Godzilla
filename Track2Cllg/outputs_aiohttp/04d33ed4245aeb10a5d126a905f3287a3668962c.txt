Why LMU Dance?

The Education of the Whole Person 

At LMU Dance, this means we hone the critical mind and resilient spirit as much as we hone the articulate body

Featured Guest Artists

LMU Dance engages a diverse array of preeminent choreographers from across the nation in contemporary, jazz, tap, hip hop, and commercial dance. We engage guest artists to work with students over multiple visits or semester-long engagements to foster impactful learning and to help to build career bridges to the professional world.

Bill T. Jones

Choreographer and co-founder of the Bill T. Jones/Arnie Zane Company and Artistic Director of New York Live Arts.

Dana Wilson

Dancer, choreographer, teacher, career coach, and podcaster.

Entity Contemporary Dance

LA-based professional contemporary dance company, directed by Marissa Osato and Will Johnston.

Amanda Grind

Versatile dancer, choreographer, and educator working to bridge the gap between community & industry movements.

Pat Taylor

Founder of Los Angeles jazz company, JazzAntiqua Dance and Music Ensemble.
