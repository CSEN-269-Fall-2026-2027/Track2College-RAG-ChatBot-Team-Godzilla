Minor in Graphic Design + Digital Media
15 Units

The Graphic Design + Digital Media (GDDM) Minor at LCAD is a focused 15-unit program that provides students with essential foundational knowledge in contemporary visual communication and digital design. This carefully curated selection of courses serves as an introduction to the comprehensive concepts and skills including Motion Design, Typography, Editorial and Product design, making it an ideal complement to any primary field of study.

Career Applications
This minor enhances any major by providing valuable visual communication skills increasingly sought across industries. Whether pursuing careers in design, UI/UX, illustration, or across the arts sector, students gain competencies in design thinking, digital literacy, and creative problem-solving that are essential in today’s visually-driven marketplace. This minor serves as either a strong foundation for students considering advanced study in graphic design or as a valuable skill set that complements other academic and professional pursuits.
