At Southwestern Adventist University, our Mathematics program offers a rigorous foundation in mathematical theory and practical application. 

Whether you are drawn to the elegance of pure mathematics or the problem-solving nature of applied fields, this degree allows you to explore topics that match your interests. Through interactive coursework, collaborative projects, and hands-on problem solving, you will cultivate the critical thinking and analytical skills needed to succeed in a broad array of professions or graduate study.

Curriculum and Courses

Our Mathematics curriculum includes essential subjects such as calculus, linear algebra, discrete mathematics, statistics, and abstract algebra. Students can also pursue independent study, research projects, or internships to deepen their knowledge and apply their skills in real-world contexts. This variety of experiences ensures a well-rounded preparation for careers in both academic and applied fields.

Admission Requirements

Applying to the Mathematics program begins with admission to Southwestern Adventist University. Visit our Admissions Page to learn how to apply, explore financial aid opportunities, and connect with an admissions counselor.

Faculty & Mentorship

Our mathematics faculty brings diverse expertise in theoretical and applied disciplines. They provide personalized mentorship and academic advising, helping students explore advanced concepts, pursue research opportunities, and prepare for graduate school or professional careers.
