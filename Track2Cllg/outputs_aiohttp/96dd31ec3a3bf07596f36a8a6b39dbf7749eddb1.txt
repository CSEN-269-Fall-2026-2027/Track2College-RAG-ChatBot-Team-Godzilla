Program Overview
The Bachelor of Sport Science in Sport Management provides a strong foundation in the business, leadership, and operational aspects of the global sport industry. Students gain knowledge of sport organizations, marketing, finance, governance, and athlete development while applying theory through hands on projects, internships, and experiential learning opportunities. This program prepares graduates to manage and lead effectively in professional sports, collegiate athletics, recreation, fitness, and community sport settings.

What you will learn

Develop a comprehensive understanding of sports business knowledge and principles across various disciplines, applicable within the sports industry.

Acquire advanced skills in sport facility and event management through the effective oversight of sports equipment, venues, and events.

Cultivate the ability to generate revenue within the sports industry.

Foster critical and independent thinking to address real-world issues encountered by sports management professionals.

Enhance ethical and analytical skills by identifying ethical principles, analyzing significant issues, and evaluating emerging trends in the sports management discipline.

Why choose this program

This bachelor’s degree provides an industry relevant curriculum that aligns with current sport management practices and prepares students for success in a competitive and growing field. Through experiential learning opportunities such as internships and applied projects, students gain valuable real world experience while building professional skills that employers seek. The program offers career focused preparation for entry level roles in the sport industry and is taught by faculty with real world experience in sport leadership and administration. Graduates are equipped with the versatility to pursue careers across professional, collegiate, nonprofit, and private sport sectors.

Who this program is for

Students who are passionate about sport and leadership

Those seeking a career in the business side of sports

Individuals interested in sport marketing, events, or operations

CAREERS IN SPORTS MANAGEMENT

The career outlook for graduates with this degree is strong as the sports industry continues to expand across professional sports, fitness, youth sports, recreation, and sport tourism. Organizations are seeking qualified professionals who can manage events, support athletic operations, coordinate marketing efforts, and lead community based programs. Graduates benefit from a combination of classroom knowledge and hands on experience gained through internships and applied learning opportunities, helping them stand out in a competitive job market.

Entry level sport management positions typically offer salaries ranging from approximately $40,000 to $60,000 annually, with many early career professionals earning higher salaries as they gain experience and complete internships during their degree program. The field also offers strong opportunities for advancement into leadership and management roles over time.

Career opportunities include:

Sport Event Coordinator • Athletic Department Assistant • Marketing or Fan Engagement Coordinator
• Facility or Operations Assistant • Community and Youth Sport Director • Recreation and Fitness Program Manager
