5 Fun Facts about our Constitution:

Federal Direct Loan Changes 
Beginning with the 2026–2027 academic year, changes in federal legislation will affect federal Direct Student Loan disbursement amounts for students enrolled 
less than full-time.
A student’s loan disbursement amount will be based on their enrollment level at the time the loan is disbursed. Students enrolled less than full-time may receive a reduced annual federal Direct Student Loan amount.
For financial aid purposes, full-time enrollment is:
12 units for undergraduate and teaching credential students
8 units for graduate students
6 units for doctoral students
Students enrolled below half-time are not eligible to receive a federal Direct Student Loan disbursement.
Students who need assistance adding or selecting courses should contact their advisor. Students should also review their enrollment carefully before the start of the term, since changes in enrollment may affect their loan eligibility or disbursement amount.
Note:
 Parent PLUS Loans are not subject to enrollment-based proration. However, the student must be enrolled at least half-time for a Parent PLUS Loan to be disbursed.

Middle Class Scholarship Estimates
Eligible undergraduate students may see an estimated Middle Class Scholarship included in their financial aid offer.
This amount is an early estimate intended to assist with financial planning and may change. Final Middle Class Scholarship eligibility and award amounts are determined by the California Student Aid Commission.
The Middle Class Scholarship eligibility is finalized later than other financial aid programs. Final awards are disbursed in October.
