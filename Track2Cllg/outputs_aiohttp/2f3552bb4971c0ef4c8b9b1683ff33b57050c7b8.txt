Department of Psychology
The FIU Department of Psychology is an engaged community of experts and students advancing understanding in child and adolescent mental health, neuroscience, legal psychology, occupational health and well-being, and mental health counseling. As one of the top-funded psychology research programs in the nation, our department offers research opportunities for graduate and undergraduate students and provides real-world experience that leaves a lasting impact for our communities.
