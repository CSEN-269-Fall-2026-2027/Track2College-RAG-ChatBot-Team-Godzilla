The Department of Art and Art History offers the Bachelor of Arts (BA) degree in Visual
                        Arts with concentrations in Art History and Studio Art, and the Bachelor of Fine Arts
                        (BFA) degree with concentrations in all of the areas listed on our home page. Please
                        click on the specific area for detailed information.

Detailed information for both of these degree programs may also be found in the University
                           Bulletin
