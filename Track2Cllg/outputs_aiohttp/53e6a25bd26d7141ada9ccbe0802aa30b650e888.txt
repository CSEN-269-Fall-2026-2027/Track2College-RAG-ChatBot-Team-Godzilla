Computer Engineering B.S.

Computer Engineering B.S.

Gain hands-on experience in digital systems design, microprocessor and embedded systems,
                              communication and networks, and robotics with University of Houston-Clear Lake's (UHCL)
                              Computer Engineering (CENG) Bachelor of Science. This undergraduate degree emphasizes
                              the study of large and small computer systems for industrial, scientific, and business
                              applications. It also addresses the evaluation, design and implementation of computer
                              systems while the curriculum and faculty research highlight the integration of systems
                              design, software applications, and hardware design.

Enhance Your Engineering Career Potential

As a computer engineering graduate of UH-Clear Lake, you can land a career in an array
                              of industries, including programming, robotics, telecommunications, computer architecture,
                              and embedded systems. You also have the option to expand your knowledge and skillset
                              by pursuing the 
Computer Engineering Master of Science
, which provides even more learning and research opportunities. 

The top industries and employers for undergraduate computer engineering graduates
                              include:

Chip design (Nvidia, AMD, Intel*)

Robotics and automation (Tesla)

Embedded system design (TI*, General Motors)

High performance computer (Google, IBM*, Microsoft)

Wireless communication (Qualcomm*)

*Alumni of the Computer Engineering B.S. at UHCL have gone on to work for these renowned
                        companies as well as Boeing, Hewlett Packard, and Amazon, to name a few.

Why the Computer Engineering B.S. at UHCL?

Robotics Courses and Research

UH-Clear Lake's CENG is one of the very first programs that offers robotics courses
                                 and research in the Houston area.

Real-World Experience

Many courses in the CENG program have laboratory components, allowing students to
                                 gain hands-on experience in computer engineering. The degree also includes senior
                                 projects I and II. Students are required to work on real-world projects in two semesters
                                 to graduate. The industry advisory board evaluates each project.

Well-Designed Curriculum Taught by Research-Driven Faculty

The CENG curriculum and faculty research highlight the integration of systems design,
                                 software applications, and hardware design, providing students with the technical
                                 skills employer seek.

ABET-Accredited Program

The Computer Engineering BS program is accredited by the Engineering Accreditation
                        Commission of ABET, 
https://www.abet.org
, under the commission's General Criteria and Program Criteria for Electrical, Computer,
                        Communications, Telecommunication(s), and Similarly Named Engineering Programs.
