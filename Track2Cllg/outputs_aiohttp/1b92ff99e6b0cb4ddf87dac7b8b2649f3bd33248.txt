            Are You Eligible for the Mustangs Guarantee?

Are you a first-time student, Texas resident, Pell Grant eligible, and have a family combined annual adjusted gross income of $85,000 or less?

If so, the Mustangs Guarantee will cover the remaining balance of your tuition of 12+ credit hours each semester for up to four years.

            Is the TASFA the Right Option for You?

Are you feeling overwhelmed about financing your college education as a DACA student? Look no further than the Texas Application for State Financial Aid (TASFA). This option serves as an alternative for students who are ineligible for federal financial aid.

The TASFA provides access to state grants and scholarships that can help offset the costs of tuition, textbooks, and other expenses.

While the application process may seem daunting, there are numerous resources available to help applicants navigate the process.

            Priority Deadlines

Did you know that there are priority deadlines for financial aid applications?

Submitting your FAFSA or TASFA early can make a huge difference in the amount of aid you receive. Not only does it give you more time to gather any necessary documents but it also ensures that your application is reviewed and processed in a timely manner.

Plus, who does not love getting good news early? So do not delay; submit your financial aid application as soon as possible and get ready to celebrate all the support that is coming your way!

Financial Aid FAQs

How much is tuition at MSU Texas?

A key to your financial health and stability is being able to accurately budget your college costs without annual tuition increases. MSU Texas sets fixed rate designated tuition plans that allow you to lock in your tuition rate for 12 consecutive semesters (four years).

Get Details about Fixed Rate Designated Tuition Plans

When will my Financial Aid application be processed?

	            Financial Aid applications are processed in order in which they are received. For this reason, it is important that you submit your application by the priority deadline if you wish your aid to be disbursed on time.

When will I receive my refund?

Refunds are issued to those who have additional financial aid funds available in their student accounts after they have registered for classes. Refunds begin processing on the Thursday before classes begin for those who are eligible.

You can check on the status of your refund by viewing your Account Summary on the 
MSU Texas Portal
.

If you have allowed ample time to receive your refund check but still have not received your refund, please contact the MSU Texas Business Office at 
bus.office@msutexas.edu
 or call 
940.397.4101
.

Can you deposit my refund directly into my bank account?

We can! In fact, we recommend it.

You can set this up by logging in to the 
MSU Texas Portal
. Once there, click on the TouchNet link to complete the Automated Clearing House (ACH) process. You will need your routing and account numbers from your bank. Once you have this set up, your refund will directly deposit into your bank account.

If you do not set up this process, the MSU Texas Business Office will mail a refund check to your current mailing address on file.

I need to have my Financial Aid Award adjusted. What do I need to do?

	            To have your financial aid adjusted either due to a chance of financial circumstances or nonattendance, you will need to complete a Change Form. All forms can be found on the 
Financial Aid Forms page
. Select the year affected, and then select the Change Form from the list.

I have been selected for verification. Why, and what does that mean?

MSU Texas aid verification
 is a process that all universities undertake to confirm the accuracy of the information you provided on your Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA). It involves comparing the data you provided with supporting documents, such as tax returns, to ensure that everything is correct.

Can I appeal to waive Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) requirements?

	            Yes, there is an appeal process for mitigating factors — death of an 
immediate
 family member, a personal illness or injury, or another extenuating circumstance — that has prohibited you from meeting SAP. 
Specific details about the process are attached to the SAP Appeal Form
.
